# From screenplay to performed action

The six withdrawn 0.3.0 films expose two different review failures. A director could accept an outcome on paper without seeing how the cast produced it, and could accept selected pictures without establishing the pace of the film. Five directors approved their cuts. The basketball director withheld the fourth cut for a bench discontinuity and learned the extent of the court problems from the commissioning viewer. ## What the screenplay should decide

The Academy distinguishes scripts submitted for reading from shooting scripts. Disney describes a later layout stage where artists put characters, props and cameras together, play the edit and refine the timing. In the published screenplay of *The Wild Robot*, Paddler listens to a tree from a moose's antlers. When the tree falls across the river, the water rises and runs downhill. The page gives the reader the support, intervening object and consequence in a few lines. Layout can then determine their precise positions and timing. [Academy formatting guide](https://www.oscars.org/nicholl/screenwriting-resources) · [The Wild Robot, printed pp. 93–94](https://universalpicturesawards.com/the-wild-robot/screenplay/The_Wild_Robot.pdf) · [Disney Animation layout](https://disneyanimation.com/process/layout/)

*The Last Carry* already uses that economy. Walking draws back the shroud, the widow pulls it down and Denis shortens his step to give her time. Preserve such passages. For simultaneous actions, let one paragraph change the reader's attention while another character's action continues. The current notation says each block starts when its predecessor ends. That invites the writer to serialize actions the screenplay contract expressly allows to overlap. Retain stable beat anchors and the shot decisions the next stage needs. Record which clock a fixed cue uses and which durations are estimates. The game clock can stop while people move, and a replay can revisit the same action later in the film. ## What research and contracts should cover

The research contract already covers tempo, sequences and the conduct of everyone present. A short can commission a scene that depends on all three, yet the configuration instructions exempt short-route studies from that contract. When a short needs a study, enable the existing research claim and have the consuming beat or module identify the observation it uses. Keep the short's brief-to-screenplay route. For a specialist ensemble, observe a complete passage at the relevant level. Identify the initiating cue, who can see it, how other people and objects respond during the same interval, and which waits have a cause. A stationary object needs its relevant surface, clearance or obstruction described when that changes an action. A crowd can act as a group until an individual changes the outcome. [Research statements](../../.agents/skills/contracts/story/shared/statements/principles/research.md#depicted-practice) · [Evidence configuration](../../.agents/skills/evidence/configuration.md)

The basketball production researched off-ball cuts, foot preparation and period rules. Its film gave the ten players separate floor tracks and selected a gait from the average speed between marks. During one dribble, a point on the planted shoe moved 22.1 cm in one third of a second near floor level. The missing handoff was from the observed play to the steps, changes of direction and reactions the cast actually performed. ## What modular review should establish

The installed scaffold requires a playable catalog and the same capabilities in the catalog and the film. Across the three current production folders containing the six cuts, all three lack the specified catalog scene directory, registered catalog film and catalog acceptance record. All three select entity and composition Evidence while omitting behavior Evidence. The source checks that did run could therefore coexist with missing module acceptance. [Catalog cases](../../.agents/skills/motion/catalog-cases.md) · [Composition statements](../../.agents/skills/contracts/development/statements/composition.md#composition-only) · [Basketball configuration](../../movies/shorts/slam-dunk/evidence.config.ts) · [Sword configuration](../../movies/shorts/sword/evidence.config.ts) · [Kickboxing configuration](../../movies/shorts/kickboxing/evidence.config.ts)

| Where to decide | Evidence the director needs |
| --- | --- |
| Reusable action | A playable case using the film's capability, body, relevant load, support or contact partner, with preparation, result and recovery visible. Record the accepted range. |
| Scene and joins | The same action on the actual cast, with its incoming speed, support, prop ownership and outgoing state. Inspect the other participant and the object in the same interval. Test a new condition where the earlier case stops applying. |
| Edited film | Independent viewing of the exact candidate at its intended speed for pace, attention and group response. Use original frames and measurements to locate faults. Record who actually watched, the cut they saw and the issues still open. |

Make missing catalog coverage visible when a film consumes a reusable capability, including one implemented inside an entity. Distinguish an intentional plant, a slipping shoe and a camera pan by checking the planted foot in scene coordinates and the unmarked delivery view. An accepted installation of a primitive can be reused within its tested range. Fitting and joining it on another body can still change the result. ## Lessons from a second production

A second 0.3.0 production withdrew ten accepted shorts. It had playable cases. A shutter press had been inspected, but the same actor's later movement carrying a sheet of paper across a tray rim had a different path and failed. Its session proposed seven skill edits in a working tree and exercised four review scenarios on paper. The team reported that acceptance of the replacement films remained open.

Adopt its practice of naming the conditions under which an action passed, checking a continuous reference passage, assigning a defect to its first failing stage, and keeping an unresolved film a candidate. Adapt its capture recipe to each event's duration and the delivery frame rate. Five adjacent frames can locate a sudden flip, while pace and the cause of a pause require the entire action at normal speed. A qualified viewer can report playback when the user delegates that judgment. If nobody has observed it, the verdict stays open.

To test a future revision, give an independent director the returned dribble, the bier at the gate, an intentional hold and a sound catalog case used with a different prop. Check whether the director finds the known faults, accepts the purposeful hold, identifies the additional untested condition and assigns each repair to its owner. Then test an unseen scene and the complete cut. This comparison and the proposed changes have yet to be tested on replacement films.
