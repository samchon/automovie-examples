## What the screening instructions tested

- The picture-only reviewer received 45 sheets with 900 chronological samples from the encoded MP4 at five frames per second, plus the final full still. The prompt said, “The display is still images without sound,” and asked for “a short observation for each four-second group.” The record establishes changes of ends, score and possession outcomes, loss of interest in the opening and timeout, and poor ball visibility in wide shots. [Exact prompt](../../.cache/sol-0.3.0/two-hands/round-4/review/audience.input.md)
- The captioned reviewer received those samples plus actual decoded captions as text. The prompt asked the reviewer to report “what the image and subtitle sequence cannot establish, including hearing and motion cadence between samples.” The reviewer understood Blue 8's contribution and explicitly left step, dribble, and jump cadence unresolved. Offline ASR recovered the new lines from the MP4. Vocal delivery and effects as heard during playback were outside these reviews. ## Causes in the production

The encoder decoded all 4,320 distinct 1280 × 720 H.264 pictures at 24 fps. The score and game clock advanced as authored. The slow-feeling performance came from route timing and motion, with wide camera coverage hiding some of what movement existed.

1. **Players barely translate in several live possessions.** A source sample of the ten player roots at the delivery clock, excluding replay cuts, found a median root speed of 0.045 m/s in the first 18 seconds. The median count under 0.35 m/s was ten in that window, nine in seconds 37–44, and eight in both seconds 44–52 and 173–180. The 25–37 second transition had a median root speed of 1.65 m/s and no player exceeded 3.454 m/s in that interval. This diagnostic threshold measures court travel. Footwork and the tactical value of a planted screen require separate inspection. [Full measured interval table](../../.cache/sol-0.3.0/two-hands/round-4/root-motion-assessment.json)
2. **The rig's feet and body move on different clocks.** Every route segment eases toward zero at both ends, including at intermediate marks, while action selection uses segment-average speed. Any non-seated autonomous route over 0.55 m/s can use the same run action, including the captain's bench walk. The run and dribble cycles use fixed cadence and local ankle swings. Root translation and turn carry those foot targets through the world. There is no explicit world-space stance lock, and ball cues can replace the locomotion pose during continued travel. The source establishes this path to sliding and unsupported turns. No full-film shoe-slip measurement was recorded. [Route sampling](../../src/composition/scenes/two-hands/010-game/game.ts#L86) · [Action and phase](../../src/composition/scenes/two-hands/010-game/game.ts#L262) · [Leg targets](../../src/entities/cast/basketball/players.ts#L201)
3. **Timed matchups omit the physical causes of some plays.** Offense and defense largely follow independently scheduled marks. Opponent position can steer facing. Braking, pushing around a set screen, and closing out require staging of their own. The last three and rebound stay readable as outcomes even where the actions that create them feel unearned or slow. Repeated reports that viewers watched the clock and referee were interpreted as visibility issues. No gate checked live competition in uninterrupted playback before the commissioning user watched. [Roster timing and matchup facing](../../src/composition/scenes/two-hands/010-game/game.ts#L254) · [Director's full assessment](history/two-hands-3d-reopened-director.md)
4. **Installed motion composition supplied specific pieces and left key joins to production.** The installed probe admitted stationary implied-ball dribble but refused `run` with `ballSport` and airborne `ballSport` because those actions claim the whole body. It also refused generic `throwObject` over `jump` because jump retains the trunk and throwing-arm channels. The adopted performance used installed canonical bodies and bench gestures with its own player root routes, local procedural limbs and authored ball path. Those refusals explain those joins. They do not explain sparse offensive and defensive staging.
