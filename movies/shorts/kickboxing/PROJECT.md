# Kickboxing

A 180-second short of one kickboxing round between two equal championship-level fighters, in which one man's patient body kicks teach the other a habit and a Brazilian kick cashes it in. The brief's [work record](docs/body/brief/world/work.md) owns the audience and the intended experience; the owner's binding rulings are in [the commission](docs/instructions/commission.md).
