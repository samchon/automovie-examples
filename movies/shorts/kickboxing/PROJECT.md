# Project

## Overview

The story of one kickboxing round, written as a short's screenplay: two equal championship fighters, a habit one of them learns during the round, and the finish the other builds from it. The [brief](docs/body/brief/world/work.md) owns the audience and the intended experience; the [commission](docs/instructions/commission.md) holds the owner's rulings.

## Commissioned scope

The commissioned output is a [screenplay](../../../.agents/skills/production/SKILL.md#commissioned-outputs): one film's text, complete when it passes the [writing workflow](../../../.agents/skills/story/workflow.md#completion). No source, Viewer, test, media, generated film or video prompt is owed, and none is made.

The work runs research, then the brief, then screenplay construction, then the screenplay's naturalness pass.

## Outside the current scope

The earlier kickboxing production's commission also ruled on the film and its making. The user adopted only its story rulings for this production, so these stay recorded here and bind nothing until a film is commissioned:

- The film itself: "Complete issue 3040 under the latest instructions", delivered there as a 180-second direct 3D film with runnable source, a working Viewer, playback review and a decoded final video.
- A motion ruling: "During every round kick the kicking-side hand reaches forward at the protective head line while the opposite hand guards, through chamber, delivery, recoil and replant."
- An ownership ruling for code: "Production owns officiating policy; Human owns reusable anatomy and motion."
- The video-generation prompts that production prepared for a diffusion model.

A later film commission decides whether each returns.

## Representation

People are polygon men and every other subject a coarse form. A polygon man has no face, hair, skin detail, sweat, blood or marks; he carries the colours of his clothing and gloves, and his attention reads from the turn of his head and trunk. The brief's [representation record](docs/body/brief/world/work.md#representation) says what the screenplay may and may not ask of it.

## Delivery profile

No video is delivered. The screenplay is written for one film of 180 seconds in a landscape 16:9 frame, the frame the camera ruling's single screen axis for feet, targets and heads assumes. Dimensions, frame rate, container, codec and audio tracks are left to a film commission.

## Adopted approach

- Route: the short route, fixed by the production's place in `movies/shorts`: research, brief, screenplay construction, screenplay naturalness.
- Research: rules, technique, bout patterns, officiating and fight coverage, indexed in [docs/research/index.md](docs/research/index.md).
- Evidence: the research and brief construction stages are enabled; the screenplay stages are enabled when its complete draft exists.

## Tools and operating constraints

- The production installs only the evidence package. The motion vocabulary is not installed, so no study can defer a movement to it; the studies cover every technique the screenplay will name.
- Research originals whose rights are unconfirmed stay in ignored `references/private/`; only openly licensed originals are kept in `references/`.

## Authority and open choices

- The user fixed the rulings in the commission, including its reading of the tell, the language and the direction.
- This production decided within that delegation: the fighters' names, corners and styles, the referee's attire and words, the ring's dressing, the hall's light and sound, and the camera's ringside grammar, each recorded in the brief with its reason.
- Open for the user: the commission places the finish "behind B's left ear at the junction of jaw and neck", and the rules forbid targeting the back of the head. The brief reads the landing point as the side of the head and neck below and just behind the ear lobe, struck by a kick aimed at the side of the head, and records the [reading](docs/body/brief/world/contest.md#finish-target) for the user to confirm or correct.
