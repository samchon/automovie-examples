# Project

A screenplay for a 180-second kickboxing short: one championship round between two equal fighters, in which a habit one man learns during the round decides it. The [work record](docs/body/brief/world/work.md) owns the audience and the intended experience, and the [commission](docs/instructions/commission.md) holds the owner's binding rulings.

## Commissioned scope {#commissioned-scope}

The commissioned output is the screenplay only, `docs/body/screenplay/kickboxing.md`, complete when its text passes the [writing workflow](../../../.agents/skills/story/workflow.md#completion). No source, test, entity, motion, render or other media is owed.

The scaffold's source, tests and configuration stay as `createAutoMovieScaffold` created them. Their `pnpm run lint` and `pnpm test` currently fail on the workspace's link to the AutoMovie checkout's package source; that failure is outside this production's scope and is not repaired here.

### Outside the current scope {#outside-the-current-scope}

The earlier kickboxing production's commission, in the read-only reference `D:\github\samchon\automovie@scaffold\.references\kickboxing-legacy`, also ruled on work this production does not make now. The user adopted only its story rulings; these stay outside the current scope until the user commissions them:

- the complete 180-second direct 3D film of issue 3040, with runnable source, the inspector page, playback review and a decoded final video;
- the motion ruling that during every round kick the kicking-side hand reaches forward at the protective head line while the opposite hand guards, through chamber, delivery, recoil and replant;
- the boundary that the production owns officiating policy while the Human package owns reusable anatomy and motion;
- the video-generation prompts that would restyle the render into live action.

The screenplay is written so that a later film can realize it, but nothing here commissions or approves that film.

## Representation {#representation}

The screenplay is written for the scaffold's representation: every person is a polygon man drawn from the Human package's body, and every other subject is the simplest form that identifies it. No detail option is selected, because no delivery is commissioned. The brief's [work record](docs/body/brief/world/work.md) states what this representation cannot show and how the screenplay writes within it.

## Delivery profile {#delivery-profile}

No video is delivered now. The story conditions the screenplay is written to are:

| Film | Running time | Frame |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `kickboxing` | 180.000 seconds, one continuous film | Landscape 16:9, the scaffold's 1280 × 720 default |

Frame rate, container, codec, colour and caption tracks are undecided and are set when a film is commissioned. The commission's anchors are given to a tenth of a second, which any of the usual frame rates can land.

## Adopted approach {#adopted-approach}

- Story route: the short route, which the workspace folder `movies/shorts/kickboxing` fixes: research, the [brief](docs/body/brief/world/work.md), screenplay construction, then the screenplay's expression pass.
- Each narrative pass runs through separate roles in separate contexts: the author writes and repairs the body, an evidence answerer writes the construction answers, and a fresh independent reviewer reviews each round.
- Sound: the screenplay names each shot's sound for a later film; no soundtrack is commissioned.
- No control channels, engine feedback, capture or render path is adopted, because no film is commissioned.

## Operating constraints {#operating-constraints}

- Shell commands run in PowerShell only.
- Agents do not stage, commit, push or otherwise change Git state; the coordinator does.
- Each run appends its start, submission and end to the ignored run record `.cache/run-record.md`.
- The earlier production supplies decisions to assess, never text.

## Authority and open choices {#authority}

- User-fixed: every ruling in the [commission](docs/instructions/commission.md), including the user's confirmation of where the finish lands, recorded under its [finish](docs/instructions/commission.md#finish) section.
- Decided within delegation: the language and the direction, recorded in the commission's [language](docs/instructions/commission.md#language) and [direction](docs/instructions/commission.md#direction) sections, and the brief's decisions.
- Open: the delivery profile's technical tracks, until a film is commissioned.
