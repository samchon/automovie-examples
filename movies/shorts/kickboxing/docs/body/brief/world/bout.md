# The bout

The contest the film shows and the rules it keeps: what the bout is, how its round starts, what each man may and may not do, what the blows before the finish may cost, and how the knockdown, the count and the result run against the commission's anchors. The rules come from the [rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md); this record settles how this bout applies them.

## The contest {#contest}

### A fictional title bout under GLORY rules {#title-bout}

The bout is a middleweight title fight of a fictional promotion, fought under the GLORY rules and claiming no accredited event ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#ruleset)). Both men are 80 kg, inside GLORY's middleweight class ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#division)). [Visser](../cast/visser.md#identity) defends the title; [Benlloch](../cast/benlloch.md#identity) challenges for it.

The promotion, the venue and the title are never named on screen.

### Round one of five {#round-one}

A title bout is scheduled for five rounds of three minutes ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#title-format)). The film is round one, the only round fought ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#one-round)). It is round one because the glove touch opens it, and because the commission's reading of the tell has Visser learn the habit on screen ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#tell)). Neither man has been hit before the film starts.

No judge's score is ever shown or heard, because the knockout ends the bout before round one is scored ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#scoring)).

## The start {#start}

### Bell, "Fight", touch, exchange {#start-sequence}

The round opens as GLORY allows and as the commission's [glove touch](../../../instructions/commission.md#glove-touch) needs, in this order ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#round-start)):

1. The two men wait in their own corners, and the referee stands in the centre.
2. The timekeeper strikes the bell once at 0.0 seconds ([bell](../props/bell.md#strikes)).
3. The referee says "Fight" and steps back off the line between the men.
4. Both men walk out and step into a real touch of gloves.
5. The gloves part, both men are back in guard, and the first exchange starts at once, with no return to the corners and no second command.

The touch is open and honest: neither man strikes during it or fakes it ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#glove-touch)).

The instructions before the bell happened before the film and are not shown.

Left to the screenplay: who opens the first exchange, and with what.

## What each man may do {#legal-actions}

### Strikes and targets {#strikes}

Each man may punch with the padded glove, kick with the foot or lower leg, and knee. Legal targets are the head and face from the front and sides, the body from the front and sides, and the legs, which may be struck only with kicks that arc ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#targets)). Every other technique or target is a foul, and those this bout comes nearest to are listed under [never done](#never-done).

The fight's whole vocabulary is the commission's: combinations, feints, checks with counters, slips, parries, rolls and blocks, with varied footwork ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#exchange-vocabulary)). Both men use all of it, each in his own style ([Benlloch](../cast/benlloch.md#repertoire), [Visser](../cast/visser.md#repertoire)).

### Never done {#never-done}

Neither man throws an elbow, a throw or a sweep. Neither strikes the back of the head, the back or the groin, strikes a man who is down, holds the ropes, pushes, or takes a hold for any purpose but one knee. Neither speaks, taunts or turns to the crowd ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#ruleset), [rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#technique-additions)).

No deliberate foul is committed in the round, so no official warning or penalty occurs. The one foul the round allows is an accidental tie-up at close range. It is a minor foul, which the referee breaks without a caution, at his discretion ([clinch](#clinch)).

### The clinch {#clinch}

Two kinds of close contact happen in the round, and the referee answers each differently ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#ruleset), [rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#clinch)):

- **The lawful clinch.** A man takes a hold only to throw one immediate knee, and releases it himself at once. In this round only Visser does ([Visser](../cast/visser.md#repertoire)). The referee closes in but gives no command.
- **The tie-up.** In close exchanges the men's arms tangle, or one man smothers a punch, and neither means it as a hold. Under GLORY even an accidental hold is a minor foul, which may draw a caution without stopping the action. This referee, at his discretion, gives no caution beyond the command ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#foul-consequences)). He parts them with "Break!", the commission's clinch break.

How the referee breaks them is his record's ([referee](../cast/referee.md#clinch-break)).

Left to the screenplay: when each happens. The round has at least one tie-up, so that the commission's clinch break is seen ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#referee)). No more than two are charged to either man, because GLORY deducts a point automatically after three fouls of any kind ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#foul-consequences)).

### The finish is legal {#finish-target}

The finishing kick is a high round kick: Benlloch's right Brazilian kick ([Benlloch](../cast/benlloch.md#brazilian-kick)). His instep and lower shin come down over Visser's lowered left arm onto the side of the head and neck below and just behind the left ear lobe ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#finish)). That is a legal target under GLORY ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#finish-target)).

The contact must read as landing on the side, forward of the line that runs down from behind the ear. A contact that read as landing on the back of the neck would be a foul, and the film never shows one.

## Blows before the finish {#before-the-finish}

### Costs without a knockdown {#costs}

Every blow that lands before 150 seconds costs breath, guard position or balance, and none puts a man down ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#contacts-before-finish)). No standing count is given and the referee never stops the action to look at a man. The costs build through the round as each man's state records ([Benlloch](../cast/benlloch.md#state), [Visser](../cast/visser.md#state)), and show as the visible signs the [representation](work.md#faceless-polygon-men) allows.

A blow that would credibly knock a man down is not written before the finish. Head contacts before 150 seconds are glancing, or are taken on the guard.

## The knockdown and the result {#knockdown-and-result}

### The fall and the call {#fall}

At 150.000 seconds the kick lands. Visser falls on his right side, and the fall is shown whole, from the loss of support to his body at rest on the canvas, without a cut ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#fall)). He is down from the first moment any part of him other than his feet touches the canvas. The referee calls "Down" and points to the canvas ([referee](../cast/referee.md#knockdown)).

### The wait before the count {#wait}

The referee sends Benlloch to the farther neutral corner, and Benlloch walks there and turns to face the centre. The count does not start until Benlloch stands there facing the centre, as GLORY requires. The time between the fall and 157 seconds is that walk ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#anchors)). No knockdown timekeeper counts from ringside, and nobody counts before the referee does.

### Conscious, and unable to rise {#unable-to-rise}

Visser is conscious throughout the count and tries to rise, and fails. He turns his head, presses a glove on the canvas or draws a knee up that does not bear his weight. He never reads as limp and still. A man who read as unconscious would invite the referee to stop the count early for the doctor, and the commission keeps the whole count ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#anchors), tension 2). He does not get his feet under him, so he is still down at "Eight" and the count runs on ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#no-injury-mechanism)).

Left to the screenplay: which of these signs he shows, and when.

### The count {#count}

The referee counts aloud, one word a second: the count starts at 157, "One" falls at 158 and so on to "Ten" at 167 ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#count)). Benlloch stays in the neutral corner and Visser stays down for the whole count, which plays in real time. If Benlloch left the corner the count would stop, so he does not leave it ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#down-procedure)).

### The result {#result}

After an uninterrupted ten, the referee signals the knockout at 167.6 seconds. That signal ends the count and releases Benlloch from the corner, and Benlloch celebrates from 168.1. The timekeeper rings the match-ending bell at 170.1 ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#count), [rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#match-bell)). The result is a knockout, not a technical knockout ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#ten-count)).

After the knockout:

- The referee calls the ring doctor ([ringside](../crowds/ringside.md#doctor)).
- The corner teams stay outside the ropes until the film ends at 180 ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#venue), [rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#ring-entry)).
- The result's announcement and the raising of the winner's hand fall after the film ([work](work.md#left-out), [rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#announcement)).
- Benlloch celebrates inside the ring. GLORY forbids approaching or inciting the crowd, so he does not climb the ropes toward it ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#technique-additions)).
