# The arena

The hall around the ring on a championship night: lit at the ring, dark beyond it ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#venue)).

## Layout {#layout-facet}

### Ring, ringside, tiers {#layout}

From the inside out ([coverage study](../../../research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md#crowd-arrangement)):

- the [ring](ring.md) stands on the arena floor;
- round it are the [officials' table, the judges, the doctor and the corners](../crowds/ringside.md);
- then the floor seats of the [crowd](../crowds/arena-crowd.md#arrangement);
- then raked tiers rising into the dark.

A lighting truss hangs above the ring. Nothing above it is ever seen.

The arena is never named, and no sign, screen text or banner is legible ([work](../world/work.md#what-it-cannot-show)).

## Light {#light}

### Bright ring, dark hall {#lighting}

The light is the championship night's real practice ([coverage study](../../../research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md#lighting)):

- **The ring:** lit brightly and evenly in near-neutral white from the truss, with no harsh shadows anywhere on the canvas.
- **Ringside:** the rows lit dimmer than the ring, in a slightly warm tint.
- **The tiers:** falling away into darkness.

No light moves, sweeps or flashes while the round is fought. The light does not change through the film, through the knockout, the count and the celebration included.
