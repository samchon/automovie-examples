# The ring

The one place the film happens: a GLORY ring, raised on the arena floor, inside which the whole round is fought and its aftermath plays ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#whole-ring)).

## Shape {#shape}

### A raised, roped square {#square}

The ring is GLORY's larger size, a square raised above the floor and closed by four ropes ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#ring)). The larger ring gives the round room for kicking distance, circling and cutting off. The measurements live in the study.

Outside the ropes, a narrow apron runs round all four sides.

### Canvas and ropes {#surface}

The canvas is plain mid-grey, with no logo or text ([work](../world/work.md#what-it-cannot-show)). Both fighters' colours, white and black, read against it, and so does a man lying on it.

The ropes are wrapped in dark grey. They give and creak when a man leans or is driven onto them.

## Corners and zones {#zones}

### The corners {#corners}

The four corners are the round's landmarks. Each is a padded post in its own colour ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#corner-colours)):

- **The white corner:** Benlloch's, with white pads ([Benlloch](../cast/benlloch.md#wardrobe)).
- **The black corner:** Visser's, diagonally across, with black pads ([Visser](../cast/visser.md#wardrobe)).
- **The two neutral corners:** on the other diagonal, with red pads. Red makes them the corners that read as neither man's.

Going round the ring, the corners run: white, the steps' neutral corner, black, the far neutral corner.

- **The officials' table:** at the centre of the side between the white corner and the steps' neutral corner ([ringside](../crowds/ringside.md#officials-table)).
- **The steps:** for the referee and the doctor, at the steps' neutral corner ([ringside](../crowds/ringside.md#doctor)).

After the knockdown, Benlloch waits in whichever neutral corner is farther from where Visser falls ([bout](../world/bout.md#wait)). Visser falls on the steps' side of the diagonal between the white and black corners, so that Benlloch's corner is the far neutral corner and the doctor's way in never passes the winner. Left to the screenplay: where on that half he falls.

### How the fight uses it {#use}

The whole ring is used ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#whole-ring), [styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#pressure-ring-and-tempo)):

- **The centre:** where the round opens and where the men fight at kicking range.
- **The ropes and corners:** where Visser's cut-offs drive Benlloch, and where Benlloch must pivot or angle out.
- **Open diagonals:** where Benlloch circles to reset the range.

Left to the screenplay: where each phase stands in the ring and how it moves on. Each phase hands the next a changed position ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#progression)).

## State {#state}

### Unchanged through the round {#unchanged}

Nothing in the ring changes during the film: no spill, no blood, nothing thrown in. At the end the canvas holds Visser, and the referee and doctor kneel at him. Benlloch celebrates on his own side of the ring.
