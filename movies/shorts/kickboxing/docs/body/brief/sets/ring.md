# The ring

The film's only place: a GLORY ring in a full arena at night, the setting of the single scene `INT. KICKBOXING RING - NIGHT` ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#phases)).

## Shape and landmarks {#shape}

### A raised square ring {#ring}

The ring is the larger of GLORY's two sizes: a square canvas floor on a raised platform, closed by four padded ropes on each side between four corner posts, with an apron outside the ropes ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#ring-and-equipment)). The larger size gives the men room to circle, cut off and reset, which the round's use of the whole ring needs ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#whole-ring)). The canvas is a pale grey, plain and unprinted, so the white and the black fighter both stand out against it and a body lying on it reads clearly. The ropes are dark grey.

### The corners {#corners}

Seen from above, the corners run in this order round the ring: the white corner, the table neutral corner, the black corner and the far neutral corner. The white and black corners face each other across one diagonal and carry padded posts in their colours; [Vos](../cast/vos.md) is the white corner's fighter and [Roux](../cast/roux.md) the black's ([gloves](../props/gloves.md#corner-colours)). At the opening bell each man stands a few steps out from his own corner and the referee stands at the centre between them ([referee](../cast/referee.md#positioning)). Each corner team sits outside the ropes at its own corner ([ringside](../crowds/ringside.md#corner-teams)).

### The neutral corners {#neutral-corners}

The two neutral corners, across the other diagonal, have grey posts. The table neutral corner is where the officials' table side meets the side toward the black corner; the ring doctor sits just outside it, by the steps up to the apron ([ring doctor](../cast/ring-doctor.md#place)). The far neutral corner is diagonally opposite it. After the knockdown Vos goes to whichever neutral corner is farther from where Roux lies ([bout rules](../world/bout.md#count)); the screenplay decides where Roux falls, and so which corner that is.

### The table side {#table-side}

The officials' table stands outside the ring along the side between the white corner and the table neutral corner, with the timekeeper and his bell, the judge supervisor and the announcer seated at it ([bell](../props/bell.md#bell); [ringside](../crowds/ringside.md)). The other three sides face the crowd's seats directly.

## Zones and use {#use}

### The zones of the canvas {#zones}

The screenplay addresses places on the canvas by these names: the centre; the ropes of a side, named by the two corners it joins or as the table side; and a corner, named by its post. The middle third of the ring is open fighting space; within a step or two of the ropes a man can still fight, but the ropes end his retreat; in a corner they end it on two sides ([exchanges study](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#ring)).

### How the round uses the space {#space-use}

Across the round the fight passes through the centre, reaches the ropes of more than one side and at least one corner, and returns to the centre by pivots and circling, as the commission's whole-ring ruling and the exchanges study's ring strategy require ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#whole-ring); [exchanges study](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#ring)). Inside the ropes there are only the two fighters and the referee until the ring doctor enters after the knockout ([bout rules](../world/bout.md#result)). The camera stays outside the ropes ([camera](../cinematography/camera.md#ringside)).

## The hall {#hall}

### An arena at night {#arena}

The ring stands in the middle of an indoor arena during an evening event. Ringside seats begin a few steps from the apron and rise in tiers into darkness on every side, and a lighting rig above the ring throws all the light down onto the canvas ([camera](../cinematography/camera.md#light)). The hall beyond the first rows is dark; what lies in it is the [crowd](../crowds/arena-crowd.md).
