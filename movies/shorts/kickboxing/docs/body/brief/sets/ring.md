# The ring

The one place the film happens: a GLORY ring, raised on the arena floor, inside which the whole round is fought and its aftermath plays ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#whole-ring)).

## Shape {#shape}

### A raised, roped square {#square}

The ring is GLORY's larger size, a square raised above the floor and closed by four ropes ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#ring)). The larger ring gives the round room for kicking distance, circling and cutting off. The measurements live in the study.

Outside the ropes, a narrow apron runs round all four sides.

### Canvas and ropes {#surface}

The canvas is plain mid-grey, with no logo or text ([work](../world/work.md#what-it-cannot-show)). Both fighters' colours, white and black, read against it, and so does a man lying on it.

The ropes are wrapped in dark grey. They give and creak when a man leans or is driven onto them.

## Corners and zones {#zones}

### The corners {#corners}

The four corners are the round's landmarks. Each is a padded post in its own colour ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#corner-colours)):

- **The white corner:** Benlloch's, with white pads ([Benlloch](../cast/benlloch.md#wardrobe)).
- **The black corner:** Visser's, diagonally across, with black pads ([Visser](../cast/visser.md#wardrobe)).
- **The two neutral corners:** on the other diagonal, with red pads. Red makes them the corners that read as neither man's.

Going round the ring, the corners run: white, the steps' neutral corner, black, the far neutral corner.

- **The officials' table:** at the centre of the side between the white corner and the steps' neutral corner ([ringside](../crowds/ringside.md#officials-table)).
- **The steps:** for the referee and the doctor, at the steps' neutral corner ([ringside](../crowds/ringside.md#doctor)).

After the knockdown, Benlloch waits in whichever neutral corner is farther from where Visser falls ([bout](../world/bout.md#wait)). Visser falls on the steps' side of the diagonal between the white and black corners, so that Benlloch's corner is the far neutral corner and the doctor's way in never passes the winner.

The count's staging sets three more conditions on the fall:

- **Near the centre.** At the finish Visser stands near the ring's centre, just on the steps' side of the white–black diagonal: far enough from it that, with his hips dropping back as he falls, he comes to rest on the steps' side, and no further, so that the far neutral corner stays within a brisk walk. Benlloch stands at kicking range from him, further toward the steps' corner, the distance the finish is thrown from ([exchange study, travel](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#bq-travel)). Benlloch's walk round Visser's feet to the far neutral corner is then a steady, brisk walk, not a hurry, and it ends, facing the centre, by 157 ([bout](../world/bout.md#wait)).
- **Back to the far corner.** Visser lies with his back toward the far neutral corner. Then the referee, kneeling at his face, has both Visser and Benlloch's corner in view ([referee](../cast/referee.md#knockdown), [referee study](../../../research/officiating/referee.md#knockdown-procedure)). So at the finish Benlloch stands on the steps' side of Visser, as the first condition places him, and Visser faces him as he falls.
- **Round the feet.** Benlloch's route to the far corner goes round Visser's feet, never past his head, with the referee between them.

Left to the screenplay: the exact place and the fall's path within these conditions.

The other half, on the far neutral corner's side of that diagonal, is the **far half**. From 168.1 Benlloch celebrates there, in the far half. Visser lies close to the diagonal, and after the result the referee kneels at his head on the far-half side of him, so Benlloch comes no nearer than a stride short of the kneeling referee, where the referee's palm stops him: beside Visser in the wide frame, and never over him ([Benlloch](../cast/benlloch.md#state), [referee](../cast/referee.md#care)).

### Seen from above {#handedness}

Seen from above, the order of the corners, white, the steps' neutral corner, black, the far neutral corner, runs anticlockwise. So a man standing near the centre and facing the steps' corner has the white corner's side on his right and the black corner's side on his left. Visser, facing that corner at the finish, falls to his right, toward the white corner's side, and lies on his right side with his head toward the white corner's side and his feet toward the black corner's ([the commission's fall](../../../instructions/commission.md#fall)).

The screenplay's [finish](../../screenplay/kickboxing.md#s11-b01) established this placement, and the ring keeps it for every scene.

### How the fight uses it {#use}

The whole ring is used ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#whole-ring), [styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#pressure-ring-and-tempo)):

- **The centre:** where the round opens and where the men fight at kicking range.
- **The ropes and corners:** where Visser's cut-offs drive Benlloch, and where Benlloch must pivot or angle out.
- **Open diagonals:** where Benlloch circles to reset the range.

Left to the screenplay: where each phase stands in the ring and how it moves on. Each phase hands the next a changed position ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#progression)).

## State {#state}

### Unchanged through the round {#unchanged}

Nothing in the ring changes during the film: no spill, no blood, nothing thrown in. At the end the canvas holds Visser, and the referee and doctor kneel at him. Benlloch celebrates in the far half.
