# The arena crowd

## Makeup and arrangement {#arrangement}

### Unseen beyond the light {#unseen}

A full arena sits around the ring beyond the apron, with the fighters' corner teams, the judges and the timekeeper at ringside below the canvas. All of them stay in the dark outside the [lit square](../sets/ring.md#lit-square); no camera shows a face or a figure among them.

## Behavior {#behavior}

### A crowd that knows the sport {#knowing}

The crowd is heard throughout as a constant bed of voices that swells with landed blows and near-misses, as [the crowd](../../../research/sound/fight-sound.md#crowd) records. This crowd also rises when a fighter is backed onto the ropes, because it expects a finish there. It reacts to what it sees, not to what the fighters know: it does not notice the arm drop, so it greets the Brazilian kick with a single burst of shock, and holds through the count in a tense, rising noise that breaks into a roar at the wave-off.

### The corners {#corners}

Each corner team shouts to its fighter during the round, short calls that carry over the crowd without distinct words, under [who is heard](../../../research/sound/fight-sound.md#voices). They fall silent at the knockdown and stay outside the ropes.
