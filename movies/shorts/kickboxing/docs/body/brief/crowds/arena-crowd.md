# The arena crowd

The paying audience of the championship night, present and heard, and in the dark beyond the ring ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#venue)).

## Makeup and arrangement {#makeup-facet}

### Who they are {#makeup}

The crowd is a full house of several thousand adults ([coverage study](../../../research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md#crowd-arrangement)). They are drawn as the simplest forms that read as seated and standing people: heads and shoulders in rows ([work](../world/work.md#what-it-cannot-show)). No one in the crowd is told apart from the rest, and no story point rests on who any of them is.

### Where they sit {#arrangement}

Floor seats surround the ring on all four sides, beyond the [officials and corners](ringside.md). Raked tiers rise behind them into the dark of the [arena](../sets/arena.md#layout). Only the first rows are lit enough to read as people. Behind them the crowd falls into silhouette and then darkness ([coverage study](../../../research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md#crowd-in-picture)).

## What they do {#behaviour}

### In picture {#in-picture}

They stay in their seats, rising in the front rows at big moments: the finish, the knockout signal and the celebration. They do not climb toward the ring, wave flags, hold up signs or flash cameras during the round. In picture they are a dim field behind the fighters, never the subject of a shot.

### In sound {#in-sound}

They are heard only as wordless noise: a low bed under the ring's own sounds, with swells, vowel calls, whistles and claps ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#venue), [coverage study](../../../research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md#crowd-sound)). Over the round:

- **Exchanges:** the noise swells when an exchange lands and peaks on a big kick. It falls back when the men reset, and it does not follow every feint.
- **The finish:** a sharp roar starts as the kick lands and holds through the fall.
- **The count:** the noise settles to a murmur with scattered calls, so that each spoken number stays clear. They do not count along.
- **The result:** the noise rises again at the knockout signal and holds high through the bell and the celebration, always under the ring's sounds ([sound](../sound/ring-sound.md#mix)).

They are never silent and never chant a name ([coverage study](../../../research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md#knockout-reaction)). A real crowd often favours one man ([coverage study](../../../research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md#crowd-sound)). This film's crowd favours neither audibly, so that its noise follows what lands rather than who throws it. That is the brief's choice.
