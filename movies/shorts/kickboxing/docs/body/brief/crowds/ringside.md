# Ringside

The people at the ring's edge, outside the ropes: the officials who sit there and the two corner teams. Each behaves as the [rules study's officials](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#officials) do. All are adults and polygon men ([work](../world/work.md#what-it-cannot-show)). Until the knockout each is on screen only at the edge of the frame or behind the fighters. After it, the ring doctor alone enters the ring and joins the group at Visser ([doctor](#doctor)).

## Officials {#officials}

### The officials' table {#officials-table}

The table sits at the centre of one side of the ring, at the foot of the apron. Seated at it are:

- the timekeeper, with the [bell](../props/bell.md#position) in front of him;
- the judge supervisor;
- the scorekeeper;
- the ring announcer.

During the round they watch the ring and keep still. The announcer, like the others, only watches, because his work falls after the film ([work](../world/work.md#left-out)).

### The timekeeper {#timekeeper}

He watches his clock and the referee, and strikes the bell:

- once at 0.0, to open the round;
- from 170.1, to end the match after the knockout signal ([bell](../props/bell.md#strikes), [bout](../world/bout.md#result)).

He does not count the knockdown, and nobody at ringside does ([bout](../world/bout.md#wait)).

### The judges {#judges}

Three judges, the number GLORY allows with its approval, sit just outside the ring, one at the centre of each side except the officials' table's ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#officials)). They watch and write, and do nothing else; no score is shown ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#scoring)). After the knockout they stop writing and watch.

### The ring doctor {#doctor}

The ring doctor sits by the [steps](../sets/ring.md#corners) at a neutral corner, with a paramedic beside him ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#officials)). The doctor is a man in his forties in a dark blue medical tunic and trousers, with pale medical gloves, so he reads apart from the cornermen in white or black and from the referee's white shirt. The doctor watches the whole round. The paramedic, in a green paramedic's uniform, stays at the steps throughout, with the medical bag, and comes in only if the doctor calls him, which does not happen before 180.

At the knockdown he rises and waits at the steps. He enters only when the referee calls him. He then climbs in by those steps and kneels at Visser with the referee. Only he may enter during his examination.

What he visibly does there is care, in the signs the [representation](../world/work.md#faceless-polygon-men) has: he kneels at Visser's head, lays a hand on his shoulder, and bends his head down close to Visser's, as if listening. He does nothing the picture would read as a diagnosis or a treatment. No light is shone at the eyes, no hand goes to the neck, and no equipment is used. The mouthguard he removes cannot be seen and is not written ([work](../world/work.md#what-it-cannot-show), [commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#no-injury-mechanism)).

Left to the screenplay: when he reaches Visser, between the referee's call and 180.

## Corner teams {#corner-teams}

### Two corners, seated {#corners-seated}

Each fighter has three cornermen, seated at his own corner in matching team sportswear: white for [Benlloch](../cast/benlloch.md#wardrobe), black for [Visser](../cast/visser.md#wardrobe). A ring inspector sits with each team and keeps it seated and in order during the round. After the knockout he lets it rise at its seats and keeps it outside the ropes ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#officials)).

They say no words at any point in the film. During the round, through the count and after the result they coach and react by gesture only, and no voice of theirs is heard ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#language), [coverage study](../../../research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md#corner-voices)).

### After the knockout {#corners-after}

- **Benlloch's corner:** they rise and raise their arms in celebration at their seats.
- **Visser's corner:** they rise and lean toward the ropes, watching the doctor.

Neither team enters the ring before 180 ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#venue)). Nobody holds Benlloch up or steps in to join him ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#ending)).
