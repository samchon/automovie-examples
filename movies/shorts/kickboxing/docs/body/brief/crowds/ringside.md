# The ringside people

The officials and corner teams seated around the ring outside the ropes. They may appear in the background of any frame, so each group has behaviour while it is on screen. The ring doctor, who enters the ring, has [his own record](../cast/ring-doctor.md).

## The corner teams {#corner-teams-facet}

### Corner teams {#corner-teams}

Each fighter's team, two men in their corner's colour, sits on the floor outside the ring at its fighter's corner, white at the white corner and black at the black ([ring](../sets/ring.md#corners)). Through the round they lean in toward the ring and react with their bodies to their man's exchanges: a lean, a hand slapping the apron, an arm thrown up. Their calls are never heard as words; in the sound they are only the nearest voices of the wordless crowd ([sound study](../../../research/sound/ring-sound.md#ring)). They stay seated and still through the count, because GLORY forbids them to approach during it ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#knockdown)). At the knockout the white corner's team jumps to its feet and throws its arms up; the black corner's team stands slowly, heads lowered. Neither team enters the ring before 180 ([bout rules](../world/bout.md#result)).

## The officials {#officials}

### Timekeeper {#timekeeper}

A man seated at the officials' table on the table side, watching the clock, who strikes the [bell](../props/bell.md) at the opening and at 170.1 and does nothing else that is seen ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#round)).

### Judges, supervisor and announcer {#table-officials}

Three judges sit at ringside on three sides of the ring, watching and writing, and do not react visibly. The judge supervisor and the ring announcer sit at the officials' table beside the timekeeper. The announcer holds a microphone and does not speak before 180, because the result is announced after the film ([bout rules](../world/bout.md#result)).
