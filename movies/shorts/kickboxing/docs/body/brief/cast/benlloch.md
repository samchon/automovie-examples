# Adrián Benlloch

The commission's A: the challenger, the patient kicker whose right body kicks build the trap, and the man who delivers the finish ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#people)).

## Identity {#identity-facet}

### Who he is {#identity}

Adrián Benlloch is 27, Spanish, from Valencia, and the challenger for the middleweight title ([bout](../world/bout.md#title-bout)). He started in karate at nine and moved to kickboxing in his teens, so his kicking carries a karate base. That base is where his Brazilian kick comes from ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#brazilian-what)). He has trained for eighteen years ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#level-numbers)).

In the screenplay he is BENLLOCH. His name is never heard or shown in the film ([work](../world/work.md#audience)).

### His body {#body}

He is 180 cm and 80 kg, the same height and mass as Visser ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#fighters)), with a long-legged, lighter-shouldered build than Visser's within the same numbers. He fights orthodox: left foot and left hand forward. His right leg is his rear, kicking leg, and his right kicks land on Visser's left side.

## Wardrobe {#wardrobe-facet}

### White, from the white corner {#wardrobe}

He fights from the [white corner](../sets/ring.md#corners). He wears white gloves, which GLORY matches to the corner, and white trunks that stop above the knee. His torso, shins and feet are bare ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#equipment)). His bare right shin and instep are the surfaces of his kicks and of the finish.

His wardrobe does not change during the round.

## Style and repertoire {#style}

### The patient kicker {#repertoire}

He is a precision fighter who wins by reading the other man and setting traps ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#counter-kicker)):

- **Kicks:** right body kicks to Visser's left side; low kicks to the legs Visser plants to punch.
- **Range-finding:** a right cross that touches Visser as it returns, finding the range for his right kick and hiding it as the kick follows. It is a real strike, not an arm held out: GLORY names an outstretched arm without a technique as stalling, and it risks a caution ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#kicker-repertoire), [the GLORY limit](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#kicker-profile)).
- **Check and counter:** he checks a kick with a raised shin, keeps his guard high through the check, and fires back at once while the kicker is still recovering, as one drilled unit.
- **No clinch:** he never takes a hold; at close range he angles out instead.
- **Pressure:** he answers it with a kick at range, lands balanced, keeps his guard high, and leaves on an angle. He gives ground by pivots, 45-degree steps and circling, never straight back ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#kicker-answers-pressure), [angles](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#kicker-angles-feints), [ring use](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#kicker-ring-use)).
- **Defence and punches:** he slips, parries, rolls and blocks the punches that reach him, and throws short combinations of his own when Visser's pressure stalls.

His style costs him ground and, in the look of the round, control: for long stretches the man coming forward seems to be winning ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#kicker-weaknesses)). When his back reaches the ropes, the low kicks he cannot check land on him.

### The feint that tests the drop {#feint}

His kick feint is the start of his right body kick, put back down: the right knee rises as for the kick, then the foot returns to the canvas. It is followed at once by a real strike to the left side of Visser's head, where the left glove has just left.

The commission's "feint that lands" is read this way: the feint draws the drop, and the strike that follows it lands ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#tell-test)). That strike proves the opening the finish will use.

Left to the screenplay: which strike follows the feint, and when.

### The Brazilian kick {#brazilian-kick}

His finish is the right Brazilian, or question-mark, kick. It starts as his right body kick, from the same chamber Visser has learned to answer. Then the knee rises higher, the support foot turns further and the hips turn over, so the shin and instep come down over the lowered left arm onto the side of the head and neck ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#finish), [styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#brazilian-why)).

He hides the difference from the body kick as far as he can. A trained eye, and the camera, can catch the knee travelling in a slightly different direction from the body kick's, but Visser has committed to his body cover before that shows ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#brazilian-chamber)).

He throws it once, and it lands at 150.000 seconds, at the legal target the [bout](../world/bout.md#finish-target) sets. Its law belongs to the screenplay's own study ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#scope)).

### His level {#level}

At championship level:

- his guard stays high through his kicks and checks;
- he lands balanced and returns to his stance after every kick;
- he throws nothing he does not need;
- he reads Visser's head and trunk rather than his limbs;
- he breathes out sharply on each strike ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#level-markers)).

## Habits {#habits}

### He watches before he commits {#watches}

He spends the early round reading: he finds the range, tests Visser's reactions and lets Visser lead ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#reading-the-opponent)). When he has seen something, he repeats the action that showed it before he exploits it. That is why the right body kick comes again and again, and why the feint comes before the finish.

He shows no tell of his own that Visser reads.

## Voice {#voice-facet}

### Breath, no words {#voice}

He never speaks ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#language)). His voice is breath:

- a short, sharp exhale through his teeth on each strike, slightly longer on a kick ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#exhale-sounds));
- breath that grows heavier through the round, though less than Visser's;
- after the knockout, loud, excited breath as he celebrates, and no word or cry ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#ending)).

## Values and motives {#motives}

### A challenger with a plan {#challenger}

He came for the title with a plan. He will kick Visser's body so that the pressure slows and costs Visser breath, take the ground he has to give, and wait. He fights patiently because he believes a pressure fighter gives himself away in the end.

He respects Visser. He touches gloves openly, obeys every break and does not taunt, even in victory. His joy is for himself and his corner, not against the fallen man ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#respect)).

## Relationships {#relationships}

### With Visser {#with-visser}

This is their first fight. He has studied the champion's pressure on footage and respects it. He expects to spend the round under it, and he does.

After the knockout, his celebration keeps to the [far half](../sets/ring.md#corners) of the ring, and he does not stand over Visser. Visser's view of him is in [Visser's record](visser.md#with-benlloch).

## Knowledge {#knowledge-facet}

### What he knows, and when {#knowledge}

He comes in knowing Visser's style and with the plan to drain him with body kicks. He has the Brazilian kick in his repertoire, but no plan yet to use it: whether it can land depends on what Visser's guard does.

During the round:

- he sees Visser's left arm begin to drop at his rising right knee ([Visser's tell](visser.md#tell));
- he tests the drop with the [feint](#feint), and the strike behind it lands;
- he then knows the opening is there, and that it comes with the start of his body kick.

He throws the finish on that knowledge. The audience learns each step as he does, and Visser never understands what is being done to him ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#direction)).

## State through the round {#state-facet}

### Ground, stance and breath {#state}

At the bell he is fresh, unhurt and fully in guard.

Through the round his state changes only as landed blows and work change it, and never as far as a knockdown ([bout](../world/bout.md#costs), [styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#kick-costs)):

- **After a landed low kick:** his lead foot settles lighter, his stance narrows or shortens, and he shifts his weight off the kicked leg a moment before he moves again.
- **After a punch to the head:** glancing or taken on his guard. He resets his stance and his range.
- **After a punch to the body:** a forced breath, his elbow clamped to that side, and a step out on an angle before he kicks again ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#body-kick-cost)).
- **After the clinch knee:** held on the ropes, he takes it with a forced breath and a fold of the trunk into the hold. Once released, he pivots off the ropes before he does anything else ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#body-kick-cost), [ring use](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#kicker-ring-use)).
- **After a kick Visser checks:** his shin is stung, and he settles that leg a moment before he moves again ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#low-kick-cost)).
- **Breath:** it grows across the round. He shows his cost more in his narrowing stance than in breath ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#round-one-cost)).

After the knockdown he goes to the [neutral corner](../sets/ring.md#corners) and waits there, facing the centre, through the whole count. From 168.1 he celebrates in the [far half](../sets/ring.md#corners) of the ring: gloves raised, quick steps on his own planted feet, each step landing and bearing him, and loud, excited breath. He stays visible beside the downed man in the wide ending ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#ending)).
