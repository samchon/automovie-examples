# Bastien

## Identity {#identity}

### The pressure fighter {#who}

Bastien is a fictional 31-year-old professional kickboxer, the commission's fighter B, who works out of the white corner. He receives the finish. The film never says his name aloud.

## Body and recognition {#body}

### Build {#build}

An adult man 180 cm tall and 80 kg, the same height and weight as Arjan, a little leaner in the trunk and broader in the calves.

### How he is told apart {#recognition}

White gloves and royal-blue trunks, and darker skin than Arjan's, so he reads as himself from every side and at every crossing of the line.

## Movement style {#movement}

### Stance {#stance}

Orthodox, like Arjan: left foot and hand forward, knees bent, feet about shoulder width and weight even, under the [stance](../../../research/kickboxing/ring-craft.md#stance). He holds his gloves high at his cheeks and his elbows on his ribs.

### Rhythm {#rhythm}

Busy and forward. He keeps a light step going between exchanges, takes the centre behind his double jab, and cuts the ring off rather than following, as [cutting off the ring](../../../research/kickboxing/ring-craft.md#cut-off) describes. When his rear foot finds the ropes he does not cover up there: he steps one way along them and drives back the other way, as [the ropes](../../../research/kickboxing/ring-craft.md#ropes) describes.

## Repertoire and proficiency {#repertoire}

### Weapons {#weapons}

A championship-level volume fighter who loves the long combination and the low kick, as [combinations](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#combinations) records: the double jab; jab, cross and left hook; jab, cross, left hook to the body and right low kick; jab, cross, hook and right high kick; the switch kick to the body; and the clinch knee. He sells the low kick by dragging his rear foot and turning his hip, then throws the cross or the real kick, under [feints](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#feints).

### Defence {#defence}

He parries the jab with his right glove, covers high, slips and rolls with his knees and hips while his head stays turned to Arjan, blocks body shots and body kicks with his near elbow and forearm, turns Arjan's teep aside with a forearm, and checks low kicks with his left shin. Arjan throws no high kick before the finish, so Bastien never needs a high block. He counters from each answer at once, under [defence that attacks](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#defence): a right cross from the replanted foot after a check, a left hook from outside after a slip, a hook or uppercut as he rises from a roll, and his own right low kick after a blocked body kick.

## Habits and tells {#habits}

### Stays on the checked leg {#stays-on-the-leg}

When his low kick is checked, he is slow to bring the checked leg back down: he stays on his support leg a beat too long before replanting, which gives a counter its moment.

### Drops the left arm {#drops-the-left-arm}

He does not have this habit when the round begins: at first he answers each of Arjan's kicks by its line. Once Arjan's right body kick has landed on his left ribs, he answers the next right kicks with his left elbow brought down over those ribs, and from then on he drops that arm whenever Arjan's right knee rises, by the time the knee is folded and before the kick can be read, every time, as [habits](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#habits) describes. The drop comes on top of his other answers, not in their place: when the rising knee turns into a right low kick he answers it as he would without the drop, checking it with his left shin, his dropped elbow closing down toward that knee, unless a hook has just loaded his lead leg, and a right knee in the clinch meets the dropped elbow. So the habit costs him nothing against Arjan's kicks to the leg and body or his knees that he would not have lost anyway; it leaves open only the left side of his head above the lowered arm. The screenplay's scene where the first body kick lands establishes it.

## Speech and voice {#voice}

### Silent {#silent}

He says nothing in the ring. His voice is breath: sharp exhales in his combinations, a grunt when his body is hit, and breathing that stays loud and ragged on the canvas after the finish.

## Values and motives {#values}

### Outwork him {#objective}

Bastien is confident and rhythmic, a fighter who wins rounds by throwing more. His aim in this round is to own the centre and break down Arjan's lead leg with low kicks. He respects Arjan as his equal.

## Relationships {#relationships}

### With Arjan and the referee {#opponent}

He touches gloves before the first exchange, treats Arjan as an equal and obeys the referee at once.

## Knowledge {#knowledge}

### Misreads the knee {#misreads-the-knee}

His body has learned that a rising right knee from Arjan means the body kick, because the first ones he felt did, and the arm goes down before he can think. Even after a rising knee brings him a cross instead of a kick, the drop stays a reflex that his judgment cannot stop in time, so he still meets the last rising knee with his arm, not his eyes.

## State changes {#state}

### Through the round {#through-the-round}

He starts fresh and unmarked. The body kicks and body punches he takes cost him breath and make the left arm's drop quicker. The finish drops him without a step onto his right side, as [the fall](../../../research/kickboxing/knockout.md#fall) describes. He lies there breathing hard; before eight he tries once to push himself up on his left arm and the arm gives way, under [on the canvas](../../../research/kickboxing/knockout.md#downed-fighter), and he stays down through the count, the wave-off and the celebration, with the referee and then the ring doctor kneeling beside him.
