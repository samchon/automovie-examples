# Bram Visser

The commission's B: the defending champion, the pressure fighter who leads much of the round, and the man whose learned habit decides it ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#people)).

## Identity {#identity-facet}

### Who he is {#identity}

Bram Visser is 31, Dutch, from Amsterdam, and the promotion's defending middleweight champion ([bout](../world/bout.md#title-bout)). He came up through a Dutch kickboxing gym from the age of thirteen and has fought professionally for most of the eighteen years since ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#level-numbers)).

In the screenplay he is VISSER. His name is never heard or shown in the film ([work](../world/work.md#audience)).

### His body {#body}

He is 180 cm and 80 kg, the same height and mass as Benlloch ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#fighters)), with a lean, heavy-shouldered fighter's build. He fights orthodox: left foot and left hand forward, right side back. His left side therefore faces Benlloch's right kicks.

## Wardrobe {#wardrobe-facet}

### Black, from the black corner {#wardrobe}

He fights from the [black corner](../sets/ring.md#corners). He wears black gloves, which GLORY matches to the corner, and black trunks that stop above the knee. His torso, shins and feet are bare ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#equipment)). The black gloves are what the audience follows when his left arm drops. They read against his bare body and against the canvas.

His wardrobe does not change during the round.

## Style and repertoire {#style}

### The Dutch pressure fighter {#repertoire}

He fights in the Dutch style that runs through K-1 and GLORY ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#pressure-fighter)):

- He walks forward behind a high, tight guard and a broad stance.
- He cuts the ring off rather than following, moving across in front of the other man to close his exits toward the ropes and corners.
- He punches in long, varied combinations, sometimes to the body, and finishes them with a low kick. He never throws the same combination twice running.
- He answers a kick with a check or a step, then his own low kick to the landing leg.
- He slips, rolls and parries inside the other man's punches to get close.
- He feints to load his attacks: a jab feint or a dip of the shoulders to draw a reaction before he steps in, and a hip-snap feint of the low kick to make the other man lift his lead leg, then a kick to the leg as it lands. Feints serve his pressure rather than replace it ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#pressure-ring-and-tempo), [Kaman's feint](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#tell-test)).

He sets a high pace from the first bell and works to win the round, and the bout, early ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#pressure-ring-and-tempo)).

### His weaknesses {#weaknesses}

His style has costs that Benlloch's plan uses ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#pressure-weaknesses)):

- He plants his feet to punch hard. While his lead leg is loaded he cannot easily lift it to check a kick.
- He spends breath fast.
- He walks into counters and kicks as he comes in.

These are why a body kick that arrives while he is planted lands on his left side, and why he covers it with his arm rather than his shin ([tell](#tell)).

### His level {#level}

At championship level he keeps his guard high while he punches and returns to his stance after every action. He varies his attacks, reads an opponent's head and trunk rather than his limbs, and breathes out sharply on each strike ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#level-markers)).

His one lapse is the habit below, which grows out of a good defence rather than out of carelessness.

## Habits and tells {#habits}

### Left elbow to the ribs when Benlloch's right knee rises {#tell}

The habit:

- **Trigger:** Benlloch's right knee rising.
- **Response:** Visser's left elbow drops to his left ribs, and his left glove comes down from the side of his head with it ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#tell)).

It is the disciplined elbow block, degraded under pressure. A rising knee tells a defender which leg is kicking, but not yet whether the kick goes to the head or the body. Visser commits to covering his body at the knee, before the kick has shown its height ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#tell-formation), [anticipation](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#anticipation-evidence)).

The habit has three stages, and each comes only after the last:

1. **Absent at the start of the round.** Against Benlloch's first right kicks he covers correctly, with the elbow at the ribs and the glove still at his head, or he checks with the shin when he is not planted.
2. **Forming.** Once right body kicks have landed on his left side while he was planted to punch, his cover comes sooner, at the first rise of the knee, and grows bigger. His left glove leaves his head with it. Each body kick that lands makes the next cover come sooner. The block also lets him counter with the same left arm, which is why he keeps doing it.
3. **Fixed.** By the time Benlloch tests it, the drop follows every rise of Benlloch's right knee, whatever that knee then does.

He does not know he is doing it ([knowledge](#knowledge)).

After Benlloch's feint proves the opening, Visser carries his left glove higher in neutral moments. But the drop is a reflex that fires before thought, so his elbow still drops when the knee rises again.

Left to the screenplay: which kicks land, when, and the moment each stage is first seen.

## Voice {#voice-facet}

### Breath, no words {#voice}

He never speaks ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#language)). His voice is breath:

- a short, sharp exhale through his teeth on each strike, slightly longer on a kick, never a shout ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#exhale-sounds));
- a hard, forced breath when a body kick lands;
- breathing that grows louder and shorter through the round;
- after the knockout, ragged breaths and the scrape of a glove on the canvas.

## Values and motives {#motives}

### A champion who presses {#champion}

He fights to keep his title by taking the fight to the challenger and finishing him early. He presses because he believes pressure wins rounds and breaks men, and in most of his fights it has.

He respects the sport and his opponent. He touches gloves openly, obeys every break and never protests to the referee ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#respect)).

## Relationships {#relationships}

### With Benlloch {#with-benlloch}

This is their first fight. He has watched Benlloch's earlier fights and expects a kicker who gives ground. He means to cut him off against the ropes before the kicks can take effect.

He treats Benlloch as a serious challenger, not as an easy defence. Benlloch's view of him is in [Benlloch's record](benlloch.md#with-visser).

## Knowledge {#knowledge-facet}

### What he knows and does not know {#knowledge}

He knows Benlloch's style from footage, and he knows that he is being kicked to the body. He knows the body kicks hurt, and he covers them because they hurt.

He does not know that his cover now starts at the knee and takes his left glove down from his head, or that Benlloch has seen it.

After the feint he knows one thing more: he has been hit on the left side of his head, after a kick that never came. So he carries his left glove higher when nothing is coming ([tell](#tell)). He does not connect the blow to his own elbow drop, and he does not read the feint as a test. He takes it as a body kick Benlloch thought better of, and the strike that followed as a blow he did not see coming.

## State through the round {#state-facet}

### Breath, guard and balance {#state}

At the bell he is fresh, unhurt and fully in guard.

Through the round his state changes only as landed blows and work change it, and never as far as a knockdown before 150 seconds ([bout](../world/bout.md#costs), [styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#kick-costs)):

- **After each landed right body kick:** a sharp, audible exhale; a brief fold toward his left side; his left elbow clamped to the ribs; and a step or two in which he does not press.
- **After kicks Benlloch checks:** his kicking shin is stung, he settles that leg a moment, and he throws fewer low kicks afterwards.
- **Breath:** it grows across the round. Because he has taken body kicks he breathes harder and shorter than Benlloch by the finish ([styles study](../../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#round-one-cost)).
- **Guard:** high and correct at first. Then it is pulled low on the left at each rising right knee ([tell](#tell)).

After 150 seconds he is down on his right side and cannot rise by ten ([bout](../world/bout.md#unable-to-rise)). The film ends with him still on the canvas, attended by the [referee](referee.md#care) and by the [ring doctor](../crowds/ringside.md#doctor) once the doctor has been called.
