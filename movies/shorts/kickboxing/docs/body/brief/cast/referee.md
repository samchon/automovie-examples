# The referee

The adult man who controls the bout from inside the ring, always visible and always active: positioning, side view, clinch breaks, signals and the knockdown procedure ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#referee)). The screenplay calls him the REFEREE; he has no name in the film.

## Identity {#identity}

### Who he is {#who}

An experienced professional kickboxing referee, an adult man of ordinary build, fit enough to move with two middleweights for a whole round. He is the only person exclusively responsible for decisions during the match ([referee study](../../../research/officiating/referee.md#officials)). He works by the [bout rules](../world/bout.md) and the conduct the [referee study](../../../research/officiating/referee.md#level-markers) records for a professional.

### Attire {#attire}

A grey short-sleeved shirt, a black bow tie, black trousers and black soft-soled shoes, within the choices the [referee study](../../../research/officiating/referee.md#attire) records. Fully dressed and in grey, he never reads as either fighter, who are bare-chested in white or black ([gloves](../props/gloves.md#corner-colours)).

## Voice {#voice}

### English commands, few and loud {#commands}

He is the only person who speaks in the film, always in English ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#language)). He speaks only the ring's words, loud and short, never a "catchy" phrase ([referee study](../../../research/officiating/referee.md#commands)):

- "Fight!" at the opening bell;
- "Break!" to separate a clinch held past its knee;
- "Down!" with a point at the canvas when Roux goes down;
- "Neutral corner!" with an arm and finger pointing Vos to it;
- "One" to "Ten", one number a second, to Roux;
- "Doctor!" after the knockout, turning to the ring doctor;
- "Stay down." to Roux, low, only after the knockout, while he keeps him lying still.

"Stop!" is in his vocabulary but no foul or stoppage calls for it before 150. Every word he speaks is one of these, and when he speaks matters: he says nothing during clean exchanges.

## How he works {#work}

### Positioning {#positioning}

At the opening bell he stands at the centre between the two men ([ring](../sets/ring.md#corners)). Through the round he keeps a side view, standing off the fighters' line, side-on to both, and moves with them smoothly, never stationary long while they move ([referee study](../../../research/officiating/referee.md#positioning)). In open exchanges he holds a working distance; when they reach the ropes, a corner or a clinch he closes in on the open side, never trapping them; and in the last phases before 150 he tightens his distance, because a stoppage may be near. He keeps to the side of the fighters away from the camera, so he reads beyond them, between and behind the two men ([camera](../cinematography/camera.md#referee-beyond)). The distances and rates he keeps are the [referee study](../../../research/officiating/referee.md#targets)'s.

### Gaze and posture {#gaze}

His attention reads from his head and trunk, because he has no face ([work record](../world/work.md#faceless-polygon-men)): in live action his head follows the midpoint of the two men and his chest stays side-on to their line; at the knockdown his head snaps to Roux and then to Vos as he sends him away; through the count his head is down to Roux with glances up to Vos in the corner; after the knockout he is low over Roux and turned away from the celebration ([referee study](../../../research/officiating/referee.md#gaze-and-posture)). He moves light on flexed knees while the men fight and is low and still over Roux at the count.

### The clinch {#clinch}

When the men clinch for one knee he closes in with a hand ready and says nothing, because a clean clinch releases itself ([bout rules](../world/bout.md#one-knee-clinch)). If a grip outlasts its knee he steps in, says "Break!", puts an open hand on each man at full arm's length, and steps straight back out, never walking between them; they step back and fight on without "Fight!" ([referee study](../../../research/officiating/referee.md#clinch-break)).

### The knockdown procedure {#knockdown-procedure}

He runs the procedure of the [referee study](../../../research/officiating/referee.md#knockdown-procedure) against the commission's anchors ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#count)):

1. As the finish lands he moves in on the line between the men, so no further strike can reach Roux.
2. As Roux's body meets the canvas he calls "Down!" and points at the floor, at once.
3. He points Vos to the farther neutral corner: "Neutral corner!"
4. While Vos walks there and turns, he goes to Roux and crouches or kneels near his head, placed so he can still see Vos.
5. With Vos in the corner facing the centre, he starts the count at 157, raising his counting hand where Roux can see it.
6. He counts "One" at 158 through "Ten" at 167, one number a second, loud and clear, showing the matching fingers to Roux, glancing at Vos; at "Eight" he does not pause ([bout rules](../world/bout.md#count)).
7. At 167.6 he rises over Roux and waves both arms crossed above his head: the knockout.
8. He calls the ring doctor in, then goes straight back to Roux.

He never touches Roux during the count; a referee does not help a counted man ([referee study](../../../research/officiating/referee.md#knockdown-procedure)).

### Care of the fallen man {#care}

After the knockout his duty is Roux, not the winner. He kneels at Roux's head and shoulder, facing him, a hand resting on his shoulder or upper arm to keep him lying still, and speaks low to him; he guides the doctor in. He does not lift Roux, roll him or take out his mouthguard, all of which are the doctor's work ([referee study](../../../research/officiating/referee.md#knockdown-procedure)). He does not go to Vos, and if Vos comes near he keeps his body between them ([referee study](../../../research/officiating/referee.md#winner)). He is still attending Roux in the wide ending at 180 ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#ending)).

## Relationships {#relationships}

### Neutral authority {#neutral}

He treats both men alike and both obey him at once: they fight on his "Fight!", step back on his "Break!", and Vos goes to the corner on his point. Neither fighter ever argues with him. With the ring doctor he works as a pair after the knockout: he calls, the doctor comes, and he stays ([ring doctor](ring-doctor.md#conduct)).
