# Andries Vos

The commission's fighter A: the white-corner man, who delivers the finish ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#fighters)).

## Identity {#identity}

### Name {#name}

Andries Vos, a Dutch professional middleweight. The screenplay calls him Vos. His name is never spoken in the film; the audience knows him as the man in white ([work record](../world/work.md#audience)).

### Body {#body}

An adult man, 180 cm tall and 80 kg, with the lean, muscled build of a full-time kickboxer at fighting weight, the same height and weight as Roux ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#fighters)). Nothing about his body gives him an advantage of size or reach; the bout is decided by craft.

### Wardrobe {#wardrobe}

White trunks that stop above the knee, white gloves, bare feet and bare upper body, as GLORY's kit rules set ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#ring-and-equipment); [gloves](../props/gloves.md#corner-colours)). His wardrobe does not change.

## How he fights {#fighting}

### Level and stance {#level}

He fights at GLORY championship level, with the markers the [exchanges study](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#level-markers) records: gloves home between strikes, every kick returned to the stance before the next action, short chains of two or three strikes with an occasional longer one, most strikes blocked rather than taken, and technique kept while breathing hard. He stands orthodox, left foot forward, so his right kick is his rear-leg kick and lands on an orthodox opponent's left side ([exchanges study](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#tell)).

### Repertoire {#repertoire}

He has the whole exchange vocabulary the commission names and uses each of it during the round ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#exchange-vocabulary)):

- combinations in the chains the exchanges study records, above all punches that open the way for a kick ([exchanges study](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#chaining));
- feints of the knee and the shoulder ([exchanges study](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#feints));
- checks of Roux's low kicks with the lead shin, each answered from the replanted leg with a right hand or a low kick ([exchanges study](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#check-counter));
- slips and parries against the jab and cross, rolls under the hook, and the high guard and arm blocks as his first defence ([exchanges study](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#defences));
- footwork that steps off the line, pivots after a kick and circles away from the ropes ([exchanges study](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#ring)).

His signature weapon is the right round kick to the body. Behind it he has drilled the right Brazilian kick to the head, which starts from the same knee lift ([Brazilian kick study](../../../research/kickboxing/brazilian-kick.md#identity)), but he has never thrown it in a recorded professional fight, so no footage of him shows it.

### The finishing kick {#the-finishing-kick}

His Brazilian kick follows the [Brazilian kick study](../../../research/kickboxing/brazilian-kick.md#law): it starts exactly as his right body kick, from the body kick's range and with no step; the knee rises toward the ribs, then the support foot turns, the hips roll over and the knee climbs above the target, and the instep and lower shin come down over the lowered arm onto the side of the head and neck ([bout rules](../world/bout.md#finish-target)). He throws it once in the round, at 150.000, and lands it ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#finish)). Before then he never throws a high kick from that knee lift, so nothing in the round, as nothing in his footage, warns Roux ([Roux](roux.md#knowledge)).

## Mind {#mind}

### Personality and values {#personality}

Patient, watchful and economical. He prefers being right to being busy, takes a blow without showing it more than his body must, and respects Roux as an equal: he means the opening touch, and he does not taunt. He wants to win by being the better-prepared man, not by overwhelming anyone.

### Game plan {#game-plan}

He expects Roux to come forward early, so he weathers the pressure behind a high guard and checks, makes Roux pay for each entry with a right body kick to his open left side, and moves off the line rather than trading on the ropes ([exchanges study](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#ring)). The plan is slow on purpose: the body kicks are meant to cost Roux breath and to make him respect the kick. When Roux begins to drop his arm for it, Vos tests the drop and then uses it ([Roux](roux.md#the-tell)). He takes the initiative in stretches, not all at once, so the lead changes hands across the round ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#progression)).

### Knowledge {#knowledge}

At the bell he knows Roux's style from footage of his earlier fights: a pressure fighter of long combinations and low kicks. He does not know Roux has any habit about his left arm, because Roux has none at the bell. He learns of the drop during the round by watching Roux's left arm when his own right knee rises, and he is sure of it only after his feint proves it. The audience sees each step of that learning as he does ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#direction)).

### Relationship with Roux {#relationship}

They have never fought before; each has studied the other on footage. Their relationship is the respect of two contenders meeting for the right to a title fight, with no grudge, and it holds from the touch to the end: after the knockout Vos celebrates but does not go near Roux while the referee attends him ([after the count](#after-the-count)). Roux's side of it is in [his record](roux.md#relationship).

## Voice {#voice}

### Breath only {#breath}

He never speaks ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#language)). His voice is breath: steady and controlled early, a short forced exhale on each committed strike, heavier as the round goes on ([sound study](../../../research/sound/ring-sound.md#breath)). In the celebration his breath is loud, fast and excited ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#ending)).

## States over the round {#states}

### The cost of blows {#cost-of-blows}

Before 150 Roux's blows cost him breath, guard and balance and never more: a low kick to his lead leg unweights it and spoils his next punch, a body shot forces breath out of him, a glancing punch turns his head, but his legs stay sturdy and his hands come back up ([exchanges study](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#non-finishing-contacts); [commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#contacts-before-finish)). Roux's low kicks accumulate on his lead leg, so he checks more as the round goes on. He is not down at any point.

### After the finish {#after-the-finish}

When the kick lands he does not strike again: he turns from Roux at the referee's "Down!" and walks to the neutral corner the referee points to, the one farther from Roux, and stands there facing the centre of the ring by 157, gloves down at his sides, off the ropes, breathing hard, until the count ends ([bout rules](../world/bout.md#count)). He watches the count without moving his feet.

### After the count {#after-the-count}

From 168.1 he celebrates: gloves raised high, quick victory steps across the open canvas, each carried on his own planted feet, loud excited breath, staying visible beside the downed Roux in the wide ending, as the commission's [ending](../../../instructions/commission.md#ending) rules and reads its "supported victory steps". He keeps clear of the place where the referee kneels with Roux.
