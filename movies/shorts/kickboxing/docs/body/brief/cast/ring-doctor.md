# The ring doctor

The physician GLORY requires at the ring during every match, who enters it in this film only after the knockout ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#medical-and-result)). The screenplay calls him the RING DOCTOR.

## Identity {#identity}

### Who he is {#who}

An adult man in dark clothes with a small medical bag, the simplest form that reads as an official who is not the referee ([work record](../world/work.md#what-it-cannot-show)). He never speaks: only the referee's words are heard ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#language)).

## Place and conduct {#place-and-conduct}

### His place {#place}

He sits on the floor outside the ring by the steps at the table neutral corner, through the whole round ([ring](../sets/ring.md#neutral-corners); [referee study](../../../research/officiating/referee.md#knockdown-procedure)). When he is in the frame before 150, he is watching the fighters, seated.

### His conduct {#conduct}

He rises when Roux goes down and waits at the steps through the count, because the count is running and a counted man is not yet his patient ([bout rules](../world/bout.md#count)). Only at the referee's call after the knockout does he climb to the apron and enter through the ropes at that corner. He kneels at Roux on the side the referee leaves him and attends him there; the referee stays beside them ([referee](referee.md#care)). Whether he reaches Roux before or after the match-ending bell at 170.1 is the screenplay's choice; what he does to examine Roux is never shown in medical detail ([work record](../world/work.md#left-out)).
