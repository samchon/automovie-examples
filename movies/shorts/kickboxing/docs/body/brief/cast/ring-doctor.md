# The ring doctor

## Identity {#identity}

### The physician on duty {#who}

The bout's ringside physician, a middle-aged woman, sitting at ringside in the dark beside a neutral corner until she is needed. The film never names her.

## Body and recognition {#body}

### Dress {#dress}

Of medium build, in a dark navy jacket and trousers and pale blue medical gloves, with a small bag in one hand, so she reads at once as neither a fighter nor the referee.

## Movement style {#movement}

### Quick and careful {#quick}

She moves fast but never runs on the canvas: up the [steps](../sets/ring.md#large-ring) to the apron, through the ropes between the second and third rope at the neutral corner nearest the fallen man, and straight to him.

## Repertoire {#repertoire}

### Attending the fallen fighter {#attending}

She kneels at the fallen fighter's head, next to the referee who stays kneeling at his side, sets her bag down and bends close to him, one hand at his head, without moving him, as the [aftermath](../../../research/kickboxing/knockout.md#aftermath) rules allow only her to do.

## Speech and voice {#voice}

### Not heard {#not-heard}

She speaks quietly to the fallen man, and nothing she says is heard over the crowd.

## Values and motives {#values}

### Her patient {#patient}

Her only concern is the fallen fighter; she ignores the winner and the crowd.

## Relationships {#relationships}

### With the referee {#with-referee}

The referee, already kneeling at the fallen man's side by his chest, leaves his head free for her and stays where he is while she works.

## State changes {#state}

### Her entrance {#entrance}

She stays outside the ring and unseen through the round and the count, and climbs in only after the bell has ended the match.
