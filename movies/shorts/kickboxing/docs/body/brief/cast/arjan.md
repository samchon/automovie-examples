# Arjan

## Identity {#identity}

### The patient kicker {#who}

Arjan is a fictional 29-year-old professional kickboxer, the commission's fighter A, who works out of the black corner. He is the man who throws the finish. The film never says his name aloud.

## Body and recognition {#body}

### Build {#build}

An adult man 180 cm tall and 80 kg, the same height and weight as Bastien, with a fighter's broad shoulders, heavy thighs and long shins.

### How he is told apart {#recognition}

Black gloves and black trunks with a red waistband, and lighter skin than Bastien's, so he reads as himself from the front, the side and behind, whichever way the camera crosses the fighters' line.

## Movement style {#movement}

### Stance {#stance}

Orthodox: left foot and left hand forward, knees bent so either leg can lift at once, feet about shoulder width and weight even, as the [stance](../../../research/kickboxing/ring-craft.md#stance) describes. He carries his chin down, his gloves at his cheekbones and his elbows close to his ribs.

### Rhythm {#rhythm}

Measured. He moves only as much as the distance needs, stepping rather than bouncing, and his lead glove keeps reaching out toward Bastien's gloves to read the range and coming straight back, never held out. After his own combinations he steps off the line toward the side his last strike came from, sliding out to his right after a right-hand or right-leg finish and pivoting on his lead foot to his left after a left-hand finish, never backing straight out, under the [exit](../../../research/kickboxing/ring-craft.md#exit).

## Repertoire and proficiency {#repertoire}

### Weapons {#weapons}

A championship-level kicker in the Dutch manner: punch combinations that end in a kick, as [combinations](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#combinations) records. His right body kick, thrown off a jab or a jab and cross, is his main tool; he also throws the right low kick, the teep to the stomach with his lead (left) leg, the right cross to the body, the clinch knee, and the shoulder feint that shows the first part of his jab.

### Feints {#feints}

Besides the shoulder feint, he owns the body-kick feint: his right knee rises into the body kick's chamber and comes back down without kicking, and his right cross follows over whatever the answer has opened, as the fake body kick in [feints](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#feints) describes.

### Defence {#defence}

He parries jabs with his right glove, covers hooks, rolls under them, slips outside the cross, blocks body shots and body kicks with his elbow and forearm, blocks high kicks with his forearm beside his head, and checks low kicks with his left shin. He counters from each answer at once, under [defence that attacks](../../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#defence).

### The Brazilian kick {#brazilian-kick}

He keeps one rare weapon in reserve: the right Brazilian kick. It leaves from the same chamber as his right body kick, so it shows the same rising knee, then turns over and comes down: his instep and lower shin descend over the opponent's lowered left arm onto the side of the neck behind the left ear, where the jaw meets the neck, as the [Brazilian kick study](../../../research/kickboxing/brazilian-kick.md#law) records. He throws it once, only after he has seen the opening it needs.

## Habits and tells {#habits}

### Checks the drag {#checks-the-drag}

When Bastien drags his rear foot and turns his hip as if to kick low, Arjan lifts his lead leg to check before he can see whether the kick is real, every time. Bastien can sell him a low kick and hit him while he stands on one leg.

## Speech and voice {#voice}

### Silent {#silent}

He says nothing in the ring. His voice is his breathing: a short exhale with each strike, a held breath when he is hit in the body, breathing that deepens as the round goes on, and a wordless shout when he wins.

## Values and motives {#values}

### Building the pattern {#objective}

Arjan is patient and methodical, a fighter who would rather be hit while he builds something than win the exchanges that do not matter. His aim in this round is to make Bastien expect the right body kick and then use that expectation. He respects Bastien as his equal.

## Relationships {#relationships}

### With Bastien and the referee {#opponent}

He touches gloves with Bastien before the first exchange and treats him as an equal throughout. He obeys the referee at once: he stops at "Down!", goes where he is sent and stays there until the result.

## Knowledge {#knowledge}

### Reading the tell {#reads-the-tell}

From the moment Bastien first blocks his body kick with a dropping left arm, Arjan watches for that arm. He knows the drop comes at the rising knee, before the kick is readable, and that it costs Bastien nothing low: his right low kicks are still checked and his clinch knee still meets the elbow. Only the head above the lowered arm is open, so he spends nothing on the drop until he can reach that head. Before he risks the finish he proves it with the body-kick feint: the knee rises, the arm drops, and his right cross lands over it. Once the habit is set he varies what follows the rising knee, so Bastien cannot tell the feint from the kick.

## State changes {#state}

### Through the round {#through-the-round}

He starts fresh and unmarked. Bastien's low kicks to his left thigh and the clinch knee to his stomach cost him breath; from the third low kick that lands on his left thigh, the leg is slow to take his weight for a moment after each landing. After the knockdown he walks to the neutral corner and stands there facing the centre through the count, still but for his breathing. He celebrates only once the referee waves the fight off: gloves raised, turning to the crowd, quick light steps that each land balanced under him, and loud breath, never coming toward Bastien or past the referee and never gesturing at Bastien, as [the winner's conduct](../../../research/kickboxing/knockout.md#celebration) describes.
