# The bell

The timekeeper's ring gong, the commission's start and match-ending bells ([commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#sound)).

## Form and position {#form}

### A gong at the foot of the ring {#position}

It is a round metal gong on a wooden mount, struck with a metal hammer on a chain ([coverage study](../../../research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md#bell-strike)). It stands on the officials' table at the centre of one side ([coverage study](../../../research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md#bell-position)), below the ropes and at canvas level, in front of the [timekeeper](../crowds/ringside.md#timekeeper). In picture it reads as a small round disc at the foot of the ring, when the frame includes that side at all.

## Use {#use}

### When it is struck {#strikes}

It is struck at two moments of the film, and only at two:

- **Opening bell:** one strike at 0.0 opens the round ([bout](../world/bout.md#start-sequence)).
- **Match-ending bell:** a run of three quick strikes from 170.1 ends the match after the knockout ([bout](../world/bout.md#result)).

No ten-second warning sounds, because the round ends before its mark ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#bells)). No gavel marks the count ([coverage study](../../../research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md#bell-strike)).

### How it sounds {#sound}

Each strike has a hard metal attack and a ringing decay that carries under the next sounds. It carries over the crowd from the officials' side of the ring ([coverage study](../../../research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md#bell-sound)).
