# The gloves

The fighters' boxing gloves: the only equipment the film shows on them besides their trunks, and the audience's way of telling them apart.

## Form and colour {#form}

### GLORY's taped gloves {#gloves}

Each man wears the gloves GLORY supplies for his weight class, laced and taped at the wrist ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#ring-and-equipment)). In the representation each glove is one rounded padded form at the end of the forearm; the padded striking face and the thumb side read from its shape, and nothing printed on it is visible ([work record](../world/work.md#what-it-cannot-show)).

### Corner colours {#corner-colours}

The gloves match the fighter's corner: [Vos](../cast/vos.md) wears white gloves from the white corner, [Roux](../cast/roux.md) black gloves from the black corner ([rules study](../../../research/kickboxing/rules.md#knockdown); [ring](../sets/ring.md#corners)). Each man's trunks are in the same colour, so a glance at either hand or the hips names the man. The colours hold from the first frame to the last; no story point needs them to change.

## Use {#use}

### What the gloves do {#glove-use}

Gloves touch gloves at the opening touch ([bout rules](../world/bout.md#glove-touch)), strike only with their padded face, parry and block, and frame the head in the high guard. In the clinch they grip the neck or arms for one knee and let go ([bout rules](../world/bout.md#one-knee-clinch)). After the knockout Vos raises them in celebration ([Vos](../cast/vos.md#after-the-count)). Nobody removes a glove before 180.
