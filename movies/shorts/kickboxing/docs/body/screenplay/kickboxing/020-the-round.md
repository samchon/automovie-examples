# The Round

## INT. KICKBOXING RING - NIGHT | 0–15 s {#p01}

- Championship night. A thin black circle on pale canvas. Red pad near left, south-west. Blue pad far right, north-east. White pads at the other two corners. The aisle outside the north rope runs clear from blue to far white. Overhead light reaches the front row. The rows behind it stay seated and wordless.

- [Owen Vale](../../brief/cast/owen-vale.md#red-corner) in scarlet and [Marek Sokol](../../brief/cast/marek-sokol.md#blue-corner) in royal blue wait on either side of the ink. Both men are bare-chested and barefoot, each 180 cm and 80 kg. [Daniel Mensah](../../brief/cast/daniel-mensah.md#working-official) stands apart on the far rim. Two seconds sit outside each coloured pad. [Alan Price](../../brief/cast/alan-price.md#timekeeper) sits at the bell table at near white. Two neighbours sit just round that corner on the east side.

- Landscape, 1280 × 720. A single view from the south apron, travelling along it and rising above the ropes as the round moves. Both fighters stay whole, with canvas under their feet. We hear the ring and the people close enough to it. The shot runs from the opening bell to black at 180 seconds. [Commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#sound) · [Ringside direction](../../../instructions/ringside-edition.md#point-of-view)

### The extra step {#p01-b01}

At 0, Alan strikes the bell. Daniel drops his hand.

DANIEL

Fight!

Owen steps in and holds a glove out. It stops short of the line. Marek steps onto the ink to tap it. The little sound carries under the bell. The gloves part and Marek's foot stays over the ink. Blue leather rattles against red. Owen's first step away opens a strip of pale canvas between them. Alan sets the striker flat.

- Start: 0 s.
- Turn: Marek stays on the ink after the courtesy and Owen takes the first step away.
- Camera: Hold the touching gloves and the two feet nearest the rim in the same wide.

### One man walking {#p01-b02}

Around 5, a blue glove goes by Owen. Owen's answer makes a blunt sound. Marek pitches away from it and comes down with his foot on the same black curve. There is a beat of quiet before Owen goes round him, three bare footfalls. Marek turns on the spot. The curve is still under him when Owen stops.

- Start: 5 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen lands the reply. Marek keeps the spot and Owen makes the three travelling steps.
- Sound: Give the blunt contact its own space before Owen's footfalls.

### Below the rope {#p01-b03}

Around 11, Marek's low kick sends Owen into one long step. The red pad fills the space behind him. [Jo Bell](../../brief/cast/jo-bell.md#chief-second) ducks under the bulging rope. Beside her [Ed Lane](../../brief/cast/ed-lane.md#assistant-second) catches the empty stool against his shins. He looks up into the back of the pad. Blue is coming on.

- Start: 11 s (approximate).
- Turn: The round reaches red. Jo and Ed lose Owen to the pad.
- Camera: Follow Owen's long step and keep the seconds below the rope.

## CONTINUOUS | 15–30 s {#p02}

### Where he was {#p02-b01}

The west rope creaks against Owen's back at 15. Gloves drum against his guard. Travel left with him as he slips clear. Marek still faces the shaking rope after Owen is out of reach. Further up the west side Owen stands in open light. Marek turns to find him.

- Start: 15 s.
- Turn: Owen gets clear before Marek sees where he went.
- Camera: Travel west with Owen and leave the shaking rope in the wide.

### Two steps to spare {#p02-b02}

Around 22, Owen's right body kick lands with a hollow thump. A breath leaves Marek. His left glove hangs low. Owen takes two steps off the rope while Marek stands still. There is enough light between their bodies to see where Owen's second foot lands. By the pad, Jo sits up.

- Start: 21 s (approximate).
- Turn: The contact around 22 costs Marek a breath and buys Owen two steps.
- Sound: Leave the hanging breath audible at red after the hollow thump.

### The closed way {#p02-b03}

Around 26, Owen turns towards the black curve. A blue glove meets him across the open strip. His breath comes out in a grunt. Marek takes the strip and stands between him and the ink. Owen looks along the west rope. Far white is the one clear way out. He takes it and Marek walks him there.

- Start: 26 s (approximate).
- Turn: Marek closes the way to the middle. Owen takes the west rope towards far white.
- Space: Frame the strip Owen can use and the ink behind Marek.

## CONTINUOUS | 30–45 s {#p03}

### Short of white {#p03-b01}

At 30 the rope runs at an angle behind Owen. Marek checks a kick and moves him half a step. Owen's bare heel squeaks. There is still daylight between it and the white pad. Marek comes up to that last piece of canvas. Their gloves fall quiet. At blue, [Pavel Havel](../../brief/cast/pavel-havel.md#chief-second) leans until one white shoulder shows beyond the north side of the pad. From there he can see Marek's face. Daniel approaches from the middle.

- Start: 30 s.
- Turn: Owen holds his last half-step at white. Pavel finds Marek's face.
- Camera: See the west rope obliquely so the last strip beside the pad stays visible.

### Break {#p03-b02}

Around 36 the fighters come together, shoulder to shoulder. A muffled knee from Marek. He releases Owen and their feet stay close.

DANIEL

Break!

Both men step back. A clear strip opens between them before Daniel walks through it. His shoes give two separate footfalls. [Ivo Kral](../../brief/cast/ivo-kral.md#assistant-second) bends over the towel on his knee. His navy shoulder shows low on the other side of the blue pad. From there Owen is whole against white, down to his bare feet. [Break procedure](../../../research/kickboxing/round-practice.md#rules-and-procedure)

- Start: 36 s (approximate).
- Turn: Both men yield on Daniel's word. Ivo finds Owen from the other edge of blue.
- Sound: Leave a pause for the gap to open and Daniel's two footfalls to fill it.

### Owen leaves first {#p03-b03}

Around 42, Daniel backs away. Ivo watches the opening. Owen steps through it and his glove brushes the blue guard. Marek turns to face him. Ivo's navy shoulder begins to move as Owen's feet leave white. Pavel's high white shoulder stays where it was.

- Start: 42 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen goes first when Daniel clears. Ivo's attention follows the red feet.
- Camera: Hold the high white shoulder and the low navy shoulder apart.

## CONTINUOUS | 45–60 s {#p04}

### Over the rim {#p04-b01}

At 45 Owen is still on his way out from white. A glancing blue punch turns his head. His next foot lands across the rim and he keeps going. A blue glove goes through the space he has left. Marek goes over the ink to stay with him. The view keeps two clear lines of feet against the canvas.

- Start: 45 s.
- Turn: Owen leads over the rim and makes Marek leave his spot to follow.
- Camera: Keep both pairs of feet visible as they cross the mark.

### A lane opens {#p04-b02}

Around 50 Owen's right kick makes a short slap. Marek rocks and grunts. The blue glove hangs low against pale canvas. Owen sets a foot down and looks up the strip towards the north rope. He gets a step along it before Marek moves. Owen takes two quick paces. Marek comes after him a pace back.

- Start: 50 s (approximate).
- Turn: The short reply earns Owen a planted foot and a lead towards the north.
- Sound: The slap ends quickly. The breaths and separate feet carry on.

### The man who stops {#p04-b03}

Around 56 Marek takes two brisk steps north. He looks back at the ink. A third step starts and checks. Marek walks back to the circle. On his own, Owen takes one more pace. The rope takes his weight. Marek's feet are planted inside the rim by the time Owen turns to look. The sound from Owen's last footfall carries across the gap. [Changes of pace](../../../research/kickboxing/competitive-exchanges.md#pace)

- Start: 56 s (approximate).
- Turn: Marek chooses the circle. Owen reaches the rope alone.
- Space: Hold the gap long enough for the separate endpoints to register.

## CONTINUOUS | 60–75 s {#p05}

### Across the gap {#p05-b01}

At 60 Owen edges along the north rope. Marek turns with him from inside the ink. Move towards the two neighbours and settle before Owen leaves the rope. In the near profile [Maya Shah](../../brief/cast/maya-shah.md#ringside-neighbour) has her hands in the lap of her yellow jumper. [Ruth Ellis](../../brief/cast/ruth-ellis.md#ringside-neighbour), in plum, has a clear view of them. When Owen crosses the gap, Maya's shoulders turn with him. The seated rows give a low wordless murmur.

- Start: 60 s.
- Turn: Owen goes to start the fight again. Maya follows him with her shoulders.
- Camera: Settle with two neighbour profiles in front of the whole ring action.

### The clap before the step {#p05-b02}

At 65.50 red glove cracks on blue glove. Maya leans towards Owen and claps at 65.95. At 66.60 she claps again. Ruth sees her hands open and joins the second clap. Beyond them, Marek's feet are still on the ink. He steps forward from the circle and sends Owen back towards the north rope. Maya's hands stay apart. Ruth looks up from them and sees Owen walking backwards.

- Start: 65 s (approximate).
- Turn: Maya's first clap precedes Marek's advance. Ruth sees Maya's hands before she sees Owen give ground.
- Sound: Guard at 65.50. Maya alone at 65.95, both women at 66.60.

### Quiet hands {#p05-b03}

Just before 70, Marek walks a pace past Owen along the north rope. Owen turns in from the rope and faces Marek along it. The blue pad is now at Marek's back. Owen's kick lands. Marek gives one clear stride and then a second. Maya raises her hands with daylight between them. Ruth looks across at her. Owen holds his place and lets Marek steady himself. By 73.5 both of Maya's hands are back in her lap.

- Start: 69.5 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen earns two strides and holds. Maya lets her hands settle without a clap.
- Camera: Hold both women and the fighters until Maya's hands reach her lap.

## CONTINUOUS | 75–90 s {#p06}

### Across the rope {#p06-b01}

At 75 Owen sends Marek the rest of the way to blue. A kick slaps the lowered left arm. Marek's shoulder brushes the pad. He looks over the north rope at Pavel. Around 78 one blue glove comes up alone and turns out towards him. Pavel sits taller and answers with an open hand, his white sleeve clear across the rope. Hold the two raised shapes apart. Marek moves away. Ivo follows him with his eyes from the other side of the pad, towel on his knee.

- Start: 75 s.
- Turn: Marek finds Pavel and gets an answer across the rope.
- Camera: Separate the blue glove and the white sleeve against open backgrounds.

### The stride he gives back {#p06-b02}

Around 82 Owen hurries to draw level along the east side. A glancing blue punch stops him short. Marek has a full stride of canvas to himself. He looks back and sees Owen drawing breath where the shuffle broke off. He walks back into that stride. Near white and the bell stand behind him.

- Start: 81 s (approximate).
- Turn: Marek gets a stride to himself and chooses to give it back.
- Sound: Let Owen's breathing mark the end of the shuffle before Marek returns.

### A yard to breathe {#p06-b03}

Around 86 the men meet close. One knee from Owen and he lets Marek go. Owen takes a step back. A yard of clear canvas opens towards the bell. Daniel lowers his hands from outside the gap. Owen fills it with one ragged breath. Marek turns towards near white before Owen moves again.

- Start: 86 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen ends the close encounter and makes his own space to breathe.
- Space: Keep Daniel outside the open yard and near white behind Marek.

## CONTINUOUS | 90–105 s {#p07}

### Two steps to one {#p07-b01}

At 90 Marek turns the near white pad and boxes along the south side. Owen goes with him on the canvas side. Alan tips his head back as the men fill the view from the table. Marek lands a low kick and Owen rocks out of his stride. Draw back along the apron with both sets of feet in view. Owen hurries to catch up, two short steps to one of Marek's.

- Start: 90 s.
- Turn: Marek checks Owen's stride. Owen takes two short steps to keep up with one.
- Camera: Draw back along the apron before the gloves and feet crowd the view.

### The clap after the step {#p07-b02}

Around 96, Owen puts both feet down and stays. There is a brush of blue leather, then the solid sound of Owen's reply. Maya watches from the east seats, hands in her lap. Marek takes one whole step off his line. Maya follows the foot to where it stops. At 99.35 she gives Owen one clap and lowers her hands again.

- Start: 96 s (approximate).
- Turn: Maya watches Marek finish the step before she applauds Owen.
- Sound: One clap at 99.35, after the reply and the completed step.

### Past the back of the pad {#p07-b03}

Around 101 Marek comes back to face Owen. Owen holds him off for a stride and goes round the red pad. Ed, low enough to see under the rope, follows the red feet first. Jo picks Owen up on the other side of the pad, upright on the west side. Owen's feet slow in the clear strip beyond it.

- Start: 101 s (approximate).
- Turn: Ed sees Owen's feet before Jo finds him past the pad. Owen slows on the west side.
- Camera: Keep the turn and Ed's view under the rope open.

## CONTINUOUS | 105–120 s {#p08}

### Wait at red {#p08-b01}

At 105 Owen stops a pace beyond red. Marek takes two steps towards the circle and looks back. Owen stays. His chest rises for one whole breath in the hush by the pad. The gap remains open for the whole of it. Marek walks back across to him. Owen is still on the same spot.

- Start: 105 s.
- Turn: Owen waits on his own ground until Marek comes back to him.
- Space: Hold the full gap through one complete red breath.

### The sound of the sole {#p08-b02}

Around 110 Owen starts a kick. The blue glove dips. At 111.45 Owen's bare sole slaps the canvas. Jo flinches. Owen's next glove glances home. One blue foot has to come a long way out before it finds the floor. Jo sees it above the rope and Ed sees it below. They start clapping together at 114.25.

- Start: 110 s (approximate).
- Turn: The feint draws the glove dip. The reply after the sole slap makes Marek take a wide step both red seconds can see.
- Sound: Sole at 111.45. Give the blue foot time to come down before the joint clap at 114.25.

### The last clap from red {#p08-b03}

Around 116 Marek steadies himself and sends one glove through Owen's guard. Owen gives the space he was holding. Marek walks him up the west side. Jo lowers her hands. At 118.10 Ed's last clap falls by itself. Then there are two bare blue footfalls and the breath that goes with them.

- Start: 116 s (approximate).
- Turn: Marek takes the west lane back. Jo's hands fall while Ed's last clap is still to come.
- Sound: Ed alone at 118.10. The next blue footfalls and breath lead into 120.

## CONTINUOUS | 120–135 s {#p09}

### Feet stop, breath goes on {#p09-b01}

At 120 the next footfall fails to come. Marek has stopped. His gloves move with a rasp in his chest. Owen's heel is up to go another pace along the rope. He brings it down in the place it left. He looks back. Marek is still there, trying for the end of that breath. Owen steps sideways onto bare canvas. Marek starts after him with a scuff.

- Start: 120 s.
- Turn: Marek runs out of steps. Owen puts his heel back and takes the open way.
- Camera: Hold the suspended and returning heel beside Marek's planted feet and breathing shoulders.

### The passage to white {#p09-b02}

Around 126 Owen takes a long pace up the clear strip towards the north rope. Marek cuts across that line towards far white. Owen plants where he is. Keep their bodies broadside as Marek carries past him to the rope. He has to turn all the way back to face Owen, still on the canvas side.

- Start: 126 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen's approach draws Marek across his path. Owen holds his ground and Marek has to turn back from white.
- Camera: Keep the passing bodies broadside until Marek faces back.

### One foot on the ink {#p09-b03}

Around 132 Owen puts his nearer foot on the black rim. Marek's reply jars him from shoulder to heel. The foot stays on the ink. Marek draws up across the rim. Daniel holds clear on the north side. The two breaths run together over the canvas.

- Start: 132 s (approximate).
- Turn: Marek's reply moves Owen above the waist. Owen's foot keeps its place.
- Space: Daniel holds clear north of the rim. Keep the foot and ink together.

## CONTINUOUS | 135–150 s {#p10}

### Two feet on the line {#p10-b01}

At 135 Marek puts his own foot on the ink. The men face each other with pale canvas between their bodies. Owen turns away a glove. Marek's reply turns Owen sideways. Both feet stay on the rim. Pavel leans out from blue until the white of both sleeves shows. His eyes are on the line.

- Start: 135 s.
- Turn: Marek reclaims his side of the ink. Neither foot leaves through the exchange.
- Camera: Show pale canvas between the bodies and Pavel's view of the rim.

### The unoccupied circle {#p10-b02}

Around 141 Owen's foot comes off the ink. Marek looks at the ink, then at Owen. His foot leaves the rim and a blue glove gets home. Pavel claps once. Owen's steps grow short and loud. The empty circle comes into view behind both men before Owen reaches the north rope. Travel east along the apron and rise above the rope, looking into the north-west quarter.

- Start: 141 s (approximate).
- Turn: Marek leaves the ink to stay with Owen. The middle empties.
- Camera: Hold the empty rim in the picture as the view travels east and rises.

### The dip {#p10-b03}

Around 147 the view settles with both men whole inside far white. Owen stops travelling and turns to face Marek with pale canvas between their feet. Far white stands just beyond Marek. Just before 150, the red kick rises and the blue glove dips as it did at red. In the space where the sole slapped the floor there, we hear Marek's breath. Stay on the same diagonal through the fall, the count and the ending. [Camera and allegiance](../../../research/kickboxing/competitive-exchanges.md#camera)

- Start: 147 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen plants. The glove dip returns as Marek's breath fills the space left by the expected sole slap.
- Camera: Settle with two whole fighters and far white on the diagonal.

## CONTINUOUS | 150–165 s {#p11}

### The step that fails {#p11-b01}

At 150.000 Owen's right kick lands. A flat crack. Marek makes one step into the open canvas beside him. His leg gives and his right thigh touches down at 150.650, inside the north rope just short of far white. Daniel points down on the touch. Owen is already backing clear.

DANIEL

Down!

Marek's right hip follows his thigh. His shoulder settles last. The view holds his whole descent, with Owen clearly apart and bare canvas on both sides of him.

- Start: 150.000 s.
- Turn: The red contact puts Marek down on his right side. Daniel signals at the first touch.
- Camera: Hold the failed step, the thigh touch at 150.650 and Daniel beside the descent.

### All the way to white {#p11-b02}

Around 151.5 Marek lies on his right side. His face is towards the north rope, the crown of his head towards blue. Daniel points across the ring to the white pad beside the bell.

DANIEL

Neutral corner!

The view widens on the same diagonal as Owen trots through the ink. He reaches the pad, turns to face centre and lowers both gloves. Daniel sees him stand still. He comes round the crown of Marek's head to stop on its blue side. From there Marek can see Daniel's hand with the north aisle clear ahead of his face. All four seconds are seated. The striker lies flat on Alan's table. [Count procedure](../../../research/kickboxing/round-practice.md#rules-and-procedure)

- Start: 151.5 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen reaches neutral white and faces centre. Daniel takes his place by Marek's head.
- Camera: Widen continuously during Owen's crossing. Keep both white pads and the open north aisle.

### The count {#p11-b03}

At 157 Daniel crouches. His counting glove rises in clear space beside Marek's face. The murmur dies with the rising hand. Owen stays by near white, the two red gloves low. Each stroke of the hand has the same interval.

At 158 the first stroke falls.

DANIEL

One!

At 159 the hand falls again. Marek's free glove is still stretched on the canvas.

DANIEL

Two!

At 160 Marek draws the glove in.

DANIEL

Three!

At 161 Marek's head lifts and the near shoulder clears the floor. The glove bears his weight. Maya leans forward from her seat, her hands still in her lap.

DANIEL

Four!

At 162 the shoulder holds in the air. His hip stays down.

DANIEL

Five!

At 163 his free elbow gives a little. Owen stays against the pad.

DANIEL

Six!

At 164 Marek's shoulder comes down. Daniel stays low within his view. The raised hand keeps its place in the light.

DANIEL

Seven!

- Start: 157 s.
- Turn: Marek lifts through the middle of the count and comes back down before Seven ends.
- Sound: One through Seven at 158–164. Leave each interval to the ring.

## CONTINUOUS | 165–180 s {#p12}

### The signal {#p12-b01}

At 165 Marek presses the free glove down again. Daniel looks along him from shoulder to hip. The hip stays flat. Daniel's hand keeps time.

DANIEL

Eight!

At 166 another stroke. Owen waits, chin tipped to see past the rope.

DANIEL

Nine!

At 167 Marek's forearm settles along the canvas.

DANIEL

Ten!

At 167.600 Daniel rises and sweeps his arms high across and apart in Alan's view above the rope. His face stays towards Marek. Knockout. Daniel's call carries across the canvas.

DANIEL

Owen!

Alan picks up the striker after the signal. The four seconds are still seated.

- Start: 165 s.
- Turn: Marek stays down through Ten. Alan sees the result and takes up the striker.
- Camera: Hold Alan's table and Daniel's sweep at 167.600 in the same diagonal.

### The bell and two stamps {#p12-b02}

At 168.100 both of Owen's gloves go high at white. His breath bursts out. Daniel gives him room to leave with a small beckon and settles on one knee near Marek's head. Owen stays at the pad with both gloves up until the bell.

At 170.100 Alan strikes the match-ending bell. Jo and Ed rise at red. Pavel rises at blue and sets out along the north aisle. Ivo gathers the towel on his lap and stays seated.

Owen gives a laughing breath and stamps once towards red. He turns both raised gloves towards Jo and Ed, with clear air between them. They lift their arms to him. His second stamp lands. All three hold the salute. Owen stands clear of the line between the neighbours and Marek.

When the stamp has died, Marek makes a small move to sit. Daniel steadies him.

DANIEL

Stay there, Marek.

Marek rests. Ruth hears his name and turns across the ring towards him. Her hands stay on the plum of her lap. For a beat Maya's yellow shoulders still face Owen.

- Start: 168.100 s.
- Turn: Owen has both gloves high. The bell moves the seconds. Daniel stays with Marek and Ruth turns towards him.
- Sound: Bell at 170.100. Separate the stamps from Daniel's words.

### Two claps across the ring {#p12-b03}

Around 173.5 Owen brings his salute round to Maya, both gloves still high. She has him in front of her. She turns her shoulders past him towards the man on the canvas. Owen follows her turn on a planted foot. His gloves stay high. His face comes round to Marek. Owen and both women come to rest across the open diagonal.

Pavel crouches outside the north rope opposite Marek. Daniel stays beside Marek's head. A blue glove rises alone against pale canvas and turns out towards Pavel. Pavel straightens and lifts an open hand, the white sleeve visible from shoulder to cuff. Let the glove and the open hand hold across the rope. Marek stays down with his face turned towards Pavel.

At 178.500 Maya gives Marek one clap from her seat. Owen keeps looking at Marek. Maya's hands settle. Ruth looks straight across the canvas at Marek with her hands still in her lap. At 179.500 Ruth lifts them for one clap of her own.

Hold the same wide. Two red gloves at the near edge. One blue glove held towards the white sleeve. Both women in profile facing the man on the canvas.

- Start: 173.5 s (approximate).
- Turn: Maya turns past Owen and Owen follows. Pavel answers Marek. Both neighbours clap to Marek from their seats.
- Camera: Give the glove and the open hand time in the wide before the claps at 178.500 and 179.500.

At 180, CUT TO BLACK.
