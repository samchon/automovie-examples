# The round

## INT. KICKBOXING RING - NIGHT | 0-15 s {#p01}

- A thin black circle on pale canvas. Red pad near left, south-west. Blue pad far right, north-east. White pads at the other two corners. Overhead light reaches the front row. Beyond it, ranks of seated heads.
- Championship night. [Owen Vale](../../brief/cast/owen-vale.md#red-corner) in scarlet and [Marek Sokol](../../brief/cast/marek-sokol.md#blue-corner) in royal blue stand either side of the ink. Both men are bare-chested and barefoot, each 180 cm and 80 kg. [Daniel Mensah](../../brief/cast/daniel-mensah.md#working-official) stands on the far rim, midway between them.
- Two seconds sit outside each coloured corner. The north aisle from blue to far white is clear. A bell table stands at near white. Just round that corner two neighbours sit on the east side, looking across the ring.
- Landscape, 1280 × 720 (16:9). From the south apron we see through a clear interval between ropes. The view can travel along this side and rise above the ropes. Keep both fighters whole, with canvas visible under their feet. One clock from the opening bell to black.
- We hear the ring, Daniel and the people within earshot. The crowd stays seated and wordless. [Commission](../../../instructions/commission.md#sound) · [Ringside direction](../../../instructions/ringside-edition.md#point-of-view)

### The extra step {#p01-b01}

At 0, [Alan Price](../../brief/cast/alan-price.md#timekeeper) strikes the bell. Daniel drops his hand.

DANIEL

Fight!

Owen steps in with a red glove held out. Marek steps in too. The red glove stops just shy of the black line. Marek takes the extra step onto the ink to touch it. A little tap underneath the bell. The gloves part and the first blows rattle against red leather. Owen has to move. Marek stays where the tap took place. At the edge of the wide Alan lays the striker flat.

- Start: 0 s.
- Turn: Marek accepts the courtesy. His foot covers the ink when their gloves part.
- View: hold wide through the tap and the first travelling red foot.

### A place to stand {#p01-b02}

Blue leather goes past Owen. His reply lands with a thud. Marek lurches and comes down on the line again. Owen goes round him. One step, another, a third. Marek turns on the spot to face him.

- Start: 5 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen scores the first clear blow and still has to do the walking.
- Sound: give the thud its own space before the three bare footfalls.

### The red seconds duck {#p01-b03}

A low kick from Marek. Owen's next step is long and loud. The red pad fills the space at his back. Below it [Jo Bell](../../brief/cast/jo-bell.md#chief-second) ducks the bulging rope. Beside her [Ed Lane](../../brief/cast/ed-lane.md#assistant-second) catches their empty stool against his shins. He looks up into the back of the pad. Blue is coming on.

- Start: 11 s (approximate).
- Turn: Marek sends Owen back to the two people with the worst view of him.
- Space: follow the long step towards near left. Keep the seconds below the rope.

## CONTINUOUS | 15-30 s {#p02}

### The rope speaks {#p02-b01}

The west rope creaks against Owen's back. A drumming of blue on red. Owen comes clear of the pad. For an instant Marek still faces the place where the rope is shaking. Further up the west side Owen stands in open light.

- Start: 15 s.
- Turn: Owen gets free long enough for Marek to have to find him again.
- View: travel left. Leave the shaking length of rope in the wide.

### An answer along the ropes {#p02-b02}

Owen's right body kick lands with a hollow thump. An audible breath leaves Marek. His left glove hangs low after the noise. For a moment he stands quite still. Owen comes off the rope. Jo sits up as daylight opens beside the pad.

- Start: 21 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen's answer buys him a strip of bare canvas.
- Sound: the breath carries back to red in the sudden quiet from Marek's gloves.

### Company {#p02-b03}

Owen sets off across the strip. A blue glove meets him there. His long breath comes out as a grunt. He gives ground until the rope bows. This time he takes the length of it towards far white. Marek goes with him, between Owen and the circle.

- Start: 26 s (approximate).
- Turn: Marek closes the way back to the mark.
- Space: show the ink over Marek's shoulder and the rope running out ahead of Owen.

## CONTINUOUS | 30-45 s {#p03}

### Half a step of daylight {#p03-b01}

Marek checks a kick. His reply moves Owen half a step. A bare heel squeaks short of the white pad. Marek takes the half-step. Their gloves patter to a stop. All four feet are quiet. At blue [Pavel Havel](../../brief/cast/pavel-havel.md#chief-second) leans out from his seat, one white shoulder showing past the north edge of the pad. From there he has Marek's face in view. He holds the lean.

- Start: 30 s.
- Turn: Owen holds the last little piece of canvas at white.
- View: take the west rope at a slight angle. Daniel approaches from the middle.

### Break {#p03-b02}

They come together. One muffled knee from Marek. He releases Owen at once. They're still shoulder to shoulder by the rope.

DANIEL

Break!

Both men step back. Daniel comes between them. For two footfalls the loudest shoes in the ring are his. In the quiet after the second, [Ivo Kral](../../brief/cast/ivo-kral.md#assistant-second) bends low over the towel on his knee. His navy shoulder shows past the other edge of the blue pad. From there the man in scarlet stands clear against the white pad, right down to his bare feet. Daniel's black shoes are a pace out on the canvas.

- Start: 36 s (approximate).
- Turn: at Daniel's word both men give up space.
- Space: let us see the gap before Daniel occupies it. [Break procedure](../../../research/kickboxing/round-practice.md#rules-and-procedure)

### Through the gap {#p03-b03}

Daniel backs clear. Owen comes straight out through the opening, his punch skimming the blue guard. Marek wheels round.

- Start: 42 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen claims the gap as soon as Daniel leaves it.
- View: keep Pavel's white shoulder high and Ivo's navy shoulder low on either side of the blue pad. Ivo holds still as the black shoes move. When the bare feet under the scarlet shorts leave white, his navy shoulder turns to follow them.

## CONTINUOUS | 45-60 s {#p04}

### Out in the light {#p04-b01}

Just north of the circle a glancing blue punch turns Owen's head. His feet carry him on over the rim. The next blue glove goes through air. To stay with him Marek has to cross the mark himself.

- Start: 45 s.
- Turn: Owen's travelling feet take Marek off his spot.
- Sound: a padded knock, then the scuff of both men changing direction.

### A shorter noise {#p04-b02}

Another right kick from Owen. Marek's left glove is already low when it arrives. A slap, much shorter than that first thump. Marek rocks, grunts and comes on again. At red Jo barely has time to straighten up.

- Start: 50 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen gets one foot down before Marek is back with him.
- View: keep both men in full, the blue glove showing against pale canvas.

### The empty middle {#p04-b03}

Owen gives ground towards the north rope. Marek takes two brisk steps after him. He looks back at the ink, checks his third step and walks back on to it. Owen takes another pace alone. His back meets the rope. Across the widening gap Marek stands waiting for him.

- Start: 56 s (approximate).
- Turn: Marek chooses the middle and leaves Owen at the boundary.
- Sound: one red foot peeling off the mat in the space where a chase was going on. [Changes of pace](../../../research/kickboxing/competitive-exchanges.md#pace)

## CONTINUOUS | 60-75 s {#p05}

### An invitation {#p05-b01}

Owen edges along the north rope. Marek turns with him, both feet inside the ink. Owen stops. Then he comes towards Marek. A low, wordless murmur swells through the seated rows at the sight of him coming in. In the front row [Maya Shah](../../brief/cast/maya-shah.md#ringside-neighbour) turns in her seat to follow the arriving red shorts. Beside her [Ruth Ellis](../../brief/cast/ruth-ellis.md#ringside-neighbour) has a view of Maya's hands against the yellow jumper.

- Start: 60 s.
- Turn: Owen has to cross the gap to start the fight again.
- View: ease right and settle before he leaves the rope. Both women's upper bodies sit in profile at the near edge, with the fighters and the circle beyond.

### The wrong cheer {#p05-b02}

Owen comes in fast. Glove cracks on glove. Maya leans towards the red shorts and claps out in front of her yellow chest. Ruth catches the opening of her hands and joins her on the second clap. Out on the ink Marek is walking Owen backwards. Maya's hands stay apart. Ruth looks up in time to see who has the ground.

- Start: 65 s (approximate).
- Turn: Maya gives Owen the sound of a clean hit. Marek sends him back from the mark.
- View: keep the meeting gloves, Maya's turned torso and Ruth looking sideways in the same wide.

### Hands in a lap {#p05-b03}

At the north rope Marek goes a pace past Owen. They face each other along it, blue now at Marek's back. Owen's kick lands. Marek gives two steps towards his pad. Maya raises her hands. Owen lets him settle. Ruth looks across for the clap. Maya puts her hands in her lap.

- Start: 69.5 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen makes ground in front of the same two seats. This time neither woman claps.
- View: stay for the hands coming to rest before Owen moves again.

## CONTINUOUS | 75-90 s {#p06}

### At his own corner {#p06-b01}

Owen sends Marek the rest of the way to blue. A kick slaps Marek's lowered left arm. His shoulder brushes the blue pad. Right there, outside the rope, is Pavel. Marek turns towards him and lifts one blue glove clear above his shoulder, out towards Pavel. The glove turns out at the top. Pavel sits up tall and raises one open hand to meet it across the rope, the whole white sleeve showing. Marek is away before it comes down. On the other side of the pad Ivo sees him go.

- Start: 75 s.
- Turn: pressed back to his own corner, Marek still finds a moment for Pavel.
- Space: set the solitary blue glove inside the north rope against the open white-sleeved hand outside it.

### Off the pad {#p06-b02}

Along the east side Owen hurries to draw level with him. A glancing blue punch brings Owen up short. Marek gets a whole stride to himself. He looks round and comes back for Owen, who is still drawing breath. Near white and the bell are behind Marek.

- Start: 81 s (approximate).
- Turn: Marek chooses to give back the stride he has won.
- Sound: the shuffle breaks in two. Owen's quick breaths come from where it broke off.

### An inch to spare {#p06-b03}

The two meet close. One immediate knee from Owen and he releases Marek. A yard of canvas opens between them towards the bell. Daniel lowers his outstretched hands. Owen takes his time over a ragged breath before he follows.

- Start: 86 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen makes his own separation and gets a breath out of it.
- Space: Daniel leaves the open yard to the fighters. The near white pad stands behind Marek.

## CONTINUOUS | 90-105 s {#p07}

### Past the table {#p07-b01}

Marek rounds near white, boxing again. Owen goes with him, on the canvas side. Alan tips his head back as the men fill the view from the table. A low kick lands. Down the south side it now takes Owen two little steps to keep up with one of Marek's.

- Start: 90 s.
- Turn: Marek sets a pace that costs Owen twice the footfalls.
- View: draw back from the approaching pair. Leave room below the gloves for the shortening steps.

### Two different sounds {#p07-b02}

Owen stands his ground. A brush of blue leather. A flat, solid sound from Owen's reply. Maya's hands stay in her lap. She watches the feet. Marek has to step off his line. Only then does she lift her hands towards Owen and give him one clap.

- Start: 96 s (approximate).
- Turn: Maya waits to see which man has moved.
- Sound: leave a footfall's distance between the contact and the clap.

### Back to red {#p07-b03}

Marek comes back to face him. Owen holds him off for a stride, then gives. The red pad slides between Jo and the men. Ed, lower down, sees Owen's feet go by under the bottom rope. He turns first. Jo picks Owen up on the other side of the pad, still on his feet.

- Start: 101 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen carries his retreat past the place where the red corner first lost sight of him.
- Space: from the south apron keep the inside of the turn onto the west side clear.

## CONTINUOUS | 105-120 s {#p08}

### Whose turn to cross {#p08-b01}

A pace past red, Owen stops. Marek takes two inviting steps towards the ink and waits. Owen stays where he is. In the hush by his own pad he draws one whole breath. At last Marek walks back across the gap. Owen is there to meet him.

- Start: 105 s.
- Turn: Owen leaves Marek to do the crossing this time.
- View: keep the gap open in front of the seated red seconds for the length of the breath.

### The kick that never comes {#p08-b02}

The start of a red kick. The familiar dip of blue leather. A bare sole slaps the floor. Jo flinches at the little noise. Owen gets a glancing punch home and Marek has to take a wide step. From their two different heights Jo and Ed both see that. They clap together.

- Start: 110 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen's feint earns the red corner a clear sight of Marek giving ground.
- Sound: let the sole striking the mat be heard distinctly at red.

### Blue answers red {#p08-b03}

Marek steadies himself. Red gloves keep coming. He meets them, gets a punch through and walks Owen away from the clapping. Jo drops her hands first. Ed's last clap falls by itself. Up the west side the feet go on without them.

- Start: 116 s (approximate).
- Turn: Marek takes Owen away from his corner's encouragement.
- Space: let red recede while Daniel keeps clear of the stretch ahead.

## CONTINUOUS | 120-135 s {#p09}

### The man making the noise {#p09-b01}

A rasp with every step. Then a rasp without a step. Marek has stopped. His gloves rise and fall together. Owen has a heel up for another pace along the rope. He sets it down where it was. He looks back. Marek is still there, trying for the end of that breath. Owen steps sideways onto bare canvas. Only then does Marek come after him with a great scuff.

- Start: 120 s.
- Turn: the pursuit stops before Marek wants it to. Owen takes the space while Marek still needs the breath.
- View: keep the still blue feet, the moving gloves and Owen's abandoned step legible together.

### Let him pass {#p09-b02}

Short of far white, Marek hurries to get across Owen's path. Owen waits where he is. Blue goes past; for a moment the rope belongs to Marek. He has to turn all the way back. Owen is facing him with open canvas at his back.

- Start: 126 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen can afford to wait and let Marek spend the stride.
- View: give the men room to exchange places in one view. Pick them up broadside as Marek turns.

### The price of the middle {#p09-b03}

Owen puts a foot on the black rim. Marek's reply jars him from heel to heel. The foot stays on the ink. Marek draws up opposite. Both men are breathing hard enough for the two sounds to run together.

- Start: 132 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen holds one foot's worth of the ground Marek took at the bell.
- Space: Daniel gives them room on the north side. Keep the narrow mark visible under Owen.

## CONTINUOUS | 135-150 s {#p10}

### Half the circle each {#p10-b01}

Marek steps onto the ink too. Red turns a blue glove aside. The blue reply turns Owen sideways. For all the noise above the mark there are still two pairs of feet on it. At blue Pavel leans out until both white sleeves hang clear of the pad.

- Start: 135 s.
- Turn: both men keep a piece of the middle through the exchange.
- View: give Pavel the same view we have of the contested line.

### A step too many {#p10-b02}

At last the rim slips out from under Owen. Marek follows and gets a glove home. One clap out of the white sleeves. Owen keeps going. His steps grow short and noisy. By the time his scarlet shorts brush the north rope, the circle lies well behind both men.

- Start: 141 s (approximate).
- Turn: Marek drives Owen from the middle, then leaves it to stay with him.
- View: travel a little east along the south apron and rise above the rope, looking diagonally into the north-west quarter.

### Recognition {#p10-b03}

Their feet scuff past each other along the north rope. Far white stands a pace beyond Marek. Owen is on the canvas side. The kick flashes up. There it is again, the dip of the blue glove. In the space where the sole slapped the floor at red we hear Marek's breath.

- Start: 147 s (approximate).
- Turn: the familiar movement draws the room quiet for the missing sound.
- View: settle with both men in full inside far white. Continue without a cut through the kick, the fall, the count and the ending. [Camera and allegiance](../../../research/kickboxing/competitive-exchanges.md#camera)

## CONTINUOUS | 150-165 s {#p11}

### The foot that finds nothing {#p11-b01}

At **150.000**, Owen's right Brazilian kick lands. A flat crack. Marek takes a step as if he still has somewhere to go. It won't carry him. His right thigh touches canvas inside the north rope, just short of white. Daniel points down on the touch. Owen is already backing clear.

DANIEL

Down!

Marek's right hip follows his thigh. His shoulder settles last. Owen has clear canvas behind him all the way back to the ink.

- Start: 150.000 s.
- Turn: Daniel calls the down at the first touch of canvas.
- View: hold contact, Marek's whole descent onto his right side and Owen clearing him in the continuous shot.

### All the way to white {#p11-b02}

Marek lies on his right side just inside far white. His face is towards the north rope, the crown of his head towards the blue end of it. Daniel points across the ring to the white pad beside the bell.

DANIEL

Neutral corner!

Owen trots through the circle to the furthest white pad. He turns to face centre and stays there. Daniel sees him in place. He comes round the crown of Marek's head and stops on the blue-corner side of his face, close to the north rope. From there he can put a hand in view without coming between Marek and the aisle straight ahead. The four seconds sit outside. The striker lies flat on Alan's table.

- Start: 151.5 s (approximate).
- Turn: Owen completes the crossing and faces centre before the count begins.
- View: widen in the same shot until both white pads fit. Hold the diagonal. [Count procedure](../../../research/kickboxing/round-practice.md#rules-and-procedure)

### What the room waits for {#p11-b03}

At **157**, Daniel crouches and holds his black glove ahead of Marek's face, a little towards blue. The sound of the seated rows dies away as the hand goes up. The first stroke falls at **158**.

DANIEL

One!

At **159**, another stroke, the same interval.

DANIEL

Two!

At **160**, Marek draws his free glove in from the canvas.

DANIEL

Three!

At **161**, his head and shoulders come up. Maya draws breath.

DANIEL

Four!

At **162**, his hip is still flat on the mat.

DANIEL

Five!

At **163**, Owen presses back against near white, his gloves down.

DANIEL

Six!

At **164**, Marek's shoulder comes down. Daniel stays low. The counting hand goes up again in front of Marek.

DANIEL

Seven!

- Start: 157 s.
- Turn: Marek's effort brings Maya forward in her seat. Owen waits through it at white.
- Sound: leave the space after each number to the ring. Nobody counts along.

## CONTINUOUS | 165-180 s {#p12}

### The hand goes across {#p12-b01}

At **165**, Marek presses his free glove into the mat. On the eighth stroke Daniel leans close, watching to see whether Marek can get up and resume. His hip stays down. Daniel's hand keeps time.

DANIEL

Eight!

At **166**, another stroke. Owen waits at white.

DANIEL

Nine!

At **167**, Marek lets his forearm rest along the canvas.

DANIEL

Ten!

At **167.6**, Daniel straightens from the crouch and sweeps his arms high across and apart, clear of the ropes in Alan's view from near white. Knockout. His face stays turned down towards Marek. His call carries to Owen.

DANIEL

Owen!

Alan sees the sweep and takes up the striker. All four seconds are still seated.

- Start: 165 s.
- Turn: Alan gets the result from Daniel's arms. Owen hears his name at white.
- View: keep the sweep above the rope and Alan's hand at the table below it.

### Who the bell is for {#p12-b02}

At **168.1**, both red gloves go high at white. A breath bursts out of Owen. Daniel beckons him clear of the neutral pad with one hand and settles on one knee beside Marek. Owen stays there a beat, breathing under his raised gloves.

At **170.1**, Alan strikes the match-ending bell. Jo and Ed rise outside red. Pavel rises on the same bell and sets off along the outside of the north rope. Ivo gathers the towel and stays at blue.

Owen gives a great laughing breath. On his first stamping step along the near rope he turns both raised gloves towards Jo and Ed. They lift their arms to him in answer. The second stamp lands. All three hold the salute. Across the open diagonal, Marek tries to sit up. Daniel steadies him.

DANIEL

Stay there, Marek.

Marek rests again. At the sound of his name Ruth leans forward in her seat and turns squarely towards him. Owen holds his salute at red. For a beat the plum cardigan faces the man on the floor while the yellow jumper still faces the man by the table.

- Start: 168.1 s.
- Turn: Owen gets the first two seconds of victory at white. The bell sets the corners in motion.
- Space: hold the wide. Owen stands at the near edge, to one side of the clear diagonal from the neighbours' seats to Marek.

### The answer from the floor {#p12-b03}

Owen turns from his corner, both gloves still high, to take his salute to the yellow jumper. He stands there beaming at her, his chest heaving. Maya turns in her seat. Her yellow shoulders come round past him towards the man on the floor. Owen turns with her, all the way round on his planted foot until he too faces Marek. His gloves stay high.

Pavel reaches the place opposite Marek and crouches outside the north rope, a hand on either knee. Daniel stays beside Marek's head. One blue glove lifts clear of the body on the floor, high and out towards Pavel, with that little turn at the top. Across the rope Pavel straightens his broad back and raises one open hand, the white sleeve showing from shoulder to cuff.

At **178.5**, as the white sleeve reaches its full height, Maya gives the blue glove one clap. Her hands come down. A beat of quiet. At **179.5**, facing Marek across the clear canvas, Ruth claps once. The sound dies away beside the bell table. Owen still holds both gloves high. Marek's glove stays up towards Pavel.

- Start: 173.5 s (approximate).
- Turn: Marek gives Pavel their salute from the floor. Ruth chooses the man she claps for.
- View: one wide to black. Keep Owen in full at the near edge, the rising blue glove against canvas and both women's turned bodies clear in profile.

At **180**, CUT TO BLACK.
