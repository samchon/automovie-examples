# The round

## INT. KICKBOXING RING - NIGHT | 0-15 s {#p01}

- Place and light: a square, pale canvas under clean overhead arena lights. Red corner southwest, blue northeast; the northwest and southeast corners are neutral. The ropes leave every fighter's head and feet visible from the working view. A dark seated crowd surrounds the bright ring. This is the [commissioned championship night](../../../instructions/commission.md#venue).
- Cast and opening positions: [Owen Vale](../../brief/cast/owen-vale.md#red-corner), red, stands just west of centre; [Marek Sokol](../../brief/cast/marek-sokol.md#blue-corner), blue, just east. Both are the [commissioned adult men](../../../instructions/commission.md#fighters), 180 cm and 80 kg, equally trained at championship level. Their left feet lead, gloves beside their heads. [Daniel Mensah](../../brief/cast/daniel-mensah.md#referee) stands north of the gap, able to see both heads in profile, the [active official](../../../instructions/commission.md#referee) throughout. Their [contrasting styles](../../../instructions/commission.md#people) follow Owen's [patient kicking](../../brief/cast/owen-vale.md#patient-kicker) and Marek's [pressure fighting](../../brief/cast/marek-sokol.md#pressure-fighter).
- Others present: two corner attendants sit outside each coloured corner in matching tracksuits. They lean forward with the action but remain outside the ropes throughout. A timekeeper in a grey jacket sits at the southeast ringside table, bell and striker within reach; his head turns towards Daniel whenever the referee signals. Crowd heads turn with the moving pair; their collective response is wordless.
- Camera: landscape [16:9 at 1280 by 720](../../../instructions/commission.md#frame). An eye-height full view from the south [relates both fighters](../../../instructions/commission.md#camera), Owen on the left and Marek on the right, with canvas below both feet and room over both heads. It includes Daniel beyond their gap. During exchanges, framing always contains both heads, the struck target and both fighters' feet. Subsequent tracking and shallow orbits show every rotation of their line; headings alone never cut the view.
- Sound: the seated crowd's low rustle lies beneath breathing, soles and cloth. Glove, body and shin contacts have distinct sounds; misses have movement and breath only. Under the [British English and speech ruling](../../../instructions/commission.md#language), the referee is the sole speaking voice. [Bells and English commands](../../../instructions/commission.md#sound) are live ring sounds; no music, commentary or replay accompanies them.
- Time and performance: one unbroken chronology, [0-180 seconds](../../../instructions/commission.md#one-round), in [twelve consecutive phases](../../../instructions/commission.md#phases). Phase boundaries and the commission's named finish/count/result/bell instants are true anchors. Other beat starts and action durations are provisional construction timing. The [practice study](../../../research/kickboxing/round-practice.md#level-and-density) supplies the activity basis; its [outcome classes](../../../research/kickboxing/round-practice.md#outcomes) apply to the exchanges. Between named attacks the men remain engaged through the specifically written recovery and footwork. Neither has an injury or an acquired defensive tell at entry.
- Sporting conditions: both men perform the [commissioned exchange vocabulary](../../../instructions/commission.md#exchange-vocabulary) under the [GLORY rules ruling](../../../instructions/commission.md#ruleset). The pre-finish [contact costs](../../../instructions/commission.md#contacts-before-finish) are breath, guard and supported balance changes. Their [changing pressure and initiative](../../../instructions/commission.md#progression) carry them through the [whole ring](../../../instructions/commission.md#whole-ring), rather than resetting at a heading.

### Touch into the first counter {#p01-b01}

At 0, the timekeeper strikes the opening bell. Daniel drops his extended hand out of the lane.

DANIEL

Fight!

Both men step forward. Their lead gloves meet with a padded tap. Marek draws his left glove back and immediately jabs Owen's right glove, drives a right straight against the closed guard and swings his right shin towards the outside of Owen's lead thigh. There is no retreat to starting positions after the touch.

Owen raises his left knee and turns the shin out. The kick cracks against it. As Marek draws the leg home, Owen puts his left foot down and sends a right straight against Marek's returning left glove. Marek's head rocks behind the glove; his right foot lands under him. Daniel steps north-east around the pair rather than backing away from the kick.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel; the timekeeper begins the round.
- Intent: Marek turns courtesy into pressure; Owen checks and answers; Daniel clears and observes the first contact.
- Outcome and reaction: the [real glove touch](../../../instructions/commission.md#glove-touch) becomes immediate exchange; the checked low kick earns Owen a guarded counter. Both recover to their left-leading bases.
- Start: 0, true opening anchor; the exchange plays to about 5 s.
- Sound: the bell's decay runs under the rapid padded taps and the sharper shin collision.

### The open body line {#p01-b02}

Marek lets Owen's next jab pass outside his head with a small bend of knees and rightward slip. He plants the right foot, puts a left hook into Owen's right forearm, then a right straight between the gloves that brushes the left side of Owen's head. Owen turns with that glancing contact and closes the gap in his guard.

Marek dips towards the body. Owen brings both elbows in. The dip becomes a jab against the top of Owen's closing right glove, a right to the front of the ribs and a short left hook against the guard. Owen's exhalation catches on the body shot. He steps back-left with the right glove still at his cheek; the red corner comes behind his left shoulder. Daniel advances on the north side, head turning from the high contact to Owen's body.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Marek uses head movement and a body feint to split Owen's defence; Owen keeps his feet beneath the retreat; Daniel watches the change of level.
- Outcome and reaction: Marek's five-punch attack costs Owen breath and ground, while the other blows miss or meet the specified guard.
- Start: about 5 s, provisional, continuing to 10 s.
- Space: centre to the southwest quarter, short of the red corner.
- Camera: follow the retreat left, retaining Daniel above the space between the fighters.

### An exit closed {#p01-b03}

Owen bats Marek's probing left jab aside with his right glove and puts a jab of his own on the blue gloves. A right straight follows; Marek joins his forearms and takes it there. Owen shifts his lead foot towards the west-side exit.

Marek steps across that route before Owen can carry his rear foot through. His left hook swishes past as Owen bends his knees and rolls beneath it. Owen rises facing him, but Marek's following right round kick lands outside Owen's left thigh before Owen can lift the leg again. Owen's weight rocks onto the rear foot. He replaces his lead foot without crossing it and closes his elbows as Marek comes on. Daniel moves towards the west half of the ring, leaving the red pad clear behind Owen.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Owen seeks the side exit; Marek cuts it off and attacks the supporting leg; Daniel keeps a view past Owen's near shoulder.
- Outcome and reaction: the parry and roll work, but the low kick prevents a clean escape. Owen ends with the red pad over his left shoulder and little canvas to give.
- Start: about 10 s, provisional; the new position carries through the 15 s phase boundary.
- Space: the red-corner approach, Owen inside the ropes rather than supported by them.
- Sound: a clean thigh thud, Owen's forced breath and a rear sole scraping to settle his weight.

## CONTINUOUS | 15-30 s {#p02}

### Around the red pad {#p02-b01}

Marek steps in behind two jabs, the first striking Owen's right glove, the second skimming over his dipping head. He turns a right straight towards the body. Owen sets his left elbow across the front of his ribs; the punch knocks elbow into torso.

Owen answers with a short left hook against Marek's right glove and uses the recoil to pivot on his lead foot. His rear foot sweeps round on the canvas, clear of Marek's feet. Marek's left hook passes behind the turning shoulder. Owen sidesteps north along the west ropes, away from the red pad; Marek turns on his planted lead foot and follows on a diagonal. Daniel circles behind Marek, watching the newly open lane.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Marek pins Owen at the corner; Owen blocks the body and makes a turn instead of another straight retreat; Daniel follows the exit.
- Outcome and reaction: Owen escapes the pad but remains beside the west ropes; Marek retains striking distance without stumbling through the pivot.
- Start: 15 s phase anchor; internal timing provisional, to about 20 s.
- Space: red corner to the lower west ropes.
- Camera: truck north along the west ropes, then shallow-orbit with their visible turn to keep the two torsos separate in the full view.

### The first right body kick {#p02-b02}

Owen stops giving ground. His jab taps Marek's right glove upwards; his left hook taps it again as his lead foot takes a small step out. Marek loads a right straight. Owen has already put his weight on the left leg.

His folded right knee comes up in front and turns across. The left forefoot pivots; the lower right shin swings into Marek's left ribs. Marek's left glove is still beside his cheek. His elbow descends only as the kick arrives, too late to close the body line. The punch he was loading never leaves his shoulder.

Marek blows out a rough, short breath and brings his left elbow over the struck side. Owen draws his right knee back and plants the foot behind his left. Marek's answering jab strikes Owen's right glove as Owen moves off the rope line. Daniel shifts sideways to see the body contact without stepping into the recovery.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Owen earns a right body kick with his hands; Marek intends to punch over them; Daniel seeks the side of the contact.
- Outcome and reaction: the kick interrupts Marek's launch and breath. His left arm moves down after the impact; no anticipatory drop has yet been shown.
- Start: about 20 s, provisional, to 26 s.
- Space: west side, with room between Owen's back and the ropes after his kicking step.
- Sound: the first low body-kick thud stands out from the preceding padded hand contacts.

### The answer to the ribs {#p02-b03}

Marek makes Owen bring both hands back up with a left-right against the guard. A left hook follows at body height; Owen clamps his right elbow down and feels the glove knock it inward. Marek pivots on his left foot and whips another right low kick onto Owen's lead thigh.

Owen's left foot shifts under the contact. He slides it back into line, taps Marek's head guard with a jab and steps north along the west side. Marek stays on the inside of that movement, his harder breaths lifting his shoulders. Daniel takes two quick side steps to remain beyond their line, rather than allowing the ropes to hide Owen's legs.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Marek immediately makes a cost for the body kick; Owen uses a jab to keep the next entry outside; Daniel protects his view of both bases.
- Outcome and reaction: Marek's pressure survives the landed kick. Owen has gained an exit from the corner, not control of the ring.
- Start: about 26 s, provisional, carrying to 30 s.
- Space: northward along the west ropes; Marek remains nearer centre.

## CONTINUOUS | 30-45 s {#p03}

### The inside route {#p03-b01}

Owen jabs Marek's right glove and starts a right round kick towards his left thigh. Marek lifts his left shin into it. The dry check stops the kick; Marek sets the checking foot down and drives a right straight into Owen's high gloves. A left hook digs into Owen's right-side ribs before his elbow finishes coming back.

Owen folds over that side without folding his stance. Marek advances with a jab and a right straight, both caught on the closing gloves. Owen punches the front of Marek's chest with a short left straight to make a little separation and steps his rear foot north. Marek takes the inside diagonal first. Daniel hurries along the open centre side, shoulders square to the two men.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Owen tries to interrupt the pursuit with a low kick; Marek checks, counters and denies the inside escape; Daniel tracks the compressed work.
- Outcome and reaction: the check has an immediate punch counter. Owen's ribs cost him a full inhalation, and Marek keeps him at the west side.
- Start: 30 s phase anchor; internal timing provisional, to about 36 s.
- Sound: the shin clack gives way to muffled glove contact and Owen's broken exhale.

### One knee and release {#p03-b02}

Marek throws a jab, right straight, left hook. Owen blocks the jab and straight, then rolls under the hook towards Marek's right side. Owen rises with a left hook to the blue body; Marek's right elbow takes it.

Their shoulders meet as Marek steps across to prevent the turn. Marek places his left glove behind Owen's upper neck, keeping the head upright, and immediately drives his right knee into Owen's joined forearms at the abdomen. His left foot bears him. The knee recoils and both of Marek's hands release at once.

Owen exhales sharply against the knee and puts his right foot back to take his weight. Marek lowers his kicking foot, hands already beside his own head. Their shoulders remain jammed for an instant as each seeks the same narrow exit. Daniel comes in with both palms between their chests.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Marek turns the failed hook into one immediate knee; Owen protects the body and seeks room; Daniel recognises the remaining shoulder obstruction.
- Outcome and reaction: one knee meets the arms and jars Owen's breath. The grip is released before Daniel intervenes; there is no second knee or pull into a throw, following the [clinch limit](../../../research/kickboxing/round-practice.md#rules-and-procedure).
- Start: about 36 s, provisional, to 42 s.
- Camera: follow into a more oblique full two-fighter view, leaving the lowered knee and Daniel's approaching palms unobscured.

### The referee makes room {#p03-b03}

DANIEL

Break!

Both men stop their hands and take a step back. Daniel passes between them, palms open. Owen opens his shoulders for a deep breath, then returns his gloves to his head. Marek shifts once on his planted feet, left elbow resting lower over the ribs the body kick reached.

Daniel looks from one man to the other and steps back towards centre. Owen moves off the west ropes with a jab that taps Marek's right glove as soon as the referee clears them. Marek gives a short rearward step, turns his left shoulder in and keeps his right hand ready.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Daniel separates the shoulder jam; the fighters obey, then Owen takes the first legal return; Marek preserves a countering base.
- Outcome and reaction: the break releases Owen from the rope line. Daniel's withdrawal, not a second Fight command, resumes the contest.
- Start: about 42 s, provisional, to 45 s.
- Space: the west half opens towards the northwest quarter.
- Sound: the brief command cuts through the crowd, followed by a long breath and the single jab tap.

## CONTINUOUS | 45-60 s {#p04}

### Under the answering hook {#p04-b01}

Marek answers that jab with a right straight. Owen slips to his own right, puts a left hook into Marek's right forearm and sends a right straight towards the head. Marek catches it on his left glove and turns a left hook back.

Owen bends both knees, rolls under the hook and rises on Marek's outside with his feet still apart. A short left to the body bumps Marek's right elbow into his ribs. Owen's right glove returns to his cheek before Marek can set his next straight. Daniel mirrors the small turn from the centre side, keeping both men in profile.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Owen makes Marek's counter create an angle; Marek closes the high line and hooks back; Daniel follows their heads through the roll.
- Outcome and reaction: Owen earns a little open canvas north of the previous jam. Marek stays balanced but must turn before pressing again.
- Start: 45 s phase anchor; internal timing provisional, to about 50 s.
- Space: northwest quarter, off the west ropes.
- Camera: shallow-orbit with Owen's visible outside turn, holding both complete bodies through the change of line.

### The second body kick {#p04-b02}

Owen steps behind a double jab: the first brushes the blue forehead as Marek leans back, the second meets his right glove. Owen's right knee rises. Marek's left elbow begins to come down before the leg has swung.

Owen pivots through the body kick. Marek has lowered the elbow but left a gap in front of it; the lower shin clips forearm and then the front edge of the left ribs. Marek's trunk contracts around the contact. Owen recoils and plants his right foot. Marek immediately throws a left hook at his head, caught on Owen's raised right forearm, and a right straight that Owen steps beyond.

Daniel has moved north of their shoulders. He leans his trunk sideways for the view of the shin without losing sight of Marek's return.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Owen repeats the body threat after raising the guard; Marek tries to close it early and punch the recovery; Daniel watches the partial block and reply.
- Outcome and reaction: the second kick still costs Marek breath, but his left arm now moves before contact. Owen must retreat from the counter.
- Start: about 50 s, provisional, to 56 s.
- Sound: a forearm knock and a lower torso thud overlap Marek's compressed breath.

### A lower shield {#p04-b03}

Marek advances with a jab onto Owen's left glove, a right straight against the guard and a left hook that glances off Owen's turning right glove. Owen takes the hook on a small outward step, then lifts his right knee again.

Marek's whole left forearm drops towards the ribs this time; his left glove leaves his cheek before Owen extends the kick. The shin lands on that low forearm with a solid knock. Marek gives a short step and sends a right straight back towards Owen's head; it falls short as Owen draws away behind both gloves while replacing his kicking foot.

Owen's head stays turned towards Marek's exposed left cheek through the recovery. Marek brings the left glove back up only after the leg is down. Daniel shifts around Marek's back shoulder to keep the next lane open.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Marek drives Owen back, then protects the sore body line early; Owen makes the same threat and attends to the changed guard; Daniel adjusts to their northward drift.
- Outcome and reaction: Marek successfully blocks the third body kick, but shows the full left-arm drop at the rising knee. His right hand still makes Owen respect the return.
- Start: about 56 s, provisional, to 60 s.
- Space: north side of centre, both clear of a corner.
- Camera: the running full view places the rising right knee and departing left glove on separate silhouettes, allowing the [audience access](../../../instructions/commission.md#direction) the trap needs.

## CONTINUOUS | 60-75 s {#p05}

### Marek gives ground {#p05-b01}

Owen steps in behind a jab, right straight and left hook. Marek nudges the jab outwards with his right glove, blocks the straight with his left and bends under the hook. His knees carry his head below it and back up as he steps away to the northeast. Owen's following right straight brushes Marek's left glove; a lead left round kick reaches the inside of Marek's right thigh as he settles.

Marek turns the knee into the contact and widens his next plant. The blue corner pad now lies behind him. Owen advances behind another jab that meets Marek's right glove; Marek drives that hand back as a straight onto the red gloves and stops the step. Daniel takes the northward route around them, head turning down to the low kick and back to the returning punch.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Owen follows his new space with a connected attack; Marek parries, rolls and arrests the pursuit; Daniel keeps the low target visible from the side.
- Outcome and reaction: Owen moves Marek towards his own corner, but the counter ends the advance before Marek reaches the pad.
- Start: 60 s phase anchor; internal timing provisional, to about 66 s.
- Space: along the north half towards the blue corner, Owen on the centre side of Marek.
- Camera: truck right with the pair; the blue pad enters behind Marek rather than replacing either set of feet.

### A check turns the pursuit {#p05-b02}

Owen paws a jab against Marek's gloves, steps his right foot closer and snaps his lead left shin towards the inside of Marek's right thigh again. Marek raises his right shin to check. He sets it down outside Owen's lead foot and, from that new base, hooks left across Owen's turning head. The glove catches a glancing edge of jaw before Owen's right forearm closes.

Marek adds a right straight against the joined gloves and a left hook at the body. Owen takes that hook on the right elbow. Marek pivots towards his own left, using the planted right foot to help carry the turn away from blue. Owen turns with him and steps down the east side rather than losing him behind a shoulder. Daniel moves back towards centre as the fighters leave the corner.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Owen tries to pin the leg with a repeated inside kick; Marek checks, counters and turns out; Daniel clears their new direction.
- Outcome and reaction: the checked lead kick is answered immediately. Marek has regained initiative, Owen has a briefly displaced head guard, and the blue corner is no longer a trap.
- Start: about 66 s, provisional, to 72 s.
- Space: the northeast approach turns down the east side; no foot crosses or sweeps the other's.
- Camera: follow the visible turn south, keeping the lateral gap between the men in the full view.
- Sound: a higher shin collision, a clipped head-contact slap and Marek's sharp exhale on the body hook.

### The return from blue {#p05-b03}

Marek drives a jab and right straight high, knocking Owen's raised gloves with both contacts. His left hook follows; Owen rolls under it as it passes over his right shoulder and sends a short right to the front of Marek's body. Marek folds his left elbow across it; the glove meets arm.

Marek immediately steps his left foot forward and lashes his right shin at Owen's left thigh. Owen draws that foot back; the kick swishes past its former position. As the blue foot returns, Owen puts his lead foot down again and moves his head away from Marek's right hand. Daniel follows from centre, watching the missed kick come safely back beneath its owner.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Marek keeps the exit offensive; Owen rolls, answers and withdraws the low target; Daniel observes the moving support legs.
- Outcome and reaction: the body counter is blocked and the low kick misses. Both retain balanced stances on the east half, with Marek pressing towards centre.
- Start: about 72 s, provisional, to 75 s.

## CONTINUOUS | 75-90 s {#p06}

### The useful low arm {#p06-b01}

Marek shows a left jab without extending it. Owen's right glove twitches outward; Marek drives the real right straight onto the closing guard and follows with a left hook to the body. Owen catches it on his elbow, turns his shoulder and jabs Marek's right glove away from his face.

Owen puts the left foot outside and raises his right knee. Marek drops his left glove immediately, laying the forearm across his left ribs. Owen's body kick thumps into it. Marek exhales hard but stays upright, then swings a right straight back at Owen's head as the kicking leg recoils. Owen takes that on the left glove and lands his right foot behind him.

Daniel takes a step east to see along the low blocking forearm. Marek's left hand returns to the cheek only after Owen has regained both feet.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Marek uses his own feint to keep initiative; Owen returns to the body; Marek anticipates that target; Daniel checks the blocked contact and counter.
- Outcome and reaction: the lowered left arm is now useful to Marek: it stops the kick. Its early departure from his head is plainly tied to Owen's right knee, as the [learned tell](../../../instructions/commission.md#tell) requires.
- Start: 75 s phase anchor; internal timing provisional, to about 81 s.
- Space: east half, Marek east of Owen and Daniel beyond their northern shoulders.
- Sound: the kick's forearm knock and Marek's reply breath replace the earlier clean ribs thud.

### The distance disappears {#p06-b02}

Marek steps through a jab and right straight; Owen parries the jab with his right glove, but the straight brushes his left temple as he turns. Marek's left hook arrives behind it. Owen blocks high with the right forearm and tries to slide left.

Marek has already placed his lead foot across that exit. A short right uppercut drives into Owen's closed gloves; a left hook reaches the right ribs before the elbow is fully down. Owen's trunk tightens and his next breath comes in two parts. He answers with a short left to Marek's body, caught on the right elbow, and takes his rear foot in to close the distance. Daniel lowers his hands and moves nearer, ready for the crowded work.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Marek denies the space for another kick; Owen uses a body answer to get inside the long punches; Daniel anticipates the compressed range.
- Outcome and reaction: Marek's five-punch chain costs Owen body contact and another interrupted breath. Owen chooses close range rather than drifting helplessly backwards.
- Start: about 81 s, provisional, to 86 s.
- Camera: follow the compressing pair without tightening below their feet; Daniel enters the clear end of their shared frame.

### Owen takes his knee {#p06-b03}

Owen places his right glove behind Marek's upper neck and his left against the near shoulder. His left foot plants; the right knee comes straight up between them. Marek's left arm drops with the lift and his right joins it over the abdomen. The knee bumps the forearms into his body.

Owen releases both hands as the knee recoils, puts the right foot down and steps away. Marek straightens over his own feet, exhaling as his arms return to guard. Daniel opens a palm between them but does not halt the separation they are already making. He follows Owen's backward step with his head, then checks Marek's raised gloves.

Owen holds his left glove a little forward. Marek inches towards it behind his raised right glove, feet carrying him rather than reaching his head into the gap.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Owen buys separation with one immediate legal knee; Marek protects his body; Daniel allows the prompt release while guarding the space.
- Outcome and reaction: the second clinch has one knee and ends by the fighters' own release. Both recover on their feet; the acquired left-arm response is present even at close range.
- Start: about 86 s, provisional, to 90 s.
- Space: the pair moves away from the east side towards centre, with the south route open beyond Owen's rear shoulder.

## CONTINUOUS | 90-105 s {#p07}

It grazes the outside of Marek's left glove and cheek. Marek lifts that shoulder, closes the glove and answers with a jab against Owen's forehead guard. Daniel follows just north of their line as they drift south of centre, watching Owen's recovery foot find support before the counter.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Marek uses a high feint to open the leg; Owen recovers through defence and a counter; Daniel watches the cost without treating every reaction as a stoppage.
- Outcome and reaction: Marek's low kick makes Owen's lead-leg recovery less easy. Owen still has the base and skill to answer; neither man loses support.
- Start: 90 s phase anchor; internal timing provisional, to about 95 s.
- Sound: the thigh thud is followed by two scraping placements rather than a planted, effortless return.

### A route through the middle {#p07-b02}

Owen steps inside Marek's next jab, moving his head to his own right. A left hook meets Marek's right ribs through a late elbow, drawing a sharp grunt. Owen turns a right straight over the low return; Marek takes it on his left glove and hooks left back at the head.

Owen rolls beneath that hook and pivots left on his lead foot, bringing his rear foot round without touching Marek's. Marek turns to find him and snaps a jab after the moving shoulder. It falls short. Owen touches Marek's gloves with a jab and shifts out towards the south half. Daniel walks the arc outside their feet, keeping his gaze between the head and body lines.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Owen makes a route with body work and a turn; Marek tries to hook across it; Daniel maintains a side view of the pivot.

- Start: about 95 s, provisional, to 101 s.
- Space: centre into the south half; the west and east ropes are both clear of the pair.
- Camera: shallow-orbit with the visible pivot and follow south, using the changing background to keep the ring's route legible.

### The red corner returns {#p07-b03}

Marek steps diagonally across Owen's southward path. He drives a right straight into Owen's left glove, a left hook against the right forearm and another right straight at the body. Owen braces his left elbow against the last punch and slides back towards the southwest.

Owen sends a jab against the blue gloves to keep Marek's head back and pushes his lead left foot against the front of Marek's abdomen. Marek draws his hips away as the sole reaches him; the contact nudges him rather than stopping him. Owen folds the knee, puts the foot back down and moves before Marek's right low kick can reach it. Daniel checks the ropes over Owen's shoulder and comes round to the west side of the narrowing lane.

- Start: about 101 s, provisional, to 105 s.
- Space: southwest quarter, one retreat from the red-corner approach.
- Sound: the lower sole-on-body bump contrasts with the shin contacts; both men now breathe audibly between short bursts.

## CONTINUOUS | 105-120 s {#p08}

### The corner is not a rest {#p08-b01}

Owen shows a left hook, turning his shoulder but keeping the glove close. Marek raises his right forearm. Owen shoots a jab through the gap instead, touching the blue forehead as Marek draws his head back, then a right straight onto the closing left glove.

Marek hooks left at Owen's head. Owen bends under it and steps along the west side as he rises. Marek turns after him with a right straight; Owen catches it on the left glove and answers a short left to Marek's right ribs. The glove meets a tight elbow. Daniel clears the westward exit, then steps round the back of Marek's line to keep the two men in view.

- Intent: Owen feints with his hands to leave the corner; Marek tries to catch the exit; Daniel makes room without giving the exit to either fighter.
- Outcome and reaction: Owen reaches the west half under his own attack, carrying a heavier breath and a more deliberate lead-foot plant.
- Start: 105 s phase anchor; internal timing provisional, to about 110 s.
- Camera: truck with Owen's exit, keeping the red pad visible long enough to show what he has escaped.
