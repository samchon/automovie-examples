# The round

## INT. KICKBOXING RING - NIGHT | 0-15 s {#p01}

- Place and light: a square, pale canvas under clean overhead arena lights. Red corner southwest, blue northeast; the northwest and southeast corners are neutral. A dark seated crowd surrounds the bright ring. This is the [commissioned championship night](../../../instructions/commission.md#venue).
- Cast and opening positions: [Owen Vale](../../brief/cast/owen-vale.md#red-corner), red, stands just west of centre; [Marek Sokol](../../brief/cast/marek-sokol.md#blue-corner), blue, just east. Both are the [commissioned adult men](../../../instructions/commission.md#fighters), 180 cm and 80 kg, equally trained at championship level. Their left feet lead, gloves beside their heads. [Daniel Mensah](../../brief/cast/daniel-mensah.md#referee) stands north of the gap, the [active official](../../../instructions/commission.md#referee) throughout. Their [contrasting styles](../../../instructions/commission.md#people) follow Owen's [patient kicking](../../brief/cast/owen-vale.md#patient-kicker) and Marek's [pressure fighting](../../brief/cast/marek-sokol.md#pressure-fighter).
- Others present: two corner attendants sit outside each coloured corner in matching tracksuits. They lean forward with the action and remain outside the ropes throughout. A timekeeper in a grey jacket sits at the southeast ringside table, bell and striker within reach; his head turns towards Daniel's signals. Crowd heads follow the moving pair. Their response is wordless.
- Camera: landscape [16:9 at 1280 by 720](../../../instructions/commission.md#frame). An eye-height full view from the south [relates both fighters](../../../instructions/commission.md#camera), Owen on the left and Marek on the right, with canvas below their feet and room above their heads. Daniel is visible beyond their gap. The working view looks over the near ropes. Every exchange retains both heads, its target and both fighters' feet. Tracking follows travel; changes of angle accompany visible turns. The corner pads and open canvas retain the geography. Headings introduce no cut.
- Sound: the seated crowd's low rustle lies beneath breathing, soles and cloth. Padded hand contacts, body impacts and shin checks sound distinct; misses carry breath and movement. Under the [British English and speech ruling](../../../instructions/commission.md#language), Daniel is the sole speaking voice. [Bells and English commands](../../../instructions/commission.md#sound) are live ring sounds. There is no score, commentary, replay or compressed time.
- Time and performance: one unbroken chronology, [0-180 seconds](../../../instructions/commission.md#one-round), in [twelve consecutive phases](../../../instructions/commission.md#phases). Phase boundaries and the named finish, count, result, celebration, bell and ending instants are true anchors. Other beat starts and action durations are provisional. The [practice study](../../../research/kickboxing/round-practice.md#level-and-density) supplies the activity basis; its [outcome classes](../../../research/kickboxing/round-practice.md#outcomes) apply to the exchanges. Recovery continues through the written guarding, breathing, following and repositioning. Neither fighter enters with an injury or the acquired defensive tell.
- Sporting conditions: both men perform the [commissioned exchange vocabulary](../../../instructions/commission.md#exchange-vocabulary) under the [GLORY rules ruling](../../../instructions/commission.md#ruleset). The pre-finish [contact costs](../../../instructions/commission.md#contacts-before-finish) affect breath, guard and supported balance. [Pressure and initiative change](../../../instructions/commission.md#progression) as the men use the [whole ring](../../../instructions/commission.md#whole-ring).

### Touch into the first counter {#p01-b01}

At 0, the timekeeper strikes the opening bell. Daniel drops his extended hand out of the lane.

DANIEL

Fight!

Both men step forward. Their lead gloves meet with a padded tap. Marek draws his left back and immediately jabs Owen's left glove, drives a right straight into the closed guard and swings his right shin towards the outside of the lead thigh. Neither returns to his starting position after the touch.

Owen raises the left knee and turns the shin out. The kick cracks against it. As the shins separate, Owen puts the checking foot down towards Marek, closing the space the kick has occupied. His right straight reaches Marek's left glove as the blue right foot returns beneath its owner. Marek draws his head behind the glove and restores his stance rather than trying to punch from the returning leg. Daniel steps northeast around them, watching the checking replant become the countering step.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel; the timekeeper begins the round.
- Intent: Marek turns courtesy into pressure; Owen checks and steps into a counter; Daniel clears and observes the exchange.
- Outcome and reaction: the [actual glove touch](../../../instructions/commission.md#glove-touch) runs directly into work. Owen restores punching distance after the check; Marek's guard receives the counter while his feet recover, as the [range study](../../../research/kickboxing/competitive-exchanges.md#checking-and-range) distinguishes.
- Start: 0, true opening anchor; the exchange plays to about 5 s.
- Sound: the bell's decay runs beneath padded contacts and the sharper shin collision.

### The open body line {#p01-b02}

Marek lets Owen's next jab pass outside his head with a bend of the knees and a small slip to his own right. He plants the right foot, puts a left hook into Owen's right forearm and sends a right straight between the gloves. It brushes the left side of Owen's head. Owen turns with the contact and closes his guard.

Marek dips towards the body. Owen brings both elbows in. The dip becomes a jab against the top of Owen's closing right glove, a right to the front of the ribs and a short left hook against the head guard. Owen's breath catches on the body shot. He steps back-left with his right glove still at his cheek; the red corner comes behind his left shoulder. Daniel advances on the north side, turning from the high contact to the body line.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Marek uses a slip and body feint to split the defence; Owen contains the damage and keeps a base under the retreat; Daniel follows the changing targets.
- Outcome and reaction: Marek's five-punch attack costs Owen breath and ground. Owen remains upright and returns his gloves between the contacts.
- Start: about 5 s, provisional, continuing to 10 s.
- Space: centre to the southwest quarter, short of the red corner.
- Camera: follow the retreat left, retaining Daniel beyond the space between the men.
- Staging: Marek's northward slip develops into pressure from Owen's north-northeast, so Owen faces that way during his southwest back-left retreat with red behind his left shoulder; the camera's westward follow keeps the turned line oblique.

### An exit closed {#p01-b03}

Owen bats Marek's probing left jab aside with his right glove and puts a jab of his own on the blue gloves. His right straight follows into Marek's joined forearms. Owen shifts his lead foot towards the west-side exit.

Marek steps across that route before Owen can bring the rear foot through. His left hook swishes over Owen as Owen bends both knees and rolls beneath it. Owen rises facing him, but Marek's following right round kick reaches the outside of his left thigh before he can lift it again.

Owen's weight rocks onto the rear foot. He replaces the lead foot without crossing his legs and closes his elbows as Marek advances. Daniel moves towards the west half, keeping the red pad clear behind Owen.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Owen seeks a side exit; Marek closes it and attacks the supporting leg; Daniel watches the restricted base.
- Outcome and reaction: Owen's parry and roll succeed locally, but Marek's kick prevents the escape. The red pad remains over Owen's left shoulder, with little canvas behind him.
- Start: about 10 s, provisional; this pressure carries through 15 s.
- Space: the red-corner approach; Owen remains clear of the ropes rather than hanging on them.
- Sound: a thigh thud, Owen's forced breath and the scrape of his rear sole taking the weight.
- Staging: Marek closes Owen's westward exit from its north and interior side, leaving Owen facing him with red still behind the left shoulder.

The left forefoot turns and the lower shin swings into Marek's left ribs. Marek's right reaches Owen's left glove, knocking it against the side of his head as the kick arrives. The body contact cuts Marek's breath and ends the drive behind his punch. His left elbow descends too late to close the ribs.

- Start: about 20 s, provisional, to 26 s.
- Space: west side; Owen's support-foot placement opens the kicking line while his head withdraws from its earlier punching position.
- Sound: a padded guard knock overlaps the lower body-kick thud, followed by Marek's shortened exhale.
- Staging: After recovering the right kick, Owen moves north and inward from the west ropes while Marek remains on his centre side.

### The answer to the ribs {#p02-b03}

Marek follows Owen's recovered kicking leg with a left-right into the high guard. A left hook follows at body height. Owen clamps the right elbow down; the punch knocks it inward. Marek pivots on his left foot and whips a right low kick onto Owen's lead thigh.

Owen's left foot shifts under the contact. He returns it to line, jabs Marek's head guard and steps north along the west side. Marek takes the inside of that movement, shoulders lifting with harder breaths. Daniel sidesteps beyond their line so that neither fighter hides the other's legs.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Marek attacks the recovery and then the supporting leg; Owen uses a jab to cover the next step; Daniel retains the side view.
- Outcome and reaction: the landed body kick has not ended Marek's pressure. Owen carries the guard contact into another defended body shot and a clean thigh contact, gaining an exit from red with a lead leg that takes more care to replace.
- Start: about 26 s, provisional, carrying to 30 s.
- Space: north along the west ropes; Marek remains nearer centre.

## CONTINUOUS | 30-45 s {#p03}

Owen gathers the rear foot behind the lead rather than crossing through the closed route. Daniel moves with them on the centre side.

### One knee and release {#p03-b02}

Marek throws a jab, right straight and left hook. Owen blocks the first two, then rolls under the hook towards Marek's right side. He rises with a left hook to the body; Marek's right elbow takes it.

Marek steps across the attempted turn and their shoulders meet. His left glove goes behind Owen's upper neck, keeping the head upright. He immediately drives his right knee into Owen's joined forearms at the abdomen, bearing his weight on the left foot. The knee recoils and the grip releases at once.

Owen exhales against the impact and places his right foot back under his weight. Marek lowers the knee and restores his gloves. Their shoulders remain jammed as each seeks the same narrow exit. Daniel approaches with open palms between their chests.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Marek prevents the roll becoming an escape and takes one immediate knee; Owen protects the body and tries to turn; Daniel recognises the remaining obstruction.
- Outcome and reaction: one knee jars Owen's arms and breath. Marek releases before the official intervenes, following the [clinch limit](../../../research/kickboxing/round-practice.md#rules-and-procedure). Neither man throws or sweeps the other.
- Start: about 36 s, provisional, to 42 s.
- Camera: follow into an oblique full view that leaves the knee, both bases and Daniel's approaching hands visible.
- Staging: Owen's roll seeks Marek's northern, own-right side, and the resulting shoulder jam occupies the northbound rope-side exit with Marek still inside.

### The referee makes room {#p03-b03}

DANIEL

Break!

Both men stop their hands and step back. Daniel passes between them, palms open. Owen opens his shoulders for a deep breath and restores his gloves. Marek shifts on his feet, left elbow resting lower over the ribs that Owen reached.

Daniel looks from one man to the other and withdraws towards centre. Owen immediately moves off the west ropes behind a jab onto Marek's right glove. Marek gives a short step, turns his left shoulder in and keeps the right hand ready.

- Cast: Owen, Marek, Daniel.
- Intent: Daniel separates the shoulder jam; Owen takes the first legal return; Marek preserves his countering base.
- Outcome and reaction: the break releases Owen from the rope line. The fighters resume on Daniel's withdrawal without a second Fight command.
- Start: about 42 s, provisional, to 45 s.
- Space: the west half opens towards the northwest quarter.
- Sound: Daniel's command, Owen's long breath and the single jab tap.
- Staging: On Daniel's withdrawal, Owen's jab covers a northeast diagonal off the west ropes, with Marek yielding only a short centreward step.
