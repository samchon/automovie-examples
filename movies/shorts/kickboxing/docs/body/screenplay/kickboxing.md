### The bell and "Fight" {#s01-b01}

Two men wait in opposite corners of a bright, roped square. In the white corner, BENLLOCH bounces lightly on his bare feet, gloves at his cheeks. Across the diagonal, in the black corner, VISSER stands squarer and stiller, chin down behind black gloves.

The REFEREE stands alone in the centre between them, white shirt, dark trousers, arms loose. He looks once to each corner.

At 0.000 the timekeeper, seated at the officials' table, strikes the bell once. The note rings out and hangs in the air.

The referee extends an arm toward the space between the corners, open hand.

REFEREE
Fight!

He steps back and to the side, off the line between the corners, toward the far neutral corner.

- Cast: [Benlloch](../brief/cast/benlloch.md#watches), [Visser](../brief/cast/visser.md#champion), [the referee](../brief/cast/referee.md#words), [the timekeeper](../brief/crowds/ringside.md#timekeeper).
- Intent: the timekeeper opens the round with one strike of the bell from his seat; the referee starts the round as GLORY orders it and clears the line; both fighters wait for the command, then for each other.
- Outcome: the round begins, as the [start sequence](../brief/world/bout.md#start-sequence) sets; the timekeeper's strike from his seat is a departure the study records ([what the vocabulary lacks](../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#vocab-lacks)).
- Consequence: none to either body; the round clock now runs, and nothing is owed to the corners until 180.
- Space: Benlloch in the white corner, Visser in the black corner, the referee from the centre to a place off their diagonal toward the far neutral corner.
- Camera: wide, from the apron on the officials' side, at its steps' corner end, at the men's head height, holding both corners and the referee across the ring; it begins to ease along that side toward its centre as the referee moves.
- Sound: dialogue the referee's "Fight!"; foley Benlloch's bouncing feet, the referee's shoes on the canvas; ambience the crowd's bed rising to a swell at the bell; effects the single bell strike; music none.
- Continuity: both fighters in guard in their own corners; the referee off the line toward the far neutral corner; the camera on the officials' side.

### The touch {#s01-b02}

Both men walk out. They meet near the centre. Benlloch's right glove reaches out at chest height; Visser's left meets it. Leather taps leather, once, light.

The gloves part. Both men are back in guard before either has finished stepping back.

- Cast: [Benlloch](../brief/cast/benlloch.md#challenger), [Visser](../brief/cast/visser.md#champion), [the referee](../brief/cast/referee.md#side-view).
- Intent: each man shows respect without lowering his guard; neither fakes the touch.
- Outcome: a one-glove touch, the vocabulary's mutual meeting ([vocabulary](../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#vocab-glove-touch)); no strike during it ([styles](../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#glove-touch)).
- Consequence: none; both men are fully in guard as the touch ends.
- Space: both men from their corners to the centre, a little apart, square to each other on the white–black diagonal; the referee on his wider circle on the far neutral corner's side of them, keeping the side view.
- Camera: base two-shot head to toe from the side, the line between the men across the frame, the camera tracking in along the apron as they close.
- Sound: dialogue none; foley bare feet on the canvas, the soft tap of the gloves; ambience the crowd's swell settling to its bed; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: the men at the centre, at kicking range, in guard; the first exchange follows without a reset.

### Visser opens {#s01-b03}

Visser does not wait. His jab snaps out. Benlloch's right glove turns it aside. The cross follows; Benlloch's left glove covers his cheek and takes it with a flat smack.

As the cross comes back, Visser's weight settles onto his lead leg. His right shin swings low at Benlloch's lead thigh.

Benlloch's lead knee is already up, shin turned out. Shin meets shin: a hard, bony knock.

Benlloch's foot is down and his right cross is coming as Visser's leg returns. Visser slips it, his head moving outside the punching arm. Both men are back in range, gloves up.

The referee drifts round with them, his head on the gap between them.

- Cast: [Visser](../brief/cast/visser.md#repertoire), [Benlloch](../brief/cast/benlloch.md#repertoire), [the referee](../brief/cast/referee.md#distance).
- Intent: Visser sets the pace at once with punches finished by a low kick; Benlloch defends and answers, reading what Visser throws.
- Outcome: the jab parried, the cross covered, the low kick checked, the counter cross slipped, as [the outcome table](../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#outcomes) allows for a parry, a cover and a check in time against an unloaded leg, and a slip in time against a straight punch ([Visser's chains](../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#visser-chains), [Benlloch's chains](../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#benlloch-chains)).
- Consequence: Visser's right shin stung; he settles that leg a moment and will throw fewer low kicks for a while ([Visser's state](../brief/cast/visser.md#state)). Benlloch unhurt.
- Space: both in the centre, turning an eighth of the way round, so that the line between them runs across the ring from Benlloch, on the white–far side, to Visser, on the steps'–black side; the referee circles wider on the far side of that line, toward the black–far ropes.
- Camera: base two-shot head to toe from the officials' side, easing along it to its centre as they turn, so the line between them stays across the frame.
- Sound: dialogue none; foley the parry's brush, the cover's flat smack, the shin-on-shin knock, sharp exhales through the teeth on each strike, feet scuffing; ambience the crowd swelling on the check; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: Visser leads; Benlloch on the white–far side of Visser, Visser facing him; the camera at the centre of the officials' side; Benlloch has shown he checks when his leg is free; both breathing easily.

### The first right body kick {#s01-b04}

Visser steps in behind a jab feint. Benlloch does not move for it.

As Visser's front foot lifts to step in, Benlloch's right knee rises across his body. The shin swings at Visser's left ribs.

Visser's weight is still on his rear leg. The lifted left knee turns up and out instead of stepping, and the kick meets his shin. Knock.

Benlloch's stung foot comes down and settles a moment. Visser plants the checking foot and answers with his own right low kick at that leg, but Benlloch is stepping back and off to his left as it comes, and the shin swings through the air short of him.

Out of range, Benlloch re-squares. Visser follows a step and stops, gloves high.

- Cast: [Benlloch](../brief/cast/benlloch.md#repertoire), [Visser](../brief/cast/visser.md#tell), [the referee](../brief/cast/referee.md#side-view).
- Intent: Benlloch tries the right body kick he has come to throw; Visser defends it with a check, as he does while his leg is free, and answers with his own low kick to the landing leg ([Visser's repertoire](../brief/cast/visser.md#repertoire)); Benlloch angles out.
- Outcome: the jab feint draws no reaction; the body kick checked in time; Visser's answering low kick falls short as Benlloch steps out of range ([outcome table](../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#outcomes): a feint drawing none, a body kick checked, a round kick falling short).
- Consequence: Benlloch's shin stung, settled a moment before he steps ([Benlloch's state](../brief/cast/benlloch.md#state)); no cost to Visser. Visser's tell is absent: this is stage one ([Visser's tell](../brief/cast/visser.md#tell)).
- Space: from the centre Benlloch angles out toward the white corner's side; Visser one step after him; the referee steps back on his circle to keep both in profile.
- Camera: base two-shot from the officials' side, tracking along it toward its white corner end with Benlloch's exit.
- Sound: dialogue none; foley the knock of shin on shin, no contact on the low kick that falls short, exhales, the scrape of Benlloch's support foot as it pivots; ambience the crowd rising on the kick, falling as they part; effects none; music none.
- Continuity: the men apart, out of range, at the white corner's side of centre; the round's first right body kick has been checked.

### The cut-off {#s02-b01}

Benlloch circles to his right, away from Visser's right hand. Visser does not chase. He steps across in front of him, then again, square, closing a little each time.

Benlloch changes direction. Visser is already there. The ropes on the white corner's side are behind Benlloch now, and his circle is getting shorter.

Benlloch flicks a jab to hold him. Visser slips it outside and keeps coming.

- Cast: [Visser](../brief/cast/visser.md#repertoire), [Benlloch](../brief/cast/benlloch.md#repertoire), [the referee](../brief/cast/referee.md#distance).

- Outcome: the jab slipped, a miss ([outcome table](../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#outcomes)); the cut-off works ([styles, ring use](../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#pressure-ring-and-tempo)).

### The hip-snap and the low kick {#s02-b02}

Nothing comes. As Benlloch's foot comes back down and takes his weight, Visser's right shin lands on that thigh. A deep, heavy slap.

Benlloch's lead foot settles lighter. He shifts his weight off it for a moment, then pivots off the ropes to his left, out along them.

### The first body kick lands {#s02-b03}

Benlloch's right cross reaches out and touches Visser's guard as it comes back.

Visser answers it at once. He plants his feet to throw his own right hand.

As he plants, Benlloch's right knee is rising. Visser's left elbow starts down, his glove staying at his cheek, but the elbow is late. The shin cracks into his ribs under it. A loud, cracking slap. His breath bursts out through his teeth, and his trunk folds toward the kick.

He does not throw the right hand. He takes a step back.

Benlloch steps off at an angle toward the centre, away from the ropes.

- Cast: [Benlloch](../brief/cast/benlloch.md#repertoire), [Visser](../brief/cast/visser.md#state), [the referee](../brief/cast/referee.md#side-view).
- Intent: Benlloch finds his range with the cross and kicks the body as Visser plants to answer it; Visser starts his disciplined cover, but planted to punch he is late.
- Outcome: the cross lands on the guard; the body kick lands on the ribs through a late cover, as [the outcome table](../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#outcomes) gives for a kick launched as the defender plants to punch ([Benlloch's chains](../../research/kickboxing/exchanges.md#benlloch-chains), [styles, what pressure costs](../../research/kickboxing/styles.md#pressure-costs)).

His cover was still the disciplined one, glove at the cheek; from here it will start sooner, the [tell's](../brief/cast/visser.md#tell) second stage beginning. No knockdown ([bout](../brief/world/bout.md#costs)).
- Space: from the white–far ropes back toward the centre on Benlloch's 45-degree exit; Visser one step back, turning to face him; the referee widens.
