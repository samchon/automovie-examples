## Reproduction

From this production, generate the native output with `pnpm run film:bake`. The film, inspector and captures read `media/automovie/kickboxing/native-motion.json`; its receipt retains the native schedule and hit records. Principal poses are sampled at every 30 Hz delivery point. Peripheral seated background uses 2 Hz sampling, with 30 Hz closing movement and the SDK's native interpolation between stored points.

Capture source with `pnpm exec ttsx --no-plugins --cache-dir node_modules/.cache/ttsx-round-capture -P tsconfig.json src/executable/captureRound.ts whole-round-001`. The ordinary picture export is `pnpm run render --subject kickboxing --backend gpu`. Capture the delivered effects file with `pnpm exec ttsx --no-plugins --cache-dir node_modules/.cache/ttsx-round-capture -P tsconfig.json src/executable/captureRound.ts whole-artifact-001 media/automovie/kickboxing/videos/film-with-effects.mp4`.

This edition preserved the first 3600 native renderer PNGs, stopped that process and captured the later ranges at 30 Hz in parallel. Rate ranges exclude their upper endpoint: those jobs cover frames 3599–4498 and 4499–5398. The final frame is reused from `closing-framing-002`, whose 276 other film PNGs match the later ranges byte-for-byte. The original renderer/capture boundary at frame 3599 also matches. No pose was changed or recomputed for assembly.

All 5400 PNGs remain under `.cache/film-production/whole-film-001/frames`, with their input identity in `checkpoint.json` and individual digests and encoder arguments in the [assembly receipt](assembly-receipt.json). Re-encoding these retained frames needs no body-motion calculation or drawing. The stock CLI does not automatically resume them; [#3577](https://github.com/samchon/automovie/issues/3577) records that gap. Changed motion, camera or SDK inputs require new matching outputs.

The native sRGB rasters were converted to BT.709 with FFmpeg's existing [colorspace filter](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#colorspace), then encoded once. The effects version copies that video stream and adds AAC. The [decoded profile](encoded-profile.json) records actual file measurements. Audio/video remain outside Git; [the media manifest](../../media/manifest.json) identifies retained files and their provenance.
