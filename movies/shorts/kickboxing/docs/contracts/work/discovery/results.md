### Conflicts reported

- The finish's landing point, "behind B's left ear", against the rule forbidding strikes targeting the back of the head. Neither was changed; the brief recorded a reading, and the user confirmed it: the side of the head and neck below and behind the left ear is a legal target ([finish target](../../../body/brief/world/contest.md#finish-target), [PROJECT.md](../../../../PROJECT.md#authority-and-open-choices)).
- "Supported victory steps" is read as steps that land their weight, not steps someone else supports: the contest keeps everyone but the referee and the doctor out of the ring while the fallen man is down, so Kessler's corner stays outside and he celebrates alone ([Kessler after the finish](../../../body/brief/cast/kessler.md#after-finish), [the corners](../../../body/brief/crowds/ringside.md#corners)).

## Language

Inspected: the screenplay's language, the referee's English, regional, period, medium, translation and register boundaries, names and their address, and number, date, age, colour, material, dimension and unit wording. The screenplay is English and only the referee speaks, in the short English commands the [referee record](../../../body/brief/cast/referee.md#words) lists; the count is spoken in English words. Names are never spoken, so the audience's capacity for names is not taxed; readers meet three cue names, KESSLER, VERMEER and REFEREE. Region and period: a present-day fictional GLORY event; no dialect, period usage or regional variety is spoken, and the referee's English is the international officiating register the referee study records. Medium: a film, so the count and commands are heard, never shown as text. Translation: none; the screenplay is written in English, and only the user-facing reports are in the user's language. Register: the referee's clipped commands; the fighters have no speech. Measurements are the commission's story values, 180 cm, 80 kg and the anchor seconds, plus the fall study's "within about a second" restated in `cast/vermeer.md#fall`, all in metric units and seconds. No date or age is stated. Colours are named plainly (black, white, mid-grey, dark blue, grey) and materials only as canvas, ropes and gloves, consistently across records. No requirement exists beyond these, so no language contract is adopted.
