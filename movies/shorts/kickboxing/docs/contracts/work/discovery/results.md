## After the brief {#after-the-brief}

Checkpoint 1 read the completed brief for anything the canon needs that the direction did not foresee. Two findings came up, and both are settled at their owners:

- **The real-time count against an early stop.** A man who reads as unconscious would have his count stopped. The full count the commission keeps therefore needs Visser to read as conscious and failing to rise. Owner: [unable to rise](../../../body/brief/world/bout.md#unable-to-rise).
- **The finish's legality depends on where the contact reads.** A contact that reads on the back of the neck is a foul. Owner: [finish target](../../../body/brief/world/bout.md#finish-target).

Author inspection of the brief then found three more needs of the same kind, each settled at its owner:

- **The commission's clinch break under a one-knee rule.** A lawful clinch ends without a command, so the commission's clinch break needs a lawful occasion. That occasion is the accidental tie-up. Owner: [clinch](../../../body/brief/world/bout.md#clinch), with the procedure in the [referee's break](../../../body/brief/cast/referee.md#clinch-break).
- **A readable count on faceless bodies.** The count is shown by voice and an arm stroke, not by finger counting. Owner: [referee's signals](../../../body/brief/cast/referee.md#signals).
- **The doctor's way in against the winner's corner.** The doctor must not have to pass the winner. Owner: the ring's [corner order](../../../body/brief/sets/ring.md#corners).

No new user-owned element was found, and no two fixed conditions conflict.
