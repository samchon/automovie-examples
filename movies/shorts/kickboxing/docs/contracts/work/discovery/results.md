### Conflicts reported

- The finish's landing point, "behind B's left ear", against the rule forbidding strikes targeting the back of the head. Neither is changed; the brief records a reading for the user ([finish target](../../../body/brief/world/contest.md#finish-target), [PROJECT.md](../../../../PROJECT.md#authority-and-open-choices)).
- "Supported victory steps" is read as steps that land their weight, not steps someone else supports: the contest keeps everyone but the referee and the doctor out of the ring while the fallen man is down, so Kessler's corner stays outside and he celebrates alone ([Kessler after the finish](../../../body/brief/cast/kessler.md#after-finish), [the corners](../../../body/brief/crowds/ringside.md#corners)).

## Language

Inspected: the screenplay's language, the referee's English, names and their address, numbers and units. The screenplay is English and only the referee speaks, in the short English commands the [referee record](../../../body/brief/cast/referee.md#words) lists; the count is spoken in English words. Names are never spoken, so the audience's capacity for names is not taxed; readers meet three cue names, KESSLER, VERMEER and REFEREE. Measurements appear only as the commission's story values, 180 cm, 80 kg and the anchor seconds, in metric units and seconds. No regional, period or register requirement exists beyond these, so no language contract is adopted.
