# The rules of a GLORY bout

What the bout's rules allow, forbid and require, for a screenplay that stages one round under GLORY rules and a brief that records them as the contest's law. The primary text is the GLORY Official Rules effective 16 March 2026; where GLORY is silent, the ABC Unified Rules of Professional Kickboxing and the WAKO ring rules supply the nearest professional and amateur practice, marked as cross-checks. Sources and locators are listed in the [research index](../index.md).

Observation kinds are marked: *rule* for rule text read in the original, *cross-check* for another ruleset's text used where GLORY is silent, *inference* for a conclusion drawn from rule text, *choice* for the production's own decision.

## The round and the match

- A single GLORY match is three rounds of three minutes; a title match is five; one-minute intervals separate rounds (GLORY 5.04 A and C). *rule*
- The timekeeper times rounds and intervals and sounds the gong or bell that begins and ends each round for the referee; ten seconds before the end of an interval he signals and the referee calls "seconds out" (GLORY 5.01 F). *rule*
- If a round's time ends during a down count, the timekeeper waits until the referee has counted eight and given "fight", then sounds the end of the round (GLORY 5.05 B4). A fighter knocked down cannot be saved by the bell (ABC item 8). *rule*
- When the referee counts to ten, the timekeeper sounds the gong or bell that ends the match (GLORY 5.05 B5). *rule*
- GLORY does not say the clock stops for a down count. WAKO stops time only on the referee's "stop time" order, for an official warning, a fighter's request, equipment, injury or a restart (WAKO Referee Rules 3.4.4), and lists no stoppage for a knockdown. The round clock therefore runs through a count. *inference*

## The ring

- A square ring of canvas that must not be slippery, its floor raised 90 to 122 cm, either 6.40 m or 7.00 m square inside the ropes (GLORY 4.06 A and B). *rule*
- Four wrapped ropes, 3 to 5 cm thick, at 40, 80, 120 and 150 cm above the floor, held rigid by two vertical cloths per side (GLORY 4.06 C). *rule*
- The athletes' corners are opposite each other, one with a white post and one with a black post; the two corners between are neutral (GLORY 4.06 D). *rule*

## Attire and gloves

- Trunks ending above the knee; the male upper body uncovered; no shoes or foot padding; mouthguard and groin protection compulsory; ankle socks allowed; no jewellery (GLORY 4.01 B to G). *rule*
- Gloves are provided by GLORY, equal in weight, and in a single match the same colour as the athlete's corner; 10 oz above 65 kg (GLORY 4.03 A and B). Hands are wrapped and taped under inspection (GLORY 4.02). *rule*
- A fighter at 80 kg falls in the middleweight class, over 77 up to 85 kg (GLORY 2.01 D). *rule*

## Legal techniques and targets

- Scoring strikes must be thrown with full power, from correct distance and body position, onto a legal target: punches with the padded glove (straights, hooks, uppercuts, spinning backfist), kicks with the foot or lower leg (front kicks, low kicks to the inside and outside of the leg, middle kicks, high kicks, side, back, axe, spinning and jumping kicks), and knees (front, roundhouse, jumping, and knees to the leg inside and outside) (GLORY 5.06 A). *rule*
- Prohibited techniques include head butts; any technique targeting the back of the head, the back or the groin; linear or thrusting kicks to the legs, which may only be attacked with arcing kicks; elbows and any strike with the arm above the glove's padding; open-glove strikes; wrestling, judo and strangling techniques; throws, leg sweeps, foot sweeps and pushing meant to off-balance or down an opponent (GLORY 5.07 A1 to A10). *rule*
- Clinching or holding is prohibited except to attack immediately with a single legal knee, after which the athlete must disengage at once (GLORY 5.07 A11). An opponent's leg may be held only to land one immediate legal strike with at most one step, then released (GLORY 5.07 A12). *rule*
- Prohibited conduct includes continuing to fight while the opponent has any part of the body other than the feet on the floor, striking after "break" or "stop", striking an opponent outside the ring or tied in the ropes, pushing an opponent out of or over the ropes, and holding the ropes (GLORY 5.07 B). Talking and rude sounds are unsportsmanlike (GLORY 5.07 D1). *rule*
- Permissible head targets are the front and sides of the head (ABC item 12; WAKO K1 Style rules article 3 adds the forehead). ABC defines the back of the head as the area from the crown down the centre of the head, spreading from ear to ear across the back of the head, and the entire rear of the neck (ABC item 11 D). WAKO K1 also forbids attacks to the neck (article 5). *cross-check*

### The finish's landing point {#landing-point}

The commission lands the finish on "the area behind B's left ear at the junction of jaw and neck". GLORY forbids techniques *targeting* the back of the head and draws no line; ABC draws its rear boundary from ear to ear across the back of the head. The angle of the jaw, where jaw meets neck, lies below and slightly in front of the ear; the hollow directly below and just behind the ear lobe is still on the side of the head, forward of a band drawn behind the ears. A kick aimed at the side of the head that lands there is a side strike, not a strike targeting the back of the head. *inference* The production keeps the owner's wording and reads it this way, and the user confirmed the reading: the side of the head and neck below and behind the left ear is a legal target. *choice*

## Officials and corners

- The referee stays in the ring for the whole match and alone makes decisions in it (GLORY 5.01 A). Three to five judges score; ring inspectors, a judge supervisor, a timekeeper, a scorekeeper and a ring announcer serve outside the ring (GLORY 5.01 B to H). *rule*
- A ring doctor must be present at the ring throughout; during an examination only the ring doctor may enter the ring (GLORY 5.01 E1 and E4). *rule*
- Each athlete may have up to three cornermen (GLORY 1.04 A). During the match they stay seated in their designated seats; excessive coaching, entering the ring and pounding the apron are prohibited; only the chief cornerman tends the athlete inside the ropes, and only between rounds (GLORY 5.03 B to D). During a down count no cornerman may approach his athlete (GLORY 5.05 B2). *rule*

## Referee commands, downs and the result

- Commands: "fight" begins or resumes the match once the athletes stand an equal distance from the referee; "break" separates them, each takes a step back, the referee steps between and they resume without "fight" as he steps back; "stop" halts the fight at the end of a round or before a caution or warning (GLORY 5.05 A). WAKO adds "shake hands", the order to touch gloves at the beginning of the fight (WAKO Referee Rules 3.4.4). *rule*, *cross-check*
- An athlete is down when a damaging attack makes any part of the body other than the feet touch the floor, or would have but for the ropes or the opponent; GLORY also allows a standing eight count for an athlete in a weakened state (GLORY 5.05 B1). ABC has no standing count (ABC item 7). *rule*, *cross-check*
- The referee announces the down by call and gesture, sends the opponent to the farthest neutral corner, and then starts the count. The opponent waits there until released; if he leaves early the count stops until he returns (GLORY 5.05 B2). *rule*
- The referee must count at least to eight before assessing the athlete; if he counts to ten without interruption, the athlete loses by knockout (GLORY 5.05 B3 and B5). A knockout is also declared before ten when the referee needs immediate medical help (GLORY 5.06 E2). *rule*
- WAKO counts from one to ten at one-second intervals, showing each second with the fingers so the fallen fighter knows the count, and declares the knockout if the fighter does not stand in fighting position with raised hands by ten (WAKO Rules chapter 7, article 9). *cross-check*
- Three knockdowns in one round end a single match by technical knockout (GLORY 5.06 E3c). *rule*

### Outcome table

| Situation | Permitted outcome |
| --- | --- |
| A legal damaging strike puts any part of the body other than the feet on the floor | Down: call and gesture, opponent to the farthest neutral corner, count from one |
| The downed fighter stands ready by eight and the referee judges him able | "Fight"; the round resumes |
| The downed fighter is not in fighting position by ten | Knockout; the bell ends the match |
| The opponent leaves the neutral corner during the count | The count stops until he returns |
| Two fighters hold without an immediate knee | Foul; the referee breaks them |
| One knee from the clinch, then release | Legal |
| A strike lands after "break" or "stop", or on a downed man | Foul: caution, warning, penalty or disqualification by severity (GLORY 5.08) |
