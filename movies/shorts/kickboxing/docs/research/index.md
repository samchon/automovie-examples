# Research index

The studies the kickboxing brief draws on, and the originals they read. Every original was retrieved on 2026-11-13 and opened before it was cited. Copies whose rights permit retention sit under `references/`; rights-unconfirmed copies sit under the git-ignored `references/private/` and are kept for local study only. No footage was acquired: no video decoder is available on this machine, so no study reads moving pictures, and each value only footage would give is marked in its study as unavailable, inferred or chosen.

The first four studies serve the brief. The screenplay's own study, [exchanges](kickboxing/exchanges.md), adds what the screenplay needs when it names the actions, as [when to study](../../../../../.agents/skills/story/planning/research.md#when-to-study) sets: the exchange outcome table, the chains, the tempo targets and the laws of the actions the motion vocabulary lacks.
