# Research index

The studies of every practice the kickboxing screenplay shows, and the originals they read. Every original was retrieved on 2026-11-13 and opened before it was cited. Originals whose rights permit retention are under `references/`; rights-unconfirmed copies are under the git-ignored `references/private/` and are for local study only, never for publication. No footage was acquired or sampled: the machine has no video decoder, so no study describes footage, and every value a footage measurement would give is marked in its study as unavailable, inferred or chosen.

## Studies {#studies}

| Study | Owns |
| --- | --- |
| [Rules](kickboxing/rules.md) | The GLORY ruleset the round follows: round and bells, legal techniques and targets, fouls, the clinch, the knockdown, count and neutral corner, medical attendance and the result, the ring and equipment, and its outcome table |
| [Exchanges](kickboxing/exchanges.md) | Championship exchanges built from the motion vocabulary's single movements: chaining, feints, the check and counter, the defences in use, the clinch knee, how a defensive habit forms and is exploited, ring use and initiative, level markers, the cost of landed blows, the outcome table and the activity targets |
| [Brazilian kick](kickboxing/brazilian-kick.md) | The finishing kick's law, why it beats a lowered guard, its setups, legality and elite use, and the struck man's visible fall |
| [Referee](officiating/referee.md) | The officials present, the referee's positioning, clinch break, commands and signals, gaze and posture, the knockdown procedure against the commission's anchors, his care of the fallen man, and his targets |
| [Fight coverage](cinematography/fight-coverage.md) | How ring combat is shot in broadcast and in single-take film, the camera's laws, its framing and movement targets, and the look |
| [Ring sound](sound/ring-sound.md) | What a live ring sounds like without commentary: bells, the referee's voice, breath, contact classes, canvas, ropes, corners and crowd, with the palette's rules and targets |
