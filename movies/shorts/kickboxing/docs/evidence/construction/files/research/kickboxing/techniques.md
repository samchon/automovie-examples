# docs/research/kickboxing/techniques.md

## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#depicted-practice

Each technique the round will show has its law: stance and steps with their foot order and weight (AIBA 2.5.1 and 2.5.2); punches with their guard, weight shift and range (AIBA 2.5.3, 2.5.5, 2.5.7); the rear roundhouse kick by its four measured phases, contact surfaces, pivot and recoil (Gavagan and Sayers 2017; Evolve); the teep and the clinch knee with their rule limits; every defence the commission names with its trigger, body action and return to stance (AIBA 2.5.4; Evolve on the check); feints (AIBA 2.5.6); and the Brazilian kick from the body-kick chamber. The outcome table pairs attacker and defender intents with permitted outcomes, including every outcome the commission fixes: costly blows without knockdowns, the feint that opens a target, the check and its counter, and the knockout, whose fall has its own measured law (Huang et al. 2023). Chains appear as combinations, check-and-counter and feint-and-follow-up; the markers separating the depicted level from a beginner are listed; measured kick and fall values are given as means with standard deviations and their method.

The Brazilian kick's landing with instep and lower shin is the commission's, not the coaching text's; the study names the coaching question-mark kick as the closest analogue and states the departure. Reach, stance width and step length have no retained source and are recorded as unavailable rather than invented. Spatial strategy and tempo belong to the [bout-patterns study](../../../../../research/kickboxing/bout-patterns.md), the conduct of officials to the [referee study](../../../../../research/officiating/referee.md) and shooting practice to the [fight-coverage study](../../../../../research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md). No installed motion vocabulary carries any of these movements, so none is deferred.

## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#traceable-sources

Each statement names its source and locator, section, table, page or named passage, and is marked *measured*, *coaching*, *rule*, *inference* or *choice*. Measured values keep their standard deviations and state their conditions: 24 skilled practitioners kicking a fixed pad at mastoid height, and 153 video-analysed knockouts. Unavailable sources and the limits they leave are recorded in the study's last section.
