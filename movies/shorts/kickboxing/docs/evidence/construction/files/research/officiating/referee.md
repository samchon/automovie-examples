# docs/research/officiating/referee.md

## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#depicted-practice

The study gives the referee and the ringside their practice: where the referee stands and when he steps in, his commands and their language, his hand signals, the knockdown procedure step by step, his conduct after a knockout, his dress, and the duties of the doctor, timekeeper, judges, announcer and cornermen while they are present. Its outcome rows are the rules study's officiating table, which this study's procedure realizes. The one value it gives, the count's one-second interval shown on the fingers, comes from WAKO's ring rules; where no source measures the referee's distance or speed, that is recorded as unavailable and the side-of-the-line position is marked as an inference from the rules.

Technique laws, chains, level markers and measured performance targets of the fighters belong to the [technique](../../../../../research/kickboxing/techniques.md) and [bout-patterns](../../../../../research/kickboxing/bout-patterns.md) studies, and shooting practice to the [fight-coverage study](../../../../../research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md). WAKO practice adopted for this fictional contest where GLORY publishes none is marked as the production's choice.
