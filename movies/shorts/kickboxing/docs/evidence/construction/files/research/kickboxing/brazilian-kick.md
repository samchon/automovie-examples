# docs/research/kickboxing/brazilian-kick.md

## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#depicted-practice

The study gives the finish the law the installed `kick` action lacks (statement 1): its phases in the kicker's own frame, from the body-kick chamber with the support foot pivoting on its ball, through the half-second sell and the turnover in which the support heel turns to the target and the hips roll over, to the downward delivery and the recoil; its contact surfaces (instep and lower shin, recorded with the sources' disagreement); its support and weight transfer through the pivot; and how the body arrives from the body kick's own distance. What it exploits and when it works are recorded (statements 3 and 4 through the outcome row it points to and the exploit law). The earlier production's reach measurement is carried as a reported limit for motion work. Statement 11 does not apply: the kick is a real technique, not an invented one. Statements 2, 5, 6, 8 and 9 are owned by the rules, ring craft, referee, coverage and exchanges studies. Statements 7 and 10 do not apply: no source measures the kick's timing or separates levels of it, and the study says so instead of inventing a value; its timing is the installed round kick's. Statements 12 to 15 hold: the only values are quoted coaching words marked as such.

## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#traceable-sources

Phases 1 to 4 of the law are marked [reported] and cite their sources by id (`EVOLVE-QMK`, `DS-KICKS`, `BB-ROBSON`, `BBM-ROUND-KICK`, `LOWKICK-QMK-2020`, `LOWKICK-QMK-2011`, `SHERDOG-FEITOSA-2005`), with the study's own readings in phases 2 and 3 marked [inference] inline; phase 5 and the `#exploit` law are marked [inference], and the production values [choice, within the sources]. The other [reported] lines cite their ids too: `SHERDOG-FEITOSA-2005`, `SENSHI-FEITOSA` and `SHERDOG-FEITOSA-INTERVIEW` in `#sources`, `EVOLVE-QMK`, `LOWKICK-QMK-2020` and `LOWKICK-QMK-2011` in `#exploit`, and `LEGACY-BRAZILIAN-MEASUREMENT` in `#earlier-measurement`, whose reading is marked [inference]; the one exception is the `#sources` record of what is unavailable, which names no retained source. The list of coaching descriptions that opens `#sources` is an inventory, not an observation. The pause's "half a second" is kept as the coaching value it is. Unavailable sources are listed: an original tying the kick to Ademir da Costa, a K-1 knockout (rather than a knockdown) by it, and any footage or measured timing or angle of the kick.
