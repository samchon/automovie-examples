# docs/research/kickboxing/rules.md

## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#depicted-practice

The study gives the brief and screenplay the contest's law without invention: the round, interval and bell (GLORY 5.04, 5.01 F, 5.05 B4 and B5), the ring's shape, ropes and corner colours (GLORY 4.06), attire and glove colour (GLORY 4.01 to 4.03), legal and prohibited techniques and conduct, including the clinch-knee and leg-catch limits (GLORY 5.06 A, 5.07), and the down, count and knockout procedure, with an outcome table for every officiating situation the round can reach. Where GLORY is silent, ABC and WAKO text fills the gap and is marked as a cross-check; the one conclusion not stated by any rule, that the clock runs through a count, is marked as an inference. The finish's landing point is compared with the back-of-head rule and the production's reading, which the user confirmed, is marked as a choice.

Statements on technique laws, chains, spatial strategy, level markers, shooting practice and measured targets do not apply to a rules study: the [technique](../../../../../research/kickboxing/techniques.md), [bout-patterns](../../../../../research/kickboxing/bout-patterns.md) and [fight-coverage](../../../../../research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md) studies own them. The conduct of officials and corners is covered here as rule and in full in the [referee study](../../../../../research/officiating/referee.md). The only values are rule values, each from its article. No invented action is involved.

## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#traceable-sources

Every statement names its rule and article: GLORY Official Rules v2.2 by section, ABC Unified Rules by item, WAKO Rules and WAKO Referee Rules by chapter, article or appendix, each retained under `references/private/` and listed with URL, date and rights in the research index. Each statement is marked *rule*, *cross-check*, *inference* or *choice*. The missing GLORY signal set and the missing clock rule are recorded as unavailable.
