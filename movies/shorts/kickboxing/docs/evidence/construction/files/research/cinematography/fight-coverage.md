# docs/research/cinematography/fight-coverage.md

## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#depicted-practice

The study records how the genre is shot: the broadcast camera package and its positions on the platform, at the corners, on the apron and in the air, the blacked-out arena, and the three fiction-film practices of the audience's view through the ropes, the camera inside the ring and the continuous take, each with the production that used it. It then states what each practice gives this film and the production's choice among them.

Technique laws, legal actions, outcome tables, chains, spatial strategy, conduct and level markers concern the depicted fight and belong to the other studies. No retained source measures shot length or cut rate, so the film's cutting is recorded as the production's choice rather than given a target; this and the unretrieved sources are listed as unavailable.

## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#traceable-sources

Every statement names its article and passage and is marked *reported* for a maker's or trade account, *analysis* for a critic's reading, *inference* or *choice*. The two cinematographers' articles and the ring sound article that could not be retrieved are recorded as unavailable.
