# docs/research/kickboxing/exchanges.md

## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#depicted-practice

The study records the practice the installed actions do not carry: how championship exchanges are built from them. Its outcome table (statement 3) says which attack, answer and timing yield which outcome class and contact, with a consequence for each class and each kind of landed blow, the high kick and the teep included, and gives the Brazilian kick its own row; every outcome the films use (parried, blocked, evaded, checked, glancing, landed, read, ignored) has a row. Chaining (statement 4) is in the combinations, defence and feints laws: punches lead and a kick finishes, a hook loads the lead leg for the low kick, every answer becomes a counter, a feint shows a technique's first third, the follow-up goes at what the answer has left open, and the follow-up's own answer decides its outcome under the outcome table. The patterns-and-habits law gives the trigger-to-response rule the finish depends on, with its reach decided: the drop answers every rising right knee and is added to the answer each technique's line calls for, so only the head above the dropped arm is opened, and a low kick is checked or lands exactly as it would without the habit; it is marked as the production's choice because no source states the arm drop. Level markers (statement 7) are in "What marks the level". Measurable targets (statements 9 and 10) give the exchange interval, the output band, the share of strikes in combinations and the initiative split, each with its source range or marked as a choice; the activity section states the translation from Ouergui's full-contact sample to a GLORY round (statement 12). Statement 1 does not apply: single techniques' body laws belong to the installed `punch`, `kick`, `knee`, `guard`, `step` and `defend` actions (statement 14). Statements 5 (spatial strategy), 6 (officials and crowd) and 8 (how it is shot) are owned by the ring craft, referee, knockout, coverage and sound studies. Statement 11 does not apply: no technique here is invented. Statement 2 is the rules study's; this study keeps every pairing legal under it.

## @evidence shared/principles/research.md#traceable-sources

Each observation is marked [reported], [inference] or [choice], and every reported one names its retained source by id, which the index resolves to its retained copy. Many citations add the table, page, section or numbered item they rest on (`OUERGUI-2014` Table 2, pp. 3539–3542; `ROMANENKO-2025` p. 69–72; `BE-DUTCH` Kaman #1, Aerts #2; `EVOLVE-20-SETUPS` set-up 5); others give the id alone and point to the retained copy as a whole, among them `ONEFC-SUPERBON`, `TBT-SUPERBON`, `SK-SUPERBON`, `DS-HOOST`, `FP-ADESANYA` and `EVOLVEVAC-FEINTS`. Uncertain values keep their ranges (2.2 ± 1.2 s, 3.4 ± 1.2 s, 52.7 ± 14.9%), the abstract-only Calabrese figures say so, and the self-contradicting Ouergui ratio is reported with both readings. The unavailable source for the arm-drop habit is recorded in the study and in the index.
