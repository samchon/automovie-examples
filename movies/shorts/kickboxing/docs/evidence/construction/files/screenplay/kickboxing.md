## @evidence shared/obligations/narratives.md#continuity-and-progression

Followed in order, every phase changes the bout (statements 1 to 3): Bastien takes the centre and loses it (s01), lands the first low kick (s02), the habits are traded (s03), the ropes and clinch (s04), the body kick lands and the drop is born (s05), its reach (s06), the third low kick slows Arjan's leg (s07), the drop meets a knee and Kaman's pattern (s08), the proof (s09), the finish (s10), the count (s11, s12). States are carried across joins (statement 5): the low-kick counts on Arjan's leg, Bastien's habit from s05-b03, breath growing heavier, who holds the centre, and each scene's last position meeting the next scene's first. The two pursuits are followed (statements 9 and 10): Arjan's plan to make Bastien expect the body kick, first recognisable at s05-b03 and cashed at s10-b04; Bastien's aim of breaking the lead leg, realised in three landed low kicks and the slowed leg, never enough. No threshold is crossed without pressure: the knockdown follows the only kick the table lets drop a man.

## @evidence shared/obligations/narratives.md#promises-and-distribution

The opening gives a reason to continue at once (statement 1): two equal men, the centre contested, a check answered. The engine is the alternation of initiative and the pattern the audience watches being built; its rewards are spaced (the first landed body kick at a third of the way, the drop just after, the proof at two thirds, the finish at 150), and its longest wait, s06 to s08, is filled by Bastien's leg war and the drop's reach (statements 2 and 3). Time is real throughout, with no compression, as the work record fixes (statement 4). Recurring exchanges are varied as the practice varies (statements 6 to 8): each fighter's signature combination recurs with different answers and results, the check is answered by different counters, and the inspection rounds removed one recurring exchange shape. The rising right knee is the motif and changes meaning each time it returns: kick, drop, feint, finish (statement 10).
