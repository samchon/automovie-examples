# docs/body/brief/cinematography/camera.md#look-facet

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises the film's look, and `#look` settles it as "one observer at ringside who reacts to the action and relates the two men", working from the apron with its lens "at about the height of the men's heads, looking slightly down", and moving along the apron when the fight comes toward its own ropes.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

The record settles where the camera may and may not be: "It never enters the ring", and "It never goes to the canvas, overhead or onto a fighter's body. It never takes a fighter's point of view." The apron position and the rope geometry cite the coverage study (`#sober-coverage`, `#apron-cameras`, `#main-cameras`).

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#camera

The look subsection realizes "The camera reacts to the action and relates the fighters": the camera "reacts to the action and relates the two men".

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#tone

The look subsection keeps the tone's sober coverage: "It is not a highlight reel".

# docs/body/brief/cinematography/camera.md#framing-facet

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises the framing, and settles a base frame, "the head-to-toe two-shot from the side, with the line between the men running across the screen", with room above the heads "for a raised kicking leg" and below the feet "for the canvas" (`#side-view`), and the exact places where it may come closer or go wider (`#sizes`).

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

The record settles the conditions on each size: closer only "Between exchanges, after the referee's break or at a reset with the men out of range", back to the base frame "before the next exchange starts", and "the clinch keeps the base frame"; wider for the count, the last image and "when the men cross the ring". Its failure rule is explicit: "A frame that drops either man's feet, the struck target or either head during an exchange is wrong, however close or dramatic."

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#camera

The side-view subsection realizes "Keep feet, targets and heads readable together on the same screen axis": "Both men, their feet, the struck target and both heads are readable together on that one screen axis".

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#ending

The sizes subsection gives the ending its wide frame: "for the last image, which holds the winner, the fallen man and the referee together".

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#count

The sizes subsection frames the count with A in the neutral corner and B down: it "holds the neutral corner and the fallen man together".

# docs/body/brief/cinematography/camera.md#movement-facet

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises how the camera moves, and `#movement` settles it: "only in answer to the fighters, smoothly and at their pace", tracking, easing in and out, moving round the ring as their line swings, and moving "round him rather than cutting" when the referee blocks the view.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

The record settles the limits: "It keeps the side view as their line turns", it works "from the side of the men away from the referee where it can", and "It never shakes for effect, whips, spins or flies." The practices cite the coverage study (`#motion`, `#referee-occlusion`).

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#camera

The movement subsection realizes a camera that reacts: "The camera moves only in answer to the fighters".

# docs/body/brief/cinematography/camera.md#cutting-facet

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises when the camera may cut, and `#cuts` settles it: "A cut is allowed only at a reset: after the referee's break, or at a moment when the men stand apart out of range", and it may cross the ring, as broadcasts do.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

The record settles the exceptions a screenplay must keep: never during an exchange, never "from the start of the finishing kick's chamber to the end of Visser's fall", never "inside the count", and "No cut skips time or repeats it". It settles how the audience keeps the men apart across a reverse cut: "by white and black, not by which side of the screen each is on". It cites the coverage study's `#cutting` and `#screen-direction`.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#fall

The cuts subsection realizes "Show the decisive kick and articulated fall without a cut", from the chamber's start to the end of the fall.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#count

The cuts subsection keeps the count uncut, "which plays in real time".

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#sound

The cuts subsection keeps the sound ruling's "No replay, time compression" in the picture: "No cut skips time or repeats it".

# docs/body/brief/cinematography/camera.md#light-facet

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises the light and grade, and `#grade` settles them: "the arena's real light", "a bright, even ring and a dim, warm ringside falling into darkness", "graded once for the whole film, near neutral, with no colour shift between phases", at real speed throughout.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

The record settles the grade's scope, the whole film with no per-phase shift, and points to the light's owner in `sets/arena.md#lighting` and to real speed's owner in `world/work.md#left-out`. It cites the coverage study's `#colour-grade` and `#no-slow-motion`.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#venue

The grade subsection keeps the venue's crowd "in the dark beyond the ring": the ringside light falls "into darkness".
