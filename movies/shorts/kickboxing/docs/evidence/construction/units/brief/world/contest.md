# docs/body/brief/world/contest.md#rules

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises the rules in force and states what may be thrown, the finish's target reading and the down and knockout law, including "Before the finish no blow puts either man down".

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The rules are narrowed to what this round uses: the clinch "for one immediate knee", the commission's landing point read against GLORY's back-of-head rule, and the running clock through the count.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

It settles legal and illegal actions, the landing point's reading with its open status, what makes a man down, when he has lost and what the bell does, so the screenplay can stage the finish and the count legally; the procedure itself is linked to the referee's record.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#record-and-currency

Rules are stated here once and cite the rules study for their text; the referee's performance of them belongs to his record, linked from "Downs and the result". The landing point is marked open for the user, as PROJECT.md records.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#ruleset

Adopts the clinch for one knee and release, no elbows, throws or sweeps, and no attack on a downed man.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#contacts-before-finish

Adopts that landed blows before the finish "cost breath, guard and balance, never the canvas".

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#finish
