## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

The record settles the condition that keeps the aftermath coherent: "Visser falls on the steps' side of the diagonal between the white and black corners, so that Benlloch's corner is the far neutral corner and the doctor's way in never passes the winner." It sets three more conditions the count's staging needs, "Near the centre", "Back to the far corner" and "Round the feet", and defines the far half where Benlloch celebrates: "he may come up to the diagonal, beside Visser in the wide frame, but not across it". It settles the neutral pads, "red pads. Red makes them the corners that read as neither man's", and cites the rules study's `#corner-colours`.

It settles which way the corner order runs, "runs anticlockwise", and what that fixes for the fall: a man facing the steps' corner "has the white corner's side on his right", so Visser falls toward it and lies "with his head toward the white corner's side and his feet toward the black corner's". The decision was established by the screenplay, and the record cites the beat that established it, "The screenplay's finish established this placement", and keeps it "for every scene".
