# docs/body/brief/sets/ring.md#zones

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

It names every zone and landmark the fight uses: the centre, the open ring, the ropes, both fighters' corners, the neutral corners and the apron.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

Each zone says how it changes the fight: taking the centre back is an act, the ropes leave two steps and a way out along them, the farther neutral corner receives the standing man.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

It settles the zones beat records will cite for where everyone stands.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#record-and-currency

The corner colours agree with the gloves and the contest's corner rule.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#whole-ring

The centre, the open ring where the fighters "circle, angle off and trade", the ropes and the corners give the screenplay the whole ring.
