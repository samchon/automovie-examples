# docs/body/brief/sets/ring.md#orientation

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

It states where the four corners lie relative to the hard camera, which the zones name but did not place.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The black corner and the near neutral corner on the hard camera's side, the black corner at its left end; the far neutral corner opposite the black corner and the white corner diagonally across from it; and the four sides named near, far, left and right as the hard camera sees them, each with its ropes and apron.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

It settles the layout every scene's space relies on, including which neutral corner is farther from a man who falls near the near side, and so which fixed camera the coverage study's count choice uses.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#record-and-currency

It records a decision the screenplay's opening beat establishes and every later scene keeps: both H3s cite `s01-b01`, where the corners are placed and the side names are first used, as statement 5 asks. Its example placement, the operator at the far end of the right-side apron, is the one s04 now uses. It was added under the closed-pass rule as a new H2, leaving the reviewed facets unchanged.
