# docs/body/brief/crowds/ringside.md#officials

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises the officials who sit at ringside, and settles each with a place and a behaviour: the table "at the centre of one side of the ring, at the foot of the apron" with the timekeeper, judge supervisor, scorekeeper and announcer (`#officials-table`); the timekeeper's two bell strikes (`#timekeeper`); three judges who "watch and write, and do nothing else" (`#judges`); and the doctor and paramedic at the steps (`#doctor`). When the doctor reaches Visser is left to the screenplay.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

The record settles the limits a screenplay keeps: the announcer "only watches, because his work falls after the film"; the timekeeper "does not count the knockdown, and nobody at ringside does"; the paramedic comes in only if called, "which does not happen before 180"; and the doctor "enters only when the referee calls him", by the steps, and "Only he may enter during his examination." The officials' roles cite the rules study's `#officials` and `#scoring`.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#venue

The timekeeper subsection realizes "a timekeeper rings the bell from ringside", striking it "once at 0.0" and "from 170.1".

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#count

The timekeeper subsection holds the match-ending bell at the count ruling's 170.1, after the knockout signal.

# docs/body/brief/crowds/ringside.md#corner-teams

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises the corner teams, and settles them: three cornermen each "in matching team sportswear: white for Benlloch, black for Visser", a ring inspector with each team, coaching "by gesture" during the round (`#corners-seated`), and what each corner does after the knockout (`#corners-after`).

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

The record settles the limits: "no word from them is ever heard", "Neither team enters the ring before 180", and "Nobody holds Benlloch up or steps in to join him." The inspector's role cites the rules study's `#officials`, and the corners' voices the coverage study's `#corner-voices`.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#venue

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#ending

The after-the-knockout subsection keeps the reading of "supported victory steps" as A's own: "Nobody holds Benlloch up or steps in to join him".
