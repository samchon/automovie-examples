# docs/body/brief/world/work.md#promise

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises the work's audience, experience, tone and film, and its three records settle each: the audience is "adults who may know nothing of kickboxing's rules", the tone is "Sober sports realism", and the film is "One film, `kickboxing`, 180 seconds long in a landscape 16:9 frame". Nothing is left for a later author.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The experience is built from this bout's own material, the body kick that lands, the left arm that drops at the rising knee and the feint that proves it, and the tone is defined by what this knockout is not given: "no slow motion, glamour lighting or heroic framing", "neither celebrated by the film nor dwelt on as horror".

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

It settles whom the film is for, what they must see in what order of understanding, who leads ("he leads much of the round"), how the film closes ("leaving nothing open"), its tone, and its running time and frame in agreement with the linked delivery profile, so a screenplay author can hold every phase to them.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#record-and-currency

The promise is the first facet of `world/work.md`, the brief's fixed-conditions file; the delivery values are owned by PROJECT.md and linked, and the experience is not restated in other records. No screenplay exists yet to establish anything this record must take up.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#direction

Adopts the delegated direction as the audience's experience: let in on the plan through the landed body kick, the dropping arm and the proving feint; the loser no victim; a complete close.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#one-round

Fixes the single film at 180 seconds.

# docs/body/brief/world/work.md#scope

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises what the film shows, what it leaves out and the seconds it lands on, and its records state all three: "One scheduled three-minute round, the bout's first", a list of everything excluded, and an anchor table from the phase boundaries to 180.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The scope is this round's: the film's clock is made the round's clock so "the round would have reached its scheduled end at 180 seconds", and the exclusions name what this commission refuses, not a generic list.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

It settles the film's boundary, the condition that "No knockdown before the finish" happens, that no medical reason is given, and every anchor second with its event, so the screenplay can land picture and sound on them and invent none.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#record-and-currency

The exclusions and the anchors are owned here; the sound record links to "what the film leaves out", and the cast, ringside and referee records link to the anchors. Two exclusions are also restated where they bind a single record: "Nothing in the film says why." closes `cast/vermeer.md#down`, and the no-knockdown condition is the last sentence of `world/contest.md#downs`. Both agree with this record. The definition, the settled content and the conditions come in that order.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#one-round

Adopts the one round, its early end by knockout and "Nothing outside that round is shown".

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#count

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#finish

Adopts 150.000 as the anchor on which the finish lands.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#no-injury-mechanism

Adopts "No line, caption or image says why the struck man cannot rise".

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#contacts-before-finish

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#sound

# docs/body/brief/world/work.md#representation

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises what the representation can and cannot show, and states both for people, "no face, eyes, mouth, hair, skin detail, sweat, blood, swelling or marks", and for things, which "show shape, position and colour, not texture".

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

Its limits are drawn for this film's needs: no visible mouthguard, wound or expression, and the referee's and the doctor's care read "from what their hands and bodies do", the readings the knockout and its aftermath depend on. The dark hall "shows no figures", and the doctor alone becomes visible by coming into the light.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

It settles who is a polygon man, the doctor included once he enters, what a beat may ask of a polygon man and what it may not, how attention, effort and care are read instead, and that nobody in the dark hall is seen, so no screenplay writes past the representation.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#record-and-currency

The representation is recorded once, here, linked from PROJECT.md's representation entry; other records describe only what their subjects do within it.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#referee

Adopts the commission's representation reading for the faceless referee: attention reads from where his head and trunk turn, effort from posture and breath, and care from what his hands and body do. The reading of his expression through his voice is held by `cast/referee.md#words`.

# docs/body/brief/world/work.md#form

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises the form every scene shares and delivers it: "one continuous scene in the ring, written as twelve fifteen-second phases", its headings and beat notation, what the beats are before and after the finish, which phase the landing at 150.000 opens, and the condition that each phase hands the next a changed situation, the fight's before the finish and the procedure's after it.

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#specificity

The form is this film's: a single ring, twelve fifteen-second phases, and change measured in "who leads, where in the ring the fight stands and what each man has learned".

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

It settles the notation and continuity of the scenes, that "The eleventh phase opens on the finish's landing at 150.000" while the uncut kick and fall cross the heading, what the eleventh and twelfth phases hold, and the condition that "No phase repeats an earlier one" while the whole ring is used, leaving each phase's events to the screenplay.

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#record-and-currency

The form is owned here once; the ring's zones that a phase moves through are owned by the ring record, not restated.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#phases

Adopts twelve fifteen-second phases, the headings and the beat notation, with the commission's reading of the beats after the finish as the bout's closing exchanges between the men and the officials.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#progression

Adopts changing pressure, angle and initiative with no looped minute up to the finish, and the procedure's change after it.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#whole-ring

Adopts the use of the whole ring: centre, sides, ropes, corners, angles and circles.
