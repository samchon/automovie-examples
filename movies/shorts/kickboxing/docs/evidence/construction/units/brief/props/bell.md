# docs/body/brief/props/bell.md#form

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises what the bell is and where it stands, and `#position` settles it: "a round metal gong on a wooden mount, struck with a metal hammer on a chain", on the officials' table "below the ropes and at canvas level, in front of the timekeeper".

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

The record settles how it reads within the frame, "a small round disc at the foot of the ring, when the frame includes that side at all", and cites the coverage study's `#bell-strike` and `#bell-position`.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#venue

The position subsection places the timekeeper's bell at ringside, as the venue ruling's "a timekeeper rings the bell from ringside" requires.

# docs/body/brief/props/bell.md#use

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises when the bell is struck and how it sounds, and settles both: "one strike at 0.0" and "a run of three quick strikes from 170.1" (`#strikes`), each with "a hard metal attack and a ringing decay that carries under the next sounds" (`#sound`).

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

The record settles the limit, "only at two" moments, and what does not sound: "No ten-second warning sounds, because the round ends before its mark", and "No gavel marks the count." It cites the rules study's `#bells` and the coverage study's `#bell-strike` and `#bell-sound`.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#sound

The strikes subsection supplies the "Start and match-ending bells" the sound ruling names.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#count

The strikes subsection holds the match-ending bell at 170.1, "after the knockout".
