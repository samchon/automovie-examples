# docs/body/brief/sound/ring-sound.md#contacts-facet

## @evidence shared/principles/common.md#scope-preservation

The facet promises how contacts sound, and `#contacts` settles a sound for each contact this fight has, from "Gloves touching" to "The finishing kick on the side of the head and neck", which is "The loudest single contact of the film, sharp and heavy".

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

The record settles the rules each contact keeps: "A contact is heard exactly where it lands, never before. A blocked strike sounds blocked, not landed. No contact is sweetened, slowed or followed by silence for effect." It tells a covered kick from a landed one: a body kick "taken on a covering arm or elbow" is "A heavy, flatter smack, duller than the landed kick, with no forced breath". It cites the coverage study's `#translation` and `#knockout-reaction`.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#sound

The contacts subsection realizes "distinct contacts": "Every contact sounds of what it is, so the ear can tell what landed and on what".

## @evidence shared/principles/brief.md#needed-decisions

The record settles the priority that holds at every level, "the referee's every number, the bell and Benlloch's breath stay above it", and points to the crowd's own course in `crowds/arena-crowd.md#in-sound`. It cites the coverage study's `#mix`.

## @evidence instructions/commission.md#sound
