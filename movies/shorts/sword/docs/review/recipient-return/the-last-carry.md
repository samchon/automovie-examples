# The Last Carry recipient review

The N2 master makes the funeral and the two men left outside the burial ground identifiable. The family quarrel is much harder to follow as a fight. The film spends half the minute getting to the separation, then holds the same wide for 19 seconds while the men mainly face each other and the funeral walks away. The script’s two strongest physical moments, the widow taking the weight and the cousins briefly holding each other up, are hard to see. The finished-film rejection is consistent with the picture and with measured intersections in the staged geometry.

## Why N2 passed and what this review establishes

The original N2 director signed off on the readable funeral outline, substitutions, shoe and sliver. The same verdict called physical staging a partial pass and specifically recorded ambiguous rail support, an apparent sleeve intersection, the hard-to-read coat catch, a loose pinch, and loss of tension from 39–42 and 45–48 s. It accepted the fixed wide because it shows the funeral leaving the men, and left the weight, two pauses, fast closing camera move and audio experience for playback. The high source-to-encode similarity established preservation of the rendered frames, which include these defects. The focused test checks stops and starts, seeking, the fixed wide and sliver marker attachment. It does not measure rail clearance, garment penetration, support or a performance that reads as fighting.
