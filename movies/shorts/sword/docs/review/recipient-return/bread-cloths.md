# Bread Cloths recipient review

The delivered minute makes the two groups and the divided bread recognizable by the end. The encounter rarely feels like a fight on a mountain road. Five people cycle along a stage-like profile while two others hold their places above. A blade moves across the brown man's chest and the group keeps going. The large load, a fall and a rescue eventually fill the frame, but the figures' balance and reactions seldom show what one person has just done to another. The viewer's rejection of pacing, fight staging and clipping is supported by the delivered picture.

## Causes

The road is a height field with two flat-looking shelves linked at the east end. `frameCamera` holds similar profile views for the first 21 s. The repeated wide composition keeps the upper route, lower route and steep bank visible, but flattens the grade and makes the short connecting bend easy to miss. In `blocking`, smoothstep position interpolation and nominal route speed set root placement and gait selection. The native gait is a flat, slow 84 m walk sampled on its own clock. The individual boots are posed from these local joints without compensating for road height or holding a planted foot in world space. Red Cap's reach moves his root toward the steep bank without changing leg supports. [Road](../../../src/composition/scenes/bread-cloths/010-two-bends/road.ts#L40) · [Blocking](../../../src/composition/scenes/bread-cloths/010-two-bends/blocking.ts#L44) · [Cameras](../../../src/composition/scenes/bread-cloths/010-two-bends/blocking.ts#L113) · [Performance](../../../src/composition/scenes/bread-cloths/010-two-bends/performance.ts#L47)

The strike and turn are timed pose and prop cues. The caller's blade is aimed at the breastplate while Perrin's recoil, Alix's lean and the impact audio run on their own time curves. The rescue similarly has an independently interpolated Alix root and Perrin's time-based wrist targets. The sleeve overlap cannot establish which arm carried the weight. The loaves travel by center interpolation while a separately modeled tear appears over an unchanged sack mesh. The knees pass through the simple, root-mounted cylinder dresses because lowering and rescaling the cylinders does not fit them to bent legs. The color and accessory choices preserve cues from the selected photographs, including Alix's white headcloth, Perrin's brown coat, the caller's ochre hood and Red Cap's headwear. The shared geometric heads have fixed eyes, brows and mouths. Rigid clothing and identical-looking posture dilute separate personalities, the armour's shape reads as a smooth gray oval, and the evenly lit ochre shelves, conical trees and faceted mountains suggest a generic backdrop. The close frames make those limits more visible at the same moments that require looks of alarm, effort and relief. ## Request and previous approval

The commission asked for a moving mountain-road fight between a knight and several bandits, with weight, footing, fatigue and visible consequences. The revised screenplay also requires the audience to read an armour impact, distinguish a miss, understand a precarious load and see Perrin choose Alix. The final picture supports the broad two-route design and the portion count. It weakens the attack, pursuit, held weight and recovery that motivate those images. The fixed screenplay spends the first 21 seconds on the opening and the split-rock encounter and reserves a further 12 seconds after 48 s for distribution and departure. The realization made those ranges feel longer through repeated gait and minimal acting. Its four-frame cut interrupts the one view that could establish both sides supporting the sack. [Commission](../../instructions/commission.md#mountain-road) · [Screenplay](../../body/screenplay/bread-cloths/010-two-bends.md)

The earlier director judged the sequence of strap release, reach, return and count, and used verified chronology, selected contact frames and source-to-encode checks. The previous picture reviewer already could not infer the men's initial intent, identify the support under Red Cap, or see why the seam opened. The director treated those as matters of emphasis because the rescue and sharing could be inferred. That standard missed how long the encounter takes to begin, how little acted cause connects the poses, and how much terrain and clothing penetrate the figures. The film regression asserts format, repeatability, object positions and visibility at chosen times. It leaves geometry, continuous support, audience legibility and playback outside those assertions.
