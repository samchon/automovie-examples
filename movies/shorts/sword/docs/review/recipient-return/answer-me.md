# Answer Me

The delivered minute reads as a token handed to an ochre rider, a long ride with a blue rider alongside, and two chestnuts reunited at a gate. The colour pairing, separately dropped lances, turn back to the gate and final separation are clear. The riders rarely look as though either can change what the other does. I could see the dark props come up and one fall. I could read them as maces in the close view, while the reason for the ride, the effects of most exchanges and Luc’s reason for releasing Rémy stayed unclear in the picture.

## Why it passed

The original reviewer saw sparring or play, could only identify the maces at full size, and read the sleeve contact as an appeal or goodbye. The director recorded the repeated travel, unclear impacts and weakened initiative of the mare, then accepted the film at the stylized stage based on readable destinations, visible prop changes, the revised sleeve placement and intended sound cues. The file profile and recorded source matches establish delivery integrity. The unit test checks scene placement, continuity and selected targets, including a palm-to-sleeve distance and the foal root’s clearance during the opening swing. Those tests miss the foal’s muzzle in the closed rail and the reins and bodies crossing other surfaces. Both riders remain mounted through substantial time windows, but the commission’s fight depends on readable consequences between them. The audience and director records had already identified the failure in that part of the film.
