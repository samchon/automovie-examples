# The Great St Bernard road

## The two approaches {#pass-country}

### The house above the carriers {#two-approaches}

Around 1460 the hospice stands in Savoy at the high end of a road people use from both sides of the pass. From Orsières, the climb with bread leads towards the house. Across the pass in the Aosta valley there is room beside the road for a household yard, a horse run and a lane through a settlement. Those three places belong to different households. From valley ground the hospice itself is out of sight.
