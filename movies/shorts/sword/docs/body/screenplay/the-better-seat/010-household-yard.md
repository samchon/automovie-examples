# The better seat

## The wager in the yard {#yard}

- Standing state. Morning in a household yard on the Aosta side of the [pass road](../../brief/world/pass-road.md#two-approaches). The household gathers on three sides of a circle scored in the dirt. Inside are two knights with blunted swords, [Martin](../../brief/cast/martin.md#appearance) in moss-green sleeves and [Carlo](../../brief/cast/carlo.md#appearance), his grey-bearded guest in a bright Milanese helmet. A [stable lad](../../brief/cast/stable-lad.md#appearance) with a split left cuff stands at a bench just outside the score. The two saddles sit side by side within reach of the men.
- Opening view and sound. Full view from the open side, level with the bench. Somebody in the crowd tries to stifle a laugh.
- Performance time. One continuous minute in authored seconds. [The wager](../../../instructions/commission.md#duel) · [Helmet and saddles](../../../research/history/road-and-households.md#riders)

EXT. HOUSEHOLD YARD - MORNING

### Terms {#terms}

A stable boy parts the split in Martin's saddle with his thumb. Carlo's fine saddle beside it makes the hole look worse. Martin sees the boy look to Carlo for the laugh.

MARTIN  
You laughed yesterday. Ride home on it?

The boy eases his thumb another inch through the hole. Carlo lays a glove on the torn seat beside the thumb.

CARLO  
First man out gets this split seat. Winner takes mine?

MARTIN  
My word on it.

Carlo nods. The boy takes his thumb out.

- Time: 0–15 s.
- Intent and change: Martin calls Carlo to account before the household. Carlo makes a bargain the boy takes seriously.
- Camera: Stay near the bench until Carlo has named both seats. Take in the score at his feet as Martin gives his word.

### The noise they make {#noise}

The two come together. The smack of blunt steel makes the boy sit down hard on the bench. A saddle rocks against his thigh.

The pair go round the circle. Martin's heel skates along the inside of the line. Half the yard sucks in its breath. Carlo has seen the heel too. A grin opens in the T of his bright helmet.

The blunt swords meet again. Martin's heel gives on the scuffed dirt. His shoulder thumps into Carlo, who catches him by the coat. For two steps they're stumbling towards the line together. Martin gets a knee down just inside it. Carlo takes one more backward step. His boot comes down flat in the smooth dirt outside.

- Time: 15–34 s.
- Intent and change: Carlo sees Martin nearly out. Then Martin slips against him and Carlo takes the extra step. The boy sees where the boot lands.
- Camera: Cut to a full pair from beside the boy. Keep the slipping heel, the two men travelling together and the line in the same full view.
- Sound: The bench knocks once under the first report. Breath fills the space between contacts.

### The first word {#word}

The boy springs up. His eyes stop at Carlo's boot. Martin, still on one knee, follows his look across the score to the print in the smooth dirt. The boy opens his mouth.

CARLO  
I was out. The split one's mine.

Martin looks up at him. Carlo lays his sword on the bench. Martin gets to his feet and sets his beside it, still trying to get his breath.

Carlo lifts his good saddle. Martin puts out a hand for a handshake and gets an armful of leather instead.

- Time: 34–46 s.
- Intent and change: Carlo claims the loss before the boy can call it. Martin has to take the good saddle with both hands.
- Camera: Hold the landing long enough for the boy and then Martin to find the boot. Come up with Martin for the hand he offers and the saddle he gets.
- Sound: Carlo's voice carries across a quiet yard. The good saddle gives a little creak.

### The road home {#fits}

The boy reaches for the split saddle. Carlo shoulders it himself. From the door he looks back at Martin.

CARLO  
Ride with me to Milan? I've done that road alone enough.

Martin nods into the leather in his arms.

As Carlo goes through the door his fingers poke through the split. He waves them at Martin. Martin laughs so hard he has to sit down with the good saddle in his lap.

- Time: 46–60 s.
- Intent and change: Carlo gives Martin a place beside him on the journey home. The boy stands back and Martin gets to laugh this time.
- Camera: Widen with Carlo on his way out. Keep his waving fingers and the man on the bench in the same view.
- Sound: Martin's laugh follows his guest through the door.
